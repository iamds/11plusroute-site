FSCE (Future Stories Community Enterprise) 11+ tests

About FSCE

FSCE stands for Future Stories Community Enterprise. It is a not-for-profit company owned by Reading School, set up in 2022.

FSCE says its tests use only Key Stage 2 content taught up to the end of Year 5. There is no non-verbal reasoning.

FSCE changes the question types, subjects and format of its tests every year, so that no one can learn the pattern. The look of the papers stays the same.

Some FSCE tests cover any Key Stage 2 subject: English, maths, science, history, geography, art and design, computing, design and technology, languages, music and PE. When a question needs facts a child may not have learned, the paper gives them. The child then uses them in a new situation.

Some schools add a creativity task. FSCE defines creativity as thinking of new ideas or solving problems in original ways.

Shared tests written by FSCE: Halifax grammar schools test.

The papers

Adventure
Multiple choice. English and maths, and at some schools other Key Stage 2 subjects.
Beacon
Short written answers. English and maths.
Compass
Multiple choice. Any Key Stage 2 subject.
Discovery
A creative written answer, up to two sides by hand. Reading School only.

Not every school uses every paper. The Lancashire and Yorkshire group (Lancaster Girls', Skipton Girls', Clitheroe Royal and Ermysted's) has two English and maths papers only. Trafford will have an English and maths paper and a paper on other subjects, with no creative writing. Each paper usually takes 45 to 60 minutes. A creativity paper takes about 20 minutes.

What the test is like

  • The tests are on paper, with a question booklet and a separate answer sheet. Children answer in black pen.
  • There are three kinds of question: multiple choice (shade one oval), short written answers (one digit or one capital letter in each box), and, at some schools, a creative writing task written by hand.
  • A recording reads out the instructions and the time for each section.
  • Wrong answers do not lose marks. FSCE says many children will not finish.

Your school may publish its own version of these guides. Use the one for your school, because the papers differ.

FSCE says there are no past papers and no practice papers. It does not endorse any commercial materials.

Marking and scores

A computer reads the multiple-choice and short-answer sheets, with checks by people. The scores are age-standardised. Where there is a creativity task, the school's own teachers mark it by comparative judgement, and only for children who reach the required score on the other papers.

Access arrangements

  • Ask for access arrangements when you register, with evidence. The school decides each case.
  • FSCE can provide braille, large-print and modified papers.
