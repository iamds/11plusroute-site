GL Assessment 11+ maths

GL maths questions follow the National Curriculum up to the start of Year 6. They are multiple choice, and some ask children to use what they know on a new kind of problem.

Schools on this site
107
Format
Paper, multiple choice

Question types

  1. Number, measurement, geometry and statistics

    Multiple-choice questions on National Curriculum maths, from five options. Some ask children to use what they know on a new kind of problem.

  2. Problems with tables, charts and graphs

    GL's free papers include questions about tables, charts, graphs, scales, angles and money.

GL describes its maths by topic, not by named question types. GL says the language is kept simple so that the test measures maths, not reading, and questions get harder through the paper. GL's free maths papers each have 50 questions; GL suggests about 50 minutes.

How it differs by area

Each area or school has its own version of the test. The same question types can come in a different paper, with a different time and weighting.

BuckinghamshireIn the Mathematical and Non-Verbal Skills paper: 25 minutes plus 5 minutes' practice. Maths counts for 25%.
Kent (to September 2026)In a combined English and maths paper.

How answers are given

  • The tests are on paper. Children read the questions in a booklet and mark their answers on a separate answer sheet.
  • Almost all questions are multiple choice. The child draws a pencil line through the box next to the answer. A computer reads the answer sheet, so mistakes must be rubbed out, not crossed out.
  • An invigilator reads out the instructions for each section. Each section starts with an example and practice questions. Only the numbered questions are timed.

Free practice materials

  • GL Assessment free 11+ familiarisation materials
    Free downloads in verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, English and maths, plus a combined non-verbal reasoning and maths test and a Verbal Skills test. Each set has test booklets, answer sheets and a parent's guide with the answers.

Many councils also publish a familiarisation booklet for their own test. Check your area's council or consortium page. GL says local timings and question numbers can differ from its general materials.

GL sells official practice paper packs in verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, English and maths. Each pack has three papers. Lincolnshire tells parents to buy the Multiple Choice packs, not the Standard Format packs.

Marking and scores

Scores are age-standardised to allow for the different ages of children in the year group. Each council or school decides how to weight the subjects and what score is needed.

Access arrangements

  • Ask the school or council for access arrangements when you register. Do not ask GL.
  • GL makes large-print and enlarged papers. There is no braille version.
  • GL guidance allows up to 25% extra time. It says more than that would make the age-standardised score unreliable. Some areas allow less: Buckinghamshire usually gives 10%.
