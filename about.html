About 11+ Admissions

11+ Admissions explains how each grammar school in England, and each state school with selective places, decides who gets a Year 7 place: the entrance test, the pass mark, the key dates and the order in which places are given out. There are 190 schools so far and 21 shared entrance tests.

How the pages are made

Each school page sets out the school's own admissions arrangements for the coming year in a consistent format, with a flow chart showing who is offered places first. Where councils publish how many children were offered places under each rule, those figures are included too. Every page shows which year's entry it covers and when it was last checked.

Please check before you apply

Admissions rules change every year and schools sometimes change dates. This site is an independent guide: it is not run by, or connected to, any school, council or test provider. Always read the school's own admissions policy (linked from each page) before you register or apply, and contact the school or your council if anything is unclear.

Spotted a mistake?

Use the "Spotted a mistake? Tell us" link at the bottom of any school page, or this form. Corrections are checked and usually made within a few days.
