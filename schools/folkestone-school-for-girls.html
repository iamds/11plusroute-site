Grammar School Admissions · Year 7 entry, school by school

The Folkestone School for Girls

Folkestone

A girl qualifies by passing either the Kent Test or the Shepway Test (run by the school with The Harvey Grammar School). If more qualified girls apply than there are places, looked-after girls come first, then Pupil Premium girls living in Folkestone and Hythe district, then other girls living in the district (nearest first), then girls from outside (nearest first).

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who have passed come first.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium girls living in Folkestone and Hythe district Girls eligible for Pupil Premium (targeted FSM in the last 6 years) who live in Folkestone and Hythe district, if the supplementary form reaches the school by 31 October.
  3. 3. Lives in Folkestone and Hythe district Other qualified girls living in the district. If there are more than places, nearest first.
  4. 4. Other qualified girls, by distance Qualified girls living outside the district, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Over 400 girls sat the Shepway Test in 2026.
  • The Shepway Test pass standard is about the level of the top 25% of girls.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • Twins and triplets are all admitted, even if this takes the school over its 180 places.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
