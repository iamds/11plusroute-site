Tiffin School

Kingston upon Thames

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A two-stage English and maths test set with Quest. Stage One sifts boys down to roughly 650, who sit Stage Two. Places go in order of Combined Score. Looked-after and Pupil Premium boys in the 10 km Priority Area who rank in the top 450 come first, then other Priority Area boys, then everyone else. Up to 18 places are kept for Priority Area boys who pass the school's sport or music aptitude assessments and rank in the top 450. In practice every place since 2019 has gone to boys living within 10 km.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. B(i). Looked after / previously looked after children in the Priority Area (top 450) Looked-after and previously looked-after boys living within 10 km who rank in the top 450 of Stage Two candidates come first, in score order.
  2. B(ii). Pupil Premium boys in the Priority Area (top 450) Pupil Premium boys (including service children) living within 10 km who rank in the top 450 come next, in score order. 15 such boys were offered places for 2026.
  3. C. Lives in the 10 km Priority Area The remaining places (less up to 18 kept for criterion E) go to boys living within 10 km, in Combined Score order. Nearly all places are filled here.
  4. D. Lives outside the Priority Area Boys living 10 km or more away, in Combined Score order, only if any places are left. None have been offered a place since 2019.
  5. E(i). Sporting aptitude (Priority Area, top 450) Up to 9 places for Priority Area boys who passed the sport benchmark, ranked in the top 450 and missed out under A-D. Places go in order of sport assessment rank.
  6. E(ii). Musical aptitude (Priority Area, top 450) Up to 9 places for Priority Area boys who passed the music benchmark, ranked in the top 450 and missed out under A-D. Places go in order of music assessment rank.

Good to know

  • Since the 10 km Priority Area began in 2019, no boy living outside it has been offered a place.
  • The school has taken 186 boys a year (six forms of 31), above its official number of 180.
  • Aptitude places don't lower the bar: boys still need a Combined Score in the top 450 of Stage Two candidates.
  • A boy can sit Tiffin's tests only once; boys below the final Year 7 cut-off can never be considered for Years 8-11.
  • Only name Tiffin on your application if your son is invited to Stage Two.
  • Tiffin doesn't publish past papers, but its website has illustrative Stage One and Stage Two questions showing the format and level.
  • Boys eligible for free school meals in the last six years can get free familiarisation materials through Atom Learning (apply via the form on the school's admissions page).
  • The school says some practice at working independently for an hour at a time may help.
