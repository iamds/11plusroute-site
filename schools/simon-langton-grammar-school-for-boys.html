Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or previously in care (including adopted from state care abroad), who have a grammar assessment.
  2. 2(i). High scorer (20+ above pass mark) with health or special access reasons Boys who scored at least 20 marks above the Kent Test pass mark and whose parents can show attendance at the school is essential for health or special access reasons.
  3. 2(ii). High scorer (20+ above pass mark) with a brother or sister at the school Boys who scored at least 20 marks above the pass mark and have a brother or sister at the school at the time of entry.
  4. 2(iii). High scorer (20+ above pass mark) receiving Pupil Premium Boys who scored at least 20 marks above the pass mark and are eligible for Pupil Premium (FSM at any point in the last 6 years). Looked-after children are already covered by criterion 1.
  5. 3. High scorer (20+ above pass mark) living within 9 miles Boys living within a 9-mile radius of the school who scored at least 20 marks above the pass mark, nearest first.
  6. 4(i). Other qualifying boys: health or special access reasons Any other boy with a grammar assessment whose parents can show attendance is essential for health or special access reasons.
  7. 4(ii). Other qualifying boys: brother or sister at the school Any other qualifying boy with a brother or sister at the school at the time of entry.
  8. 4(iii). Other qualifying boys: Pupil Premium Any other qualifying boy eligible for Pupil Premium.
  9. 4(iv). Other qualifying boys by distance All remaining boys with a grammar assessment, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Boys scoring at least 20 marks above the Kent Test pass mark get priority over other qualifying boys.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • The school has a Specialist Resourced Provision for autistic students; entry needs an EHCP naming it and a pass in the Kent Test.
  • Being on the waiting list doesn't affect your right of appeal.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
  • Pupil Premium children can get free 11+ preparation and practice papers through Atom Learning.
  • GL Assessment, which writes the Kent Test, publishes free familiarisation tests online.
