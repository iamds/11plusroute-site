Watford Grammar School for Boys

Watford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A partially selective school. 65% of the 224 places (146) are community places: looked-after children, the nearest 10% by distance, staff children, siblings and cross-siblings at Watford Grammar School for Girls, then distance. Up to 35% (78) are specialist places: 22 for music aptitude and 56 for academic ability, using the SW Herts Consortium tests and split between the Watford area and the rest of the admission area. Children who don't sit the tests are still considered for community places, but in practice only families inside the admission area get in.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Community place. Includes children adopted (or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order) after state care outside England. No need to live in the admission area.
  2. 2. Nearest to the school: first 10% (22 places) Community place. The 22 children living nearest the school who don't qualify under any other criterion (apart from criterion 6). This is the first 10% of the distance list.
  3. 3. Children of staff Community place. No need to live in the admission area.
  4. 4. Brother at the school Community place. No need to live in the admission area or to sit the tests.
  5. 5. Sister at Watford Grammar School for Girls Community place for children with a sister at the partner school, living in the admission area. Boys with a sister there are advised to sit the tests too in case there aren't enough community places.
  6. 6. Distance (remaining places) Community place. Any places left after criteria 1-5, 7 and 8 go back down the same distance list. Unfilled music or academic places are added here.
  7. 7. Musical aptitude (22 places, 10%) Specialist place, in merit order on the music test. 13 places for the Watford area and 9 for the rest of the admission area. A child who qualifies for both music and academic places gets a music place.
  8. 8. Academic ability (56 places, 25%) Specialist place, strictly in descending order of standardised test score. 34 places for the Watford area and 22 for the rest of the admission area, each ranked separately (so the cut-off scores differ).
  9. Outside area. Children living outside the admission area Only if places are left after every in-area application and criteria 1, 3 and 4 have been considered, by distance. Out-of-area applications for specialist places are only looked at if in-area children don't fill them. Out-of-area applicants have no realistic chance.

Good to know

  • Families outside the admission area are very unlikely to be offered a place.
  • Boys who sit neither test and have no sibling are considered only on distance.
  • Boys with a brother at the school don't need to sit the academic test.
  • Applying for a specialist place automatically counts as applying for a community place too.
  • In 2026 the lowest academic scores offered were 200 in the Watford area and 214 in the rest of the admission area.
