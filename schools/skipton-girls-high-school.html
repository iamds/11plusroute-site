Skipton Girls' High School

Skipton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter sits the school's own FSCE tests in English and maths on one Saturday morning (26 September 2026). Girls who reach the required standard in each test are 'deemed suitable'. If more qualify than there are 120 places, places go to looked-after girls first, then girls on Pupil Premium or whose families receive Universal Credit, then girls in the Priority Area (Skipton, Craven and the upper Dales), then everyone else. Within each group the nearest girls come first. It has always been possible to offer places to every qualifying girl in the Priority Area. In 2026, places reached girls outside it as far as 10 miles away.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who have been deemed suitable, including girls adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Universal Credit Girls registered as Pupil Premium, or whose families receive Universal Credit, at the time of application. This criterion has no address condition, so it applies inside and outside the Priority Area. The school can withdraw an offer made on incorrect or misleading information.
  3. 3. Lives in the Priority Area Qualifying girls living in the school's Priority Area. It has always been possible to offer places to all qualifying girls in the Priority Area.
  4. 4. Lives outside the Priority Area All other qualifying girls, nearest first. In 2026 the last place went to a girl in this group 10.03 miles away.

Good to know

  • Every qualifying girl living in the Priority Area has always been offered a place. In 2026 places also reached girls outside it as far as 10 miles away.
  • Within each group the nearest girls come first; test scores don't rank girls for places.
  • Ermysted's tests on the same Saturday with the same kind of papers, but it is a separate test with its own registration and standard.
  • Pupil Premium girls are offered free 11+ familiarisation support through Atom Learning.
  • Results arrive by 19 October, before the application deadline.
