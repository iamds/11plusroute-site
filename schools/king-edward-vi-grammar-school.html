King Edward VI Grammar School

Louth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must pass the Lincolnshire 11+ (220). The school is oversubscribed every year. Qualified looked-after children come first, then siblings, then twins who both passed. Next come Year 6 children living in the catchment (the free bus zone) or attending one of 25 named primary schools, in score order, then everyone else in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Siblings Qualified children with a brother or sister still at the school when they start. This supports rural families with poor transport links.
  3. 3. Twins or multiple births who all passed Twins (or triplets etc.) who have all passed the test for the same year.
  4. 4. Catchment area or named primary school Qualified Year 6 children who live in the traditional catchment area (the county's free bus travel zone) or attend one of the 25 named primary schools. Ranked by test score if oversubscribed. All such children have been offered places for the past five years.
  5. 5. All other qualified children by score Remaining qualified candidates, by test score. About a third of places a year are expected to go to children from other primary schools, including North East Lincolnshire.

Good to know

  • Every qualified child in the catchment or at a named primary school has been offered a place for the past five years.
  • About a third of places a year go to children from other primary schools, including North East Lincolnshire.
  • Children living outside the free transport zone can use a private bus service run by the school.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
