Parmiter's School

Watford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A comprehensive with selective places. Up to 25% (54) go by rank in the SW Herts Consortium academic test (GL verbal reasoning and maths), and up to 10% by the consortium music aptitude test. 95% of these selective places are for children in the priority postcodes (WD3-WD25, AL1-3, HP1-3, HA6). Looked-after children come first, then 10% of places go to the nearest children, then siblings, staff children and the test places, and any places left go by distance. Your child doesn't need to sit a test to apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Proximity: 10% of places to the nearest children 10% of places go to the children living nearest the school. A child who also qualifies under criteria 3-6 gets the place under that criterion instead, so these places stay available to other local families.
  3. 3. Siblings Children with a sibling at the school. If one twin or multiple-birth child gets a place, the others who applied are admitted too (above PAN from the second continuing-interest round).
  4. 4. Children of staff Children of staff. Ties are broken by the parent's length of service.
  5. 5. Academic ability (up to 25% of places) Up to 54 places in descending order of standardised score: 51 for the priority postcodes and 3 for everyone else. Equal scores at the cut-off are split by distance.
  6. 6. Music aptitude (up to 10% of places) Up to about 21 places in order of practical-assessment score: 20 for the priority postcodes and 1 for everyone else.
  7. 7. Remaining places, nearest first Any places left go to the children living nearest the school.

Good to know

  • Your child doesn't need to sit a test to apply under the non-selective criteria.
  • Siblings of current students don't need to sit the consortium tests unless applying to another consortium school as a higher preference.
  • There is no pass mark. For 2026 entry the lowest academic score offered was 216 in the priority postcodes and 245 elsewhere.
  • Ties in any criterion go to the child living nearest the school.
  • The school checks addresses: moving shortly after the closing date can trigger an investigation, and offers based on false addresses are withdrawn.
  • The medical criterion has been removed for 2027 entry.
  • The consortium publishes free familiarisation papers for verbal reasoning and maths, and there is a short break between the two tests.
