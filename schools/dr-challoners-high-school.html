Dr Challoner's High School

Amersham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after girls and Pupil Premium girls in catchment, up to 12 places go to catchment Pupil Premium/looked-after girls scoring 115+ (or 110-114). Then come daughters of staff, then girls who have lived in catchment since 1 April 2026, then sisters, then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after / internationally adopted girls Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium in catchment, qualified Qualified girls receiving Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium who have lived in catchment since 1 April 2026.
  3. 3. Up to 12 places: catchment Pupil Premium / looked-after girls scoring 115+ (or 110-114) Up to 12 places for catchment girls receiving Pupil Premium/Service Pupil Premium (or looked after) who scored at least 115 but below 121. If not all are taken, up to 6 can go to such girls scoring 110-114. Looked-after girls come first, then the nearest.
  4. 4. Daughters of staff Qualified daughters of eligible staff.
  5. 5. Catchment (resident since 1 April 2026) Qualified girls who have lived in catchment continuously since 1 April 2026.
  6. 6. Sisters Qualified girls with a sister at the school (in practice, those outside catchment).
  7. 7. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified girls with exceptional medical or social needs; an independent panel decides.
  8. 9. Everyone else by distance Remaining places to other qualified girls, nearest first.

Good to know

  • You must have lived in catchment since 1 April 2026, earlier than most Bucks grammar schools require.
  • The school asks for SIF2 to confirm catchment residence and SIF1 for the staff rule.
  • In recent years every qualified catchment girl has been offered a place.
  • Catchment Pupil Premium or looked-after girls can get in with a score of 110-120.
