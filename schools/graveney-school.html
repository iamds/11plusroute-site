Graveney School

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Graveney is a partially selective comprehensive. 70 of its 280 places (25%) go to the children with the highest total scores in the Wandsworth Year 6 Test (non-verbal plus verbal reasoning), wherever they live. The other 210 places go to looked-after children, then siblings, then exceptional medical/social need, then children of staff, then by distance. Every applicant should sit the Wandsworth Test; it is only used for the 70 ability places, and the 210 open places don't depend on the score. In 2026 the open places went no further than 525 m from the school.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. Category 1. Ability places: highest Wandsworth Year 6 Test scores (70 places) The 70 applicants with the highest total NVR + VR scores are offered places, whether or not they would also qualify under Category 2. Distance doesn't matter except to split equal scores.
  2. 2(i). Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after children, and children who stopped being looked after because they were adopted or became subject to a residence/child arrangements or special guardianship order, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  3. 2(ii). Siblings at the school Applicants not admitted under Category 1 who have a brother or sister attending Graveney when you apply.
  4. 2(iii). Acute medical or exceptional social need Needs supporting letters by the application closing date, otherwise the application isn't considered under this criterion. Applying under it doesn't guarantee priority.
  5. 2(iv). Children of staff Children of staff who meet the conditions.
  6. 2(v). Everyone else, nearest first Remaining places by straight-line distance from home to school, nearest first. In 2026 this reached 525 m.

Good to know

  • Children who don't sit the Wandsworth Test can still get one of the 210 open places.
  • The ability-place waiting list moves a lot: in 2026 the cut-off went from 250 on offer day to 240 by 31 August.
  • In 2026 the open places went no further than 525 m from the school.
  • Graveney has no supplementary form.
