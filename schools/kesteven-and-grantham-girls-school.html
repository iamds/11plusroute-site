Kesteven and Grantham Girls' School

Grantham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must score at least 220 in the Lincolnshire consortium 11+ (two GL reasoning papers). If more qualified girls apply than there are places, looked-after girls come first, then sisters of current pupils, then girls on free school meals living within 12 miles, then children of staff, then everyone else by distance. Test score doesn't affect priority once she has qualified. In recent years the furthest offer has been about 20 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including children adopted from state care outside England) who have qualified.
  2. B. Sister at the school Girls with a sibling attending KGGS when the application is made. Qualified twins or sisters in the same year are treated as one application, and the school will go over its PAN to admit both unless classes would become too large.
  3. C. Free school meals, within 12 miles Girls currently eligible for free school meals who live within 12 miles. This priority changed from Pupil Premium to current FSM eligibility in September 2026, after a change in government rules.
  4. D. Children of staff Children of staff employed at KGGS for at least two years, or recruited to fill a skill-shortage post.
  5. E. Distance from home All other qualified girls, nearest to the school first.

Good to know

  • The number of places rises from 174 to 180 for 2027 entry.
  • Test score doesn't rank qualified girls. Once she reaches 220, distance decides most places.
  • In recent years the furthest offer has been about 20-22 miles, and 17 to 40 qualified girls a year were not offered a place, mainly because of distance.
  • The 11+ can be sat only once, at one consortium school, but the result counts for all of them.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
