Urmston Grammar Academy

Manchester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 334 in the Trafford Consortium test (324 for Pupil Premium children; a Local Review is possible just below). After EHCP and looked-after children, about 15 places go to the highest-scoring Pupil Premium children and about 20 to the top scorers from anywhere. Then come children of long-serving staff and siblings, and the remaining ~120 places go by distance, M41 and M31 postcodes first.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A. Looked after / previously looked after children (after EHCPs) Category A: first EHCP children naming the school, then looked-after and previously looked-after children (including adopted from state care abroad).
  2. B. Pupil Premium - about 15 places by score About 15 places for the highest-scoring Pupil Premium children, wherever they live. Ties at the cut-off are all offered places.
  3. C. Top scorers - about 20 places The 20 highest-scoring children not already placed, wherever they live.
  4. D. Children of long-serving staff Children of staff who have had a permanent contract at the school for at least 8 continuous years, if they qualify.
  5. E. Sibling at the school Children with a brother or sister on roll at Urmston Grammar on test day, wherever they live.
  6. F. Everyone else by distance, M41/M31 first - about 120 places All remaining places (about 120) by straight-line distance: children in M41 and M31 postcodes first, nearest first, then everyone else nearest first.

Good to know

  • Service Premium doesn't give any priority; only Pupil Premium does.
  • If you move into the priority area after registering, your registration address is used until after the first-round offers. Moving further away counts straight away.
  • Places have been withdrawn after National Offer Day where address information was false or misleading.
  • For 2028 entry the test changes to one based on the KS2 curriculum up to the end of Year 5.
