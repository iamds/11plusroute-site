Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those who left state care through adoption) with an eligible score.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (including Service Premium) Girls on the eligible list entitled to Pupil Premium: current or Ever-6 free school meals, or a parent serving in the regular UK armed forces now or in the past 3 years (or receiving a pension under the Armed Forces Compensation Scheme / War Pensions Scheme because a parent died in service).
  3. 3. Everyone else by total score Remaining girls on the eligible list in order of total test score.

Good to know

  • The rules changed for 2027 entry: there are no longer rank bands or priority for Torbay residents.
  • All girls with an eligible score are ordered looked-after first, then Pupil Premium or Service Premium, then by score.
  • Scores are never disclosed, not even after offers.
  • Girls who want to join the Year 7 waiting list after the autumn term must sit new tests in spring 2028.
