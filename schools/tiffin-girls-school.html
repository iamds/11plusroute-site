The Tiffin Girls' School

Kingston upon Thames

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A two-stage English and maths test. Stage One decides who is invited to Stage Two, and only girls who sit Stage Two can get a place. Looked-after girls come first, then up to 60 places go to Pupil Premium girls in the catchment and girls in the Inner Area (13 local wards) who score at or above the 350th-ranked mark. All remaining places go in score order to girls in the Designated Area (44 postal districts) before anyone outside it.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reach the 350th-ranked mark (with 10% leeway) come first.
  2. 2i. Pupil Premium girls living in the Inner Area or Designated Area (within 60-place quota) First call on the up-to-60 shared places: Pupil Premium girls living in the catchment (Inner or Designated Area), in score order, with a 10% leeway below the 350th-ranked mark. This is a much lower bar than the general cut-off.
  3. 2ii. Girls living in the Inner Area (within 60-place quota) Then girls living in the 13 Inner Area wards whose combined mark is at or above the 350th-ranked mark, in score order, until the 60 shared places are filled.
  4. 3. Lives in the Designated Area All remaining places go to Stage Two candidates living in the Designated Area (44 postal districts), in combined-mark order. Since 2014 every place has gone to girls in this area.
  5. 4. Lives outside the Designated Area Girls outside the Designated Area are ranked below every Designated Area girl, however high their score. The school does not expect any places to reach this group.

Good to know

  • Since 2014 every Year 7 place has gone to girls living in the Designated Area; families outside it should think carefully before registering.
  • The Inner Area route (up to 60 places at the 350th-ranked mark, from about 470 Stage Two candidates) is a much lower bar than the general cut-off.
  • English counts for 60% of the final mark and maths for 40%.
  • Results arrive in October only as an invitation to Stage Two; you name the school without knowing her final rank.
