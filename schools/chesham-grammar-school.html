Chesham Grammar School

Chesham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after children, up to 6 places go to catchment Pupil Premium children scoring 110-120. Then come qualified Pupil Premium children in catchment, siblings, staff children and the rest of the catchment (resident since 1 September 2026), and then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 4.1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify.
  2. 4.2. Up to 6 places: catchment Pupil Premium scoring 110-120 Up to 6 places for Pupil Premium children living in catchment since 1 September 2026 who scored 110-120 and haven't gone through Selection Review. Looked-after children first, then the nearest.
  3. 4.3. Pupil Premium in catchment, qualified Qualified children living in catchment (since 1 September 2026) who qualify for Pupil Premium.
  4. 4.4. Siblings Qualified siblings of current pupils, wherever they live.
  5. 4.5. Children of staff Qualified children of eligible staff.
  6. 4.6. Catchment Qualified children living in catchment continuously since 1 September 2026.
  7. 4.7. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified children with exceptional medical or social needs.
  8. 4.8. Everyone else by distance Remaining places to other qualified children, nearest first.

Good to know

  • The admission number rises from 180 to 186 for 2027 entry.
  • From 2027 the up-to-6 Pupil Premium places for scores of 110-120 are inside the admission number. Children who applied for Selection Review can't use this route.
  • Pupil Premium entitlement is taken from the most recent school census before entry.
