Caistor Grammar School

Market Rasen

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Caistor sets its own 11+: two verbal reasoning papers (one written, one multiple choice), and your child needs a total of at least 220. If more qualified children apply than there are places, looked-after children come first, then children living within 6.5 miles (by score). Up to 12 places go to Pupil Premium children (counting any already placed from the catchment). All other places go by verbal reasoning score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 5.1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including children adopted from state care outside England) who have qualified.
  2. 5.2. In catchment (within 6.5 miles) Qualified children whose main home is within 6.5 miles of the school, highest VR score first.
  3. 5.21. Pupil Premium (Ever 6), up to 12 places In the main Year 7 round, up to 12 places for Pupil Premium (Ever 6) children. Applies only until the council hands the waiting list to the school on 1 September 2027.
  4. 5.3. All other qualified children by score Everyone else, highest VR score first.
