Rugby High School

Rugby

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the Warwickshire 11+ (shared West Midlands test). After EHCPs, places go to looked-after girls, then up to 30 Pupil Premium and 5 Service Premium girls in the catchment areas, all of whom may score up to 20 marks below the qualifying score. Next come staff children, then girls in the Eastern Area of Warwickshire (Rugby and surrounding parishes) and then the rest of the 10-mile Priority Circle who reach the qualifying score, in score order. For 2027 the criteria have been restructured (the former 50-place cap on the Rugby area has gone) and the PAN is 150.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) at the AQS, up to 20 marks below it, or at the waiting-list score if that is lower. No residence condition.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium girls in the catchment areas (up to 30, shared with criterion 7) Up to 30 places for girls living in the Eastern Area or the Priority Circle who were eligible for the Pupil Premium when registered for the test, on the lower academic bar. The 30 places are shared with criterion 7 (re-offers included).
  3. 3. Service Premium girls in the catchment areas (up to 5, shared with criterion 8) Up to 5 places for girls in the Eastern Area or Priority Circle eligible for the Service Premium (children of Armed Forces personnel) when registered for the test, on the lower academic bar. Shared with criterion 8.
  4. 4. Children of staff Daughters of staff with two or more years' permanent employment at the school who reach the AQS. No residence condition.
  5. 5. Eastern Area of Warwickshire Girls living in the Eastern Area of Warwickshire (Rugby and surrounding parishes) who reach the AQS, highest scores first. No longer capped (until 2026 the equivalent category had at most 50 places).
  6. 6. Priority Circle (10.004 miles from Rugby Water Tower) Other girls living within the 10.004-mile Priority Circle who reach the AQS, highest scores first.
  7. 7. Pupil Premium girls outside the catchment areas (balance of the 30) If the PAN has not been reached and fewer than 30 went under criterion 2, the balance goes to Pupil Premium girls living outside the catchment areas, on the lower academic bar.
  8. 8. Service Premium girls outside the catchment areas (balance of the 5) If the PAN has not been reached and fewer than 5 went under criterion 3, the balance goes to Service Premium girls living outside the catchment areas, on the lower academic bar.
  9. 9. Outside the catchment areas, at or above the AQS Girls living outside both catchment areas who reach the AQS.
  10. 10. At or above the waiting-list score (anywhere) Girls inside or outside the catchment areas who reach the waiting-list score but not the AQS. In practice a waiting-list category.

Good to know

  • The admission criteria changed for 2027 entry, so past allocation figures don't map neatly onto the new criteria.
  • In 2026 every qualifying girl living in the Priority Circle (score 213 or more) got a place.
  • The school now has 150 places, up from 120 before September 2026.
  • There is no priority for siblings or twins.
  • Pupil Premium means eligible for Pupil Premium funding when you register for the test, not just free school meals.
  • Test registration closes at 4pm on 30 June 2026.
