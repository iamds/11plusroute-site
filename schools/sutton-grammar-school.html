Sutton Grammar School

Sutton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must pass the shared Sutton SET (English and maths, multiple choice) and then sit a written English and maths Second Stage test, and their combined score must reach the school's required standard. Among boys who pass, looked-after children and children of staff come first. Next come up to 10 Pupil Premium boys and then 75 boys living in the local postcodes (SM1-7, KT4, KT17, CR0 4, CR4), both in score order. The remaining places go to the highest scorers from any postcode.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Boys who reached the required standard and are looked after (at the time of SET registration) or were previously looked after, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Children of permanent staff (2+ years) Boys who reached the required standard and whose parent has worked at the school on a permanent contract for at least two continuous years by the time of SET registration.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium boys in the local postcodes (up to 10 places) Up to 10 places for Pupil Premium boys (FSM Ever 6) who live in the local postcodes, in order of combined score. Only FSM-based Pupil Premium counts.
  4. 4. Boys in the local postcodes (75 places) 75 places for the highest-scoring boys living in postcodes SM1-7, KT4, KT17, CR0 4 or CR4.
  5. 5. All remaining places by score (any postcode) All remaining places, including any unused from criteria 3 and 4, go to the highest-scoring boys from any postcode, including local boys who missed out under criterion 4.

Good to know

  • Every boy must reach the same required standard: there is no lower bar for disadvantaged boys.
  • 75 places are kept for the local postcodes and up to 10 for local Pupil Premium boys, so about 65 or fewer go to top scorers from anywhere.
  • Final scores are rounded to the nearest 4 marks, so many boys tie and distance from the Manor Lane front door often decides.
  • Only FSM-based Pupil Premium counts; Universal Credit free school meals from September 2026 don't count unless your son is registered for Pupil Premium.
  • Only the CR0 4 sector of CR0 counts as local.
  • Requests for out-of-age-group admission go in with the SET registration.
