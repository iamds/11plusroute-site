Sir William Borlase's Grammar School

Marlow

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after children and Pupil Premium children in catchment (up to 20 places for those scoring 110-120), 15 places go by lottery to children in the rural Priority Admission Area, then staff children, then the rest of the catchment by distance. Siblings outside catchment come after that.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify. They need a score of at least 110 rather than 121.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium in catchment, scoring 110-120 Up to 20 places for Pupil Premium children living in catchment (since 1 September 2026) who scored 110-120. If more apply, the 20 nearest get places. Places not applied for by 31 October 2026 pass to the next rule.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium in catchment, qualified Pupil Premium children living in catchment who scored 121+ or qualified through Selection Review or appeal.
  4. 4. Priority Admission Area (15 places by lottery) Up to 15 places for qualified children living in the Priority Admission Area (the rural western part of the catchment around Stokenchurch, Lane End, Frieth and Hambleden) on 1 September 2026, drawn by lottery. Children who miss out are still in catchment, so they are considered again under rule 6.
  5. 5. Children of staff Qualified children of eligible staff.
  6. 6. Catchment (including the Priority Admission Area) Qualified children who have lived continuously in the catchment since 1 September 2026. In practice, catchment children with a sibling at the school are placed first, then by distance.
  7. 7. Siblings Qualified siblings of current pupils who live outside the catchment (catchment siblings are already placed under rule 6).
  8. 8. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified children with exceptional medical or social needs that only this school can meet. A panel of independent education professionals decides.
  9. 9. Everyone else by distance Any remaining places go to other qualified children, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Once your child scores 121, a higher score makes no difference: places go by the admission rules and distance.
  • 15 places are given by lottery to children living in the rural Priority Admission Area.
  • Siblings living outside the catchment come after all catchment children.
  • The catchment extends into Windsor and Maidenhead (Cookham, Hurley and north Maidenhead).
  • In 2026 places reached 3.12 miles within the catchment; in 2025 every catchment child was offered a place.
  • A familiarisation booklet with example questions and a guide to the answer sheets is posted home in early July; GL Assessment also offers free materials online.
  • Children usually sit an unmarked practice test two days before the real test.
