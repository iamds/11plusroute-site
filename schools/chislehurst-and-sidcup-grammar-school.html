Chislehurst and Sidcup Grammar School

Sidcup

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must be deemed selective in the Bexley Selection Test. Places go first to looked-after children, then up to 12 to Pupil Premium children (nearest first), then to the Bexley-wide top 180 scorers, siblings and staff children. All other places go by straight-line distance to the Hurst Road entrance; in 2026 the last offer was at 3.086 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who are deemed selective.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (up to 12 places) Up to 12 places for deemed-selective Pupil Premium children (FSM now or in the last 6 years), nearest first.
  3. 3. Top 180 scorers in the Bexley Selection Test The 180 highest scorers across everyone who sat the Bexley Selection Test (for any of the four schools) are 'guaranteed' a place at their preferred Bexley grammar school. Bexley sets the cut-off each year, and it varies with that year's scores.
  4. 4. Siblings Children with a brother or sister (including half, adopted, step and foster siblings at the same address) at the school in Years 7-12 on the closing date.
  5. 5. Children of staff Children of permanent staff with at least two years' service at the closing date.
  6. 6. Distance All other deemed-selective applicants, nearest first, by straight-line distance to the school's Hurst Road entrance.

Good to know

  • Appeals rarely succeed: none was upheld from 2020 to 2024 (148 appeals), and 1 was upheld in each of 2025 and 2026.
  • About 5,900 children sit the Bexley test each year and about 30-35% are deemed selective (5,866 sat in 2025 and 2,070 were selective).
  • If grammar places remain after the first allocation, they go to children one mark below the selective score, then two marks below, and so on.
  • Both papers are sat in one session lasting two and a half to three hours including instructions. All working out goes in the test booklet, as no rough paper is given.
