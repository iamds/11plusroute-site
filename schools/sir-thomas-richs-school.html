Sir Thomas Rich's School

Gloucester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Boys who reach the qualifying standard are offered places in this order: looked-after boys, then boys registered for Pupil Premium, then children of staff, then everyone else in test rank order. Pupil Premium and looked-after boys have a lower qualifying standard. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after, previously looked-after and internationally adopted previously looked-after boys who reach the (lower) qualifying standard.
  2. b. Boys registered for Pupil Premium Boys who have had income-related free school meals at any point in the six years before test registration closed, and who reach the lower qualifying standard. No limit on numbers.
  3. c. Children of staff Qualifying sons of staff employed at the school for two or more years, or recruited to fill a skill-shortage post.
  4. d. Other qualifying boys in test rank order All other boys who reach the qualifying standard, highest rank first.

Good to know

  • Boys ranked in the top 155 are guaranteed a place if Rich's is their highest preference; offers went down to rank 332 in 2026.
  • Siblings, feeder schools and catchment count for nothing. The school has no catchment area and accepts boys from outside Gloucestershire.
  • Name the school on your application only if your son met its qualifying standard; qualifying is not an offer.
  • Pupil Premium boys can get free 11+ familiarisation through Atom Learning via the school.
  • From September 2028 entry the Gloucestershire grammar schools switch to an FSCE test sat at the end of Year 5 (Saturday 3 July 2027).
