Colyton Grammar School

Colyton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One test day at the school (Quest Assessments English, maths and a short writing task). Children who reach the eligible score are ranked by total test score. Looked-after children come first, then Pupil Premium and Service Premium children on the eligible list, then everyone else in score order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from care) with an eligible score.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Service Premium Children on the eligible list who are entitled to Pupil Premium (Ever 6 FSM) or Service Premium. There is no cap on numbers.
  3. 3. All other eligible children by rank Everyone else on the eligible list, in order of total test score.

Good to know

  • There are no sibling, catchment or distance priorities. Distance only breaks a tie for the last place.
  • The test takes about 2.5 hours including breaks.
  • Anonymised standardised scores are published on the school website after offers are made, but the eligible score itself isn't.
  • Joining in Years 7-8 later in the year needs the school's in-year test (CEM Select Evaluate).
  • A free familiarisation guide is on the school website (there are no commercial practice papers), and children eligible for free school meals in the last six years can get free Atom Home materials.
