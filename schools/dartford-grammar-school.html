Dartford Grammar School

Dartford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must pass the Kent Test. 130 places go to boys in the Priority Area (Dartford Borough plus 10 nearby parishes): looked-after boys first, then up to 13 Pupil Premium boys scoring 20+ points above the pass mark, then boys scoring 40+ points above it, all in score order. The other 50 places (plus any unused Priority Area places) go to boys from any address in score order, with looked-after boys first and up to 5 Pupil Premium places. There is no sibling priority. For 2027 entry the pass mark was 345, so the Priority Area needed 385.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1.1. Priority Area: looked after / previously looked after boys Category 1 (Priority Area, 130 places), criterion 1. A looked-after child, or a child previously looked after who then became subject to an adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order (including children adopted from state care outside England). He needs the grammar assessment only, not the higher Priority Area score.
  2. 1.2. Priority Area: Pupil Premium boys scoring 20+ points above the threshold (up to 13 places) Up to 13 Priority Area places for boys currently eligible for Pupil Premium whose Kent Test aggregate is at least 20 points above the threshold (365 for 2027 entry), in score order.
  3. 1.3. Priority Area: boys scoring 40+ points above the threshold The rest of the 130 Priority Area places go in score order to boys living in the Priority Area whose aggregate is at least 40 points above the threshold (385 for 2027 entry). A Priority Area boy below 385 is still considered in Category 2.
  4. 2.1. All addresses: looked after / previously looked after boys Category 2 (all applicants regardless of address, 50 places plus any unused Priority Area places), criterion 1. A looked-after child, or a child previously looked after who then became subject to an adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order (including children adopted from state care outside England).
  5. 2.2. All addresses: Pupil Premium boys scoring 20+ points above the threshold (up to 5 places) Up to 5 places, from any address, for Pupil Premium boys scoring at least 20 points above the threshold (365 for 2027 entry), in score order.
  6. 2.3. All addresses: all remaining eligible boys, by score All remaining boys with a grammar assessment, from any address (including Priority Area boys not yet offered), in score order.

Good to know

  • About two-thirds of applications come from outside Kent (1,008 of 1,574 for 2026 entry), but only 47 of those boys were offered places.
  • There is no sibling priority.
  • The school's appeals deadline for 2027 entry is 7 April 2027.
  • Late paper registration for the Kent Test (to 21 July 2026) is only for children at Kent County Council primary schools.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
