Royal Latin School

Buckingham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 121 or more in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Test score gives no further priority. Among qualified children, priority goes to looked-after children, Pupil Premium children in catchment, staff children, catchment, siblings, medical/social need, then distance. Up to 12 places go to looked-after or catchment Pupil Premium children scoring 115-120.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified children in care and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Pupil Premium children living in catchment Qualified children living in catchment who qualify for Pupil Premium or Service Premium at the application deadline.
  3. c. Up to 12 places: looked-after children, or catchment Pupil Premium children, scoring 115-120 A route for children who did NOT qualify. Up to 12 places go to children scoring 115-120 who are looked after / previously looked after (living anywhere) or on Pupil Premium and living in catchment. Looked-after children come first, then distance.
  4. d. Children of staff Qualified children of staff with at least two years' permanent employment (or recruited to a skill-shortage post).
  5. e. Lives in catchment Qualified children living in the catchment area, resident at their home address continuously since 1 September 2026.
  6. f. Siblings at the school Qualified children with a sibling who will be at the school when they start. This comes after catchment.
  7. g. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified children whose exceptional medical or social needs can only be met at the Royal Latin School.
  8. h. Everyone else, nearest first Remaining qualified children, by straight-line distance from the front door to the school's nearest entrance gate (Buckinghamshire scheme definition).

Good to know

  • Catchment comes before siblings. In 2025 places ran out partway through the siblings criterion (at 7.459 miles), so siblings living outside the catchment weren't guaranteed a place.
  • A higher test score doesn't help: places depend on the admission rules and distance.
  • Twins and other multiple qualifiers from the same address are all admitted, even above the PAN.
  • The school may ask you to prove your home address. Owning another property doesn't by itself decide where you live.
