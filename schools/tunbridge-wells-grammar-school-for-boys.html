Tunbridge Wells Grammar School for Boys

Tunbridge Wells

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must pass the Kent Test. His score doesn't matter beyond that. Places go to looked-after children, then siblings, health reasons, boys within 3 miles, boys in named West Kent parishes, then everyone else, by distance to the Tunbridge Wells campus. Within each criterion, Pupil Premium boys (with the SIF) come first. After offers, the 90 boys living nearest the Sevenoaks campus are placed there, and the other 210 at Tunbridge Wells.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in local authority care, and children previously looked after (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight from care), including those who left state care outside England by being adopted.
  2. 2. Current family association (brother or sister at the school) Boys with a brother or sister at TWGSB when they start (the Sixth Form takes girls, so a sister can count).
  3. 3. Health and special access reasons Applied in line with the Equality Act 2010.
  4. 4. Lives within 3 miles of the school Boys living within a 3-mile straight-line radius of the school, nearest first.
  5. 5. Lives in a named parish Boys in one of 34 named parishes (Tunbridge Wells, Tonbridge, Sevenoaks, Edenbridge, Pembury and villages), nearest first.
  6. 6. All other children, by distance Everyone else, nearest to the Tunbridge Wells campus first.

Good to know

  • Any grammar-assessed boy is eligible: his score doesn't affect his chances.
  • You can't choose a campus. The school assigns campuses after offer day by distance, and you can join a waiting list for the other campus.
  • Late offers and successful appeals rarely get a Sevenoaks place.
  • The furthest distance offered has averaged 18.81 miles over the last three years.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
