Bourne Grammar School

Bourne

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score at least 220 in the Lincolnshire consortium 11+ (two GL reasoning papers). If more qualified children apply than there are places, looked-after children come first, then siblings, then children of staff, then up to 35 Pupil Premium or service children (nearest first), then the 24 highest scorers living within 15 miles, then everyone else by distance. The last place on distance in 2025 went at about 7 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including children adopted from state care outside England) who have qualified.
  2. 2. Sibling on roll Children with a brother or sister on roll at the school (including the sixth form) when the application is made. Siblings in the same year group are treated as one application.
  3. 3. Children of staff Children of staff.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium or service children (35 places) 35 places for qualified children eligible for Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium, nearest first.
  5. 5. 24 highest scorers within 15 miles After criteria 1-4, the next 24 places go to the highest 11+ scorers living within 15 miles. Used only for the main Year 7 transfer, not in-year places.
  6. 6. Distance from home All remaining places by distance, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Once your child qualifies, most places go by distance. The 24 score-based places for children within 15 miles are the only route where a higher score helps.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
