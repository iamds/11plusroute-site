The Spires College

Torquay

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

The Spires is a bilateral school with two streams. Up to 60 places go to the selective stream, in score order, to children who reach the College's eligible score in the shared Torbay 11+ (GL English and maths, 19 September 2026). All other places, including any unfilled selective places, are non-selective: looked-after children, then children of staff, siblings, children in the designated area, and everyone else. Children who don't sit the test, or don't qualify, are considered for non-selective places automatically. In 2026 every place was filled and none went to children outside the designated area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. S. Selective stream: highest 11+ scores at or above the eligible score Up to 60 selective places, filled in Torbay 11+ score order from children who reach the eligible score set by the Admissions Panel. If several children tie on score for the final place, the non-selective criteria below decide it. Children who don't get a selective place are automatically considered for a non-selective place; you don't name the school twice.
  2. 1. Cared for / previously cared for children Children in care and children previously in care (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order), including those adopted from state care outside England.
  3. 2. Children of staff Children of staff with at least two years' service or recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  4. 3. Siblings Children with a brother or sister at the College when the application goes in.
  5. 4. Living in the designated area Other children living in the College's designated area. Those furthest from Torquay Academy come first, so that families east of the College (towards the sea) aren't ranked below those living between The Spires and Torquay Academy.
  6. 5. Living outside the designated area Everyone else, nearest to The Spires College first.

Good to know

  • Selective places go purely by score: looked-after children, siblings and the designated area only matter to break a tie for the last place.
  • If one twin or other same-year sibling gets a place, the others do too, but not necessarily a selective place.
  • In 2026 every place was filled and none went to children outside the designated area.
  • GL's free familiarisation material has sample questions; only the English and maths sections apply.
