Poole Grammar School

Poole

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the shared Bournemouth and Poole consortium test (maths, English and verbal reasoning) in late September, at Bournemouth School or Poole Grammar, and must reach the required standard. If more eligible boys apply than the 180 places, looked-after boys come first, then Pupil Premium boys in the priority area (historic Borough of Poole or postcodes BH12-BH18 and BH21 3), then Pupil Premium boys outside it, then sons of staff, then other boys in the priority area, then everyone else. Within each group places go in test-score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after boys Eligible boys who are looked after or were previously looked after, in England or another country, in score order.
  2. b. Pupil Premium, in the priority area Eligible boys living in the historic Borough of Poole or Poole postcodes BH12-BH18 and BH21 3 who receive Pupil Premium, in score order.
  3. c. Pupil Premium, outside the priority area Eligible boys living outside the priority area who receive Pupil Premium, in score order.
  4. d. Sons of staff (employed 2+ years) Eligible sons of current staff employed at the school for at least two years, in score order.
  5. e. Lives in the priority area Other eligible boys living in the historic Borough of Poole or Poole postcodes BH12-BH18 and BH21 3 (not receiving Pupil Premium), in score order.
  6. f. Lives outside the priority area Other eligible boys living outside the priority area (not receiving Pupil Premium), in score order.

Good to know

  • Results arrive on 16 October 2026, before the 31 October council application deadline.
  • Reaching the required standard doesn't guarantee a place: in 2026 only 17 of 148 out-of-area applicants were offered places, against 145 of 154 in the priority area.
  • One test covers all four Bournemouth and Poole grammar schools, and your son can sit it at Bournemouth School or Poole Grammar, whichever is more convenient.
  • Within each priority group, places go purely in test-score order; distance is only used to break a tie for the last place.
  • The consortium moves to Quest Assessments tests for September 2028 entry (tests in September 2027).
  • GL Assessment publishes free 11+ familiarisation papers in maths, English and verbal reasoning. Answers go on a pre-printed answer sheet, and time limits are strict.
