Bournemouth School for Girls

Bournemouth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the shared Bournemouth and Poole consortium test (GL maths, English and verbal reasoning) in late September. Girls who reach the required standard are 'eligible' and are ranked: looked-after girls, then Pupil Premium girls by score, then 100 places for the highest scorers wherever they live, then girls in the catchment area (Bournemouth postcodes BH1-BH11 plus parts of BH12 and BH23) by score, then everyone else by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after children Eligible looked-after, previously looked-after and internationally adopted previously looked-after girls, all with equal priority. The general tie-break is distance.
  2. ii. Pupil Premium Girls eligible for the Pupil Premium Grant, in order of test score. No address requirement.
  3. iii. Top 100 scorers 100 places go to the girls with the highest total test scores, regardless of address.
  4. iv. Lives in the catchment area Remaining eligible girls living in the designated catchment area who aren't Pupil Premium, in order of test score.
  5. v. All other eligible girls Remaining eligible girls (outside the catchment area, not Pupil Premium), in order of test score.

Good to know

  • The four Bournemouth and Poole grammar schools share one test, so one sitting lets your daughter be ranked for both BSG and Parkstone.
  • 100 places go to the highest scorers wherever they live. Girls not placed this way are still considered under the later criteria.
  • From 2028/29 the Pupil Premium priority also covers Service Premium, and the 100 top-scorer places stay.
  • The consortium test switches to Quest Assessments for September 2028 entry (tests in September 2027).
