King Edward VI School (Stratford-upon-Avon)

Stratford-upon-Avon

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Warwickshire 11+ (shared West Midlands test). Places go first to looked-after boys and Pupil Premium (FSM) boys in the priority circles who reach the qualifying score or come within 20 marks of it, then up to 70 places to boys in the 13-mile inner circle who reach the qualifying score, then other boys within the 16.9-mile outer circle, all in score order. For September 2027 the school will admit 116, above its PAN of 87.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the AQS or come within 20 marks of it. No residence condition.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (FSM) boys in the priority circles Boys living within the outer priority circle (which includes the inner circle) who have had free school meals in the last six years and reach the AQS or come within 20 marks of it. Capped at 13 places.
  3. 3. Inner priority circle (13 miles), up to 70 places Up to 70 places for boys living within 13 miles of the Fountain in Rother Street who reach the AQS, highest scores first. Inner-circle boys who miss out here are considered again in category 4.
  4. 4. Outer priority circle (16.936 miles, including the inner circle) Other boys living within the outer priority circle, including inner-circle boys not placed under category 3, who reach the AQS. Highest scores first. In recent years this is where places run out.
  5. 5. Outside the priority circles, at or above the AQS Boys living outside the outer circle who reach the AQS. They get first-round offers only if there are not enough qualifying boys inside the circles. Otherwise they normally go on the waiting list.
  6. 6. Below the AQS but at or above the waiting-list score Boys between the waiting-list score and the AQS. In practice this is a waiting-list category: boys below the AQS go on the waiting list, and no first-round offers went to this category in 2023-2026.

Good to know

  • The school will admit 116 boys in September 2027, above its PAN of 87, with up to 70 inner-circle places.
  • In 2023-2026 every place went to boys living inside the outer priority circle.
  • Outer-circle places needed scores of 245-263, well above the AQS. Inner-circle boys only needed the AQS.
  • There is no twin concession: if one twin scores above the AQS and the other below, the second gets no priority.
