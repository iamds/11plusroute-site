South Wilts Grammar School

Salisbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the school's own 11+ (three GL Assessment multiple-choice papers: verbal reasoning, maths and English) on Saturday 26 September 2026, after registering online by 1 September. They must reach the cut-off score; Pupil Premium girls (including looked-after girls) need a mark 3% lower, with 10 places reserved for them. If more eligible girls apply than the 160 places, looked-after girls come first, then Pupil Premium girls in the catchment area and Service Premium girls, then all girls in the catchment area, then Pupil Premium girls outside it, children of staff, siblings outside the catchment, and everyone else by straight-line distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. b. Looked after / previously looked after girls Eligible girls who are looked after or were previously looked after (including those adopted from state care abroad).
  2. c(i). Pupil Premium girls in the catchment area Eligible girls who attract the Pupil Premium and live in the catchment area.
  3. c(ii). Service Premium girls Eligible girls who attract the Service Premium (a parent serving in the UK regular armed forces, or who served in the last six years, or who died in service with a pension paid), wherever they live.
  4. d. Lives in the catchment area Other eligible girls permanently living in the catchment area (resident since 1 May 2026).
  5. e. Pupil Premium girls outside the catchment area Eligible girls who attract the Pupil Premium and live outside the catchment area.
  6. f. Children of staff (employed 2+ years) Eligible girls living with a parent or step-parent who has been on the school's staff for at least two years.
  7. g. Sibling at the school, outside the catchment area Eligible girls living outside the catchment area with a sibling at the school.
  8. h. Everyone else, by distance Other eligible girls, nearest first (straight line).

Good to know

  • Test score isn't used to rank eligible girls: places go by the criteria, with nearest first.
  • All girls living in the catchment area come before siblings living outside it.
  • Pupil Premium girls qualify with a mark 3% lower, and 10 places are reserved for them.
  • To count as living at an address, 11+ applicants must have lived there since 1 May 2026. A rental must be for at least 12 months.
  • There are no concessions for twins or other multiple births.
