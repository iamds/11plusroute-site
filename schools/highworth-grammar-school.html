Highworth Grammar School

Ashford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must be assessed suitable for grammar school through the Kent Test. If more qualified girls apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after girls, then girls on targeted free school meals/Pupil Premium (supplementary form needed), then sisters of current pupils, then health/special access reasons, then everyone else by straight-line distance. There is no catchment area and test scores don't rank qualified girls.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who have passed come first.
  2. 2. Targeted free school meals and Pupil Premium Girls receiving targeted free school meals and Pupil Premium, if the supplementary form reaches the school by 31 October. Expanded FSM (the wider Universal Credit entitlement) doesn't count.
  3. 3. Current family association (sister/brother at the school) Girls with a sibling at the school when they start.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons Girls (or families) with a significant medical, health, social or access need that only this school can meet, backed by professional evidence.
  5. 5. Nearness of home to school All other qualified girls, nearest first (straight-line distance).

Good to know

  • The school has sometimes taken more than its 210 places: 214 offers were made for September 2026 entry.
  • Only targeted free school meals and Pupil Premium count for priority. Expanded FSM alone doesn't qualify.
  • The school takes boys in the sixth form, so a brother there counts as a sibling.
  • Twins and triplets are all admitted, even if this takes the school over its places.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
