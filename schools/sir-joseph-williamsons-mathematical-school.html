Sir Joseph Williamson's Mathematical School

Rochester

September 2026 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test. These are the school's 2026/27 admission rules; its 2027/28 rules haven't been published yet. If more grammar-assessed boys apply than the 203 places, priority goes to looked-after children, then boys with a brother at the school, then children of staff. Everyone else is ranked by distance by road. Test score gives no priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked after boys and previously looked after boys (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight after being looked after), including those who were in state care outside England and left it by being adopted.
  2. 2. Brother at the school Boys who will have a brother at SJWMS when they start, living in the same house (natural, adopted, step or foster). Cousins don't count.
  3. 3. Children of staff Sons of staff who have worked at SJWMS for at least two years when you apply, or who fill a skill-shortage post.
  4. 4. Nearest to the school (route distance) All other grammar-assessed boys, nearest first, by the shortest route along roads and paths known to Medway Council's GIS.

Good to know

  • Places go mainly by distance by road: in September 2026 the furthest boy offered a place lived about 3.3 miles (5,388 m) away.
  • Once your son is assessed as grammar, a higher Medway Test score gives no extra priority.
  • Medway Test results arrive on 14 October 2026, before the 31 October application deadline.
  • Girls join only in the sixth form; Year 7 is boys only.
  • To ask for a place outside the normal age group, write or email the Headteacher, ideally with supporting evidence from a medical or other professional.
