Rainham Mark Grammar School

Gillingham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test. If more grammar-assessed children apply than the 235 places, priority goes to looked-after children and Pupil Premium/FSM children (both need the school's SIF), then brothers and sisters, health/special access reasons and staff children. Everyone else is ranked by route distance from home. Test score gives no priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked after children, previously looked after children (adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order) and children adopted from state care outside England, if assessed as grammar. You must send the school's SIF and proof of status.
  2. ii. Pupil Premium / Free School Meals Children registered for free school meals at any time in the last six years (Universal Infant Free School Meals alone doesn't count). You must send the SIF by 31 October 2026.
  3. iii. Brother or sister at the school Children with a brother or sister at RMGS when they start. Multiple-birth siblings are all offered places even if that takes the school above PAN.
  4. iv. Health and special access reasons Children who, because of their own or a parent's health or social needs, have a significant need to attend RMGS, supported by professional evidence.
  5. v. Children of staff Children of staff employed at RMGS for at least three years, or recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  6. vi. Nearness of home to school All other grammar-assessed children, nearest first by the shortest route measured by Medway Council's GIS.

Good to know

  • In September 2026 the furthest distance offered was 4,160.96 m (about 2.6 miles) by route.
  • There is only one test date: no alternative for clashes, no resit and no remark.
  • Medway Test results arrive before the application deadline, so you know whether your child is assessed as grammar before you apply.
  • Medway Council's waiting list for RMGS is only for parents who ranked it above the school they were offered.
  • Medway Council publishes a free familiarisation booklet showing the format and layout of the test papers.
