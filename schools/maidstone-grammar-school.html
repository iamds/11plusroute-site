Maidstone Grammar School

Maidstone

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must pass the Kent Test. Boys from a list of local parishes who score 360 or more get priority, then other qualifying boys from those parishes, then boys from elsewhere. Within each group: free school meals, then siblings, then staff children, then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualifying boys who are looked after or previously looked after.
  2. 2. Listed parishes, Kent Test score 360+ Qualifying boys living in one of the listed parishes with a Kent Test aggregate of 360 or more. Within this group: free school meals, then siblings, then children of staff, then distance.
  3. 3. Listed parishes, any pass Other qualifying boys from the listed parishes, whatever their score. Same order within: free school meals, siblings, staff children, distance.
  4. 4. Outside the listed parishes Qualifying boys from outside the listed parishes. Same order within: free school meals, siblings, staff children, distance.

Good to know

  • In 2026, 97 places went to local boys scoring 360 or more and 102 to other local boys, with distance offers reaching about 10 miles.
  • Only 4 places in 2026 went to boys from outside the listed parishes.
  • Appeal panels have taken the school over its admission number in most years, and it could take up to 216 boys in Year 7 for 2026.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
