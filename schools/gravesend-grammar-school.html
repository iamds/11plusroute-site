Gravesend Grammar School

Gravesend

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must pass the Kent Test; the score doesn't matter after that. Places go to looked-after boys, then medical/social need, siblings and staff sons, then boys living in Gravesham, then two bands of nearby parishes, then everyone else. Within every criterion, Pupil Premium boys come first, then the rest by distance. Families in the school's priority areas usually get a place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after boys, and boys previously looked after who were then adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Medical, health, social and special access reasons Boys with a demonstrable and significant need to attend this school because of their own, or a parent's, health or social needs. Needs written evidence from a suitably qualified medical or other practitioner showing a special connection with the school.
  3. 3. Sibling at the school Boys with a brother or sister (in the same house, including step and foster siblings) who will be at the school when he joins.
  4. 4. Children of staff Sons of staff employed for at least two years, or recruited to a skills-shortage post.
  5. 5. Lives in the Borough of Gravesham Boys living in the Borough of Gravesham.
  6. 6. Lives in the first group of named parishes Boys living in Ash-cum-Ridley, Bean, Fawkham, Hartley, Longfield and New Barn, Southfleet, Swanscombe and Greenhithe, Stansted or the Unparished Area of Ebbsfleet.
  7. 7. Lives in the second group of named parishes Boys living in Stone, Darenth, Horton Kirby and South Darenth, Cliffe or Cliffe Woods.
  8. 8. All other eligible boys All other boys assessed suitable for grammar school.

Good to know

  • Families in the school's priority areas usually get a place.
  • Pupil Premium boys only get priority if the school receives the supplementary form by 31 October.
  • The school filled all 210 places in 2024 and 2025.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
