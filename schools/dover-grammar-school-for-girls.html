Dover Grammar School for Girls

Dover

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter qualifies by passing either the Kent Test or the school's own Dover Assessment Test (she can sit both; passing one is enough). If more qualified girls apply than the 140 places, priority goes to looked-after girls, then sisters/brothers at the school, health/special access needs, Pupil Premium girls, then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, and children previously in care (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order straight after care), including those adopted from state care outside England. No address requirement.
  2. 2. Brother or sister at the school A brother or sister in the school when your daughter starts: children living as brother and sister in the same house, including adopted, step- and foster siblings.
  3. 3. Health and special access reasons Applied in line with the Equality Act 2010. You need written professional evidence linking the need to DGGS specifically.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium Girls registered for (targeted) free school meals at any time in the last 6 years. Return the school's form with evidence by 31 October and name DGGS on your application.
  5. 5. Distance from home to school Everyone else, nearest first, by straight-line distance from home to school.

Good to know

  • The school has filled all 140 places in each of the last three years.
  • Kent uses an equal-preference system, so the order of your preferences only matters if more than one school could offer a place.
  • Only 'targeted' free school meals count for Pupil Premium priority. Eligibility under Expanded Free School Meals alone doesn't qualify.
  • The Headteacher Assessment Panel can still find a child suitable for grammar school if her Kent Test scores fall short.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
