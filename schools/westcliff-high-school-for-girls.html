Westcliff High School for Girls

Westcliff-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (English and maths) and must reach the pass mark. After looked-after girls, up to 154 places go to girls living in postcode areas SS0-SS9 in score order (15 of them kept for girls on free school meals or the Pupil Premium). The other 38 places go to the highest scorers from outside SS0-SS9 (4 kept for free school meals / Pupil Premium).

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked-after / previously looked-after girls who pass Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who pass the test, wherever they live.
  2. ii(a). Catchment (SS0-SS9): up to 15 'preferential consideration' places (FSM / Pupil Premium) First, up to 15 catchment places go to the highest-scoring girls who pass and were receiving free school meals or the Pupil Premium when they registered for the test.
  3. ii(b). Catchment (SS0-SS9): everyone else by score, up to 154 places in all The rest of the catchment quota goes to girls living in postcode areas SS0-SS9 who pass, highest score first. The catchment category is full when 154 places are used or no more catchment girls who passed have applied.
  4. iii(a). Outside SS0-SS9: up to 4 'preferential consideration' places Of the places left after the catchment quota, up to 4 go to the highest-scoring girls from outside SS0-SS9 who pass and qualify for preferential consideration.
  5. iii(b). Outside SS0-SS9: everyone else by score All remaining places go to girls from outside SS0-SS9 who pass, highest score first, until the PAN of 192 is reached.

Good to know

  • The intake rose to 192 from 2026 (it was 184 in 2022-2025).
  • Girls on free school meals or the Pupil Premium are on both the preferential list and the full list for their area, so they get two chances.
  • The lowest scores offered in 2026 were 303 inside SS0-SS9 and 332 outside.
  • Results arrive on 12 October, before the 31 October application deadline.
  • The published lowest scores leave out places given for preferential consideration and on appeal.
  • Free sample papers are on the CSSE website (csse.org.uk), and past papers can be bought from the CSSE.
