Calday Grange Grammar School

Wirral

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must reach the qualifying score in the Wirral 11+ (register with Wirral Council by 31 May in Year 5). If more qualified boys apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after children, then medical/social need, then up to 15 Free School Meals boys, then siblings, then shortest distance from the school by road. A higher test score gives no extra priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after boys and boys who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order.
  2. 2. Medical or social reason Boys with a valid medical or social reason, backed in writing by a doctor, social worker or appropriate professional.
  3. 3. Free School Meals (up to 15 places) Up to 15 places for boys on Free School Meals when the application is made, in order of shortest walking distance. FSM boys must still reach the qualifying score.
  4. 4. Siblings Boys who will have a brother (same parents, half, step, or adopted in the same household) at the school when they start.
  5. 5. Shortest distance All other qualified boys, nearest first, by shortest walking distance on Wirral Council's computer mapping system.

Good to know

  • In 2026 places went down to the distance criterion for the first time in recent years, with the last offer at 14.68 miles.
  • In 2024 and 2025 every qualified boy who applied was offered a place.
  • The test is only a pass/fail gate: among qualified boys, places go by the criteria (mostly distance), not by score.
  • 2026 was the first year Quest Assessments set the test; the qualifying standard, format and weightings stayed the same.
