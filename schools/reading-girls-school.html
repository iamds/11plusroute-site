Reading Girls' School

Reading

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

An all-ability girls' school with a selective stream. After looked-after girls, up to 42 places go to girls ranked highest in the school's own selection tests (sat at the school in late September/early October; apply direct to the school). The other places go by social/medical need, siblings, children of staff, the feeder school (Battle Primary Academy) and distance. Girls who don't sit the test can still apply for the other places. In 2025 and 2026 the school had fewer applicants than places, so every applicant got a place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and all previously looked-after girls, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Selective stream: up to 42 places by test results Up to 42 places for girls selected by ability in the school's selection tests, ranked by their results. Girls who don't get a selective place are considered under criteria 3-7.
  3. 3. Social or medical need Girls with a social or medical need, backed by a professional's written confirmation that this is the right school for her. The school is largely led by medical practitioners' recommendations.
  4. 4. Siblings Girls with a sibling at the school.
  5. 5. Children of staff Children of staff with two years' service or recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  6. 6. Attending the feeder school Girls attending the named feeder school, Battle Primary Academy.
  7. 7. Distance All other girls, nearest first (straight line between Ordnance Survey data points for the home and the school, using Brighter Futures for Children's mapping software).

Good to know

  • In 2025 and 2026 the school had fewer applicants than its 180 places, so every applicant got a place.
  • All 42 selective places were filled in 2023.
  • Girls who don't sit the selection test can still apply for the other places.
  • Selective stream pupils don't get help with school transport from Reading unless they qualify under the council's transport policy for other reasons.
