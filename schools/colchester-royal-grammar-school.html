Colchester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (English and maths). Up to 12 places go to looked-after boys and then Pupil Premium boys who score more than 320. All other places go to the highest scorers. There is no catchment or priority area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Up to 12 places: looked-after boys, then Pupil Premium boys, scoring over 320 Up to 12 places go first to looked-after or previously looked-after boys, then to boys receiving the Pupil Premium Grant when they registered for the test, who score more than 320, highest score first.
  2. 2. All other places: highest scorers The remaining places go to the highest-scoring boys who named CRGS, wherever they live, until 128 places are filled.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment or priority area: after Priority 1, places go purely by score.
  • A looked-after or Pupil Premium boy scoring 320 or below can still win a place if his score is high enough (recent last scores were 337-346).
  • Free English and maths familiarisation papers can be downloaded from the CSSE website. Shop-bought practice books are at a similar level but not in the same format as the real papers.
