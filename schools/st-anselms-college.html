St Anselm's College

Prenton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

He sits St Anselm's own entrance exam (English, maths and verbal reasoning papers) and must pass at least two of the three (55% in maths, 55% in English, 118 in verbal reasoning). If more than 156 qualifying boys apply, places go in faith-based order: looked-after Catholic boys first, then Catholic boys living in Wirral, Cheshire West and Chester or within 10 miles, then other looked-after boys, then baptised Christian boys in that area, then non-baptised boys whose parents want a Catholic education. Within each group, boys on Free School Meals come first, then the highest exam scores.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Baptised Catholic looked-after / previously looked-after boys Baptised Catholic boys who are looked after, or were previously looked after (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, including adoption from state care abroad). No residence requirement.
  2. 2. Baptised Catholic boys in the relevant area Baptised Catholic boys living in Wirral, Cheshire West and Chester, or anywhere else within 10 miles of the college.
  3. 3. All other looked-after / previously looked-after boys Looked-after or previously looked-after boys who aren't baptised Catholic.
  4. 4. Baptised Christian (non-Catholic) boys in the relevant area Boys baptised into another Christian church, living in Wirral, Cheshire West and Chester, or within 10 miles of the college.
  5. 5. Non-baptised boys wanting a Catholic education Boys who aren't baptised and whose parents want them to have a Catholic education. No residence requirement. In 2026 the last places went to this category, in score order.

Good to know

  • He doesn't have to pass all three papers: reaching the pass mark in any two of maths (55%), English (55%) and verbal reasoning (118) is enough.
  • The entrance exam is run by the college itself and isn't part of the Wirral 11+, so don't use Wirral's Request for Assessment form.
  • Faith only needs a copy of a baptismal or Holy Communion certificate sent with the online registration form; there is no Mass-attendance requirement.
  • Baptised Catholic or Christian boys living outside Wirral, Cheshire West and Chester and the 10-mile radius don't fit any of the faith criteria, so ask the college how they are treated.
  • In 2026 the last places went to non-baptised boys (category 5), in score order.
  • If he is unsuccessful, he can re-sit the exam 12 months later.
