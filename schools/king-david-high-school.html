King David High School

Liverpool

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Not a grammar school. This is an all-ability Jewish (Modern Orthodox) school in Childwall. There is no academic test. Up to 18 places go to the top scorers in an optional music aptitude assessment, ranked below looked-after children, children meeting the Jewish practice criteria and King David Primary pupils. Everyone else applies under staff, sibling, other-faith and 'all other' criteria, by distance. Children who don't take the music assessment still apply under those criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A1. Looked after / previously looked after children meeting the Jewish practice criteria Looked-after and previously looked-after children (adoption, residence/child arrangements or special guardianship order) who reach 4 points on the Jewish Certificate of Religious Practice. Those with fewer points are considered under the next category that fits.
  2. A2. Other looked after / previously looked after children All other looked-after and previously looked-after children.
  3. A3. Children meeting the Jewish Religious Practice Criteria Children who reach 4 points on the Certificate of Religious Practice. Points: synagogue attendance on Friday night or Shabbat morning at least 8 times between 17 April and 24 October 2026 (4 points; at least 4 times = 2), Jewish educational activities on 6+ occasions (2), Jewish communal/charitable volunteering on 12+ occasions by parent or child (2), or the Tribe CRP Judaism course (2). There is no advantage in scoring more than 4.
  4. A4. All other children attending King David Primary School Children at King David Primary School on 31 October 2026.
  5. A5. Aptitude for music (up to 18 places) Up to 18 places for children who show an aptitude (not ability) for music. Successful children are encouraged to contribute to the school's musical life. A child who misses out is reconsidered under A6-A9 if the parent ticks that option on the SIF, with no advantage or disadvantage.
  6. A6. Children of staff Children of teaching and support staff directly employed by the school.
  7. A7. Siblings Children with a brother or sister on roll at King David High School at the time of application.
  8. A8. Other faiths, with religious commitment Children of other faiths whose families show religious commitment, by distance.
  9. A9. All other applicants Everyone else, by distance.
