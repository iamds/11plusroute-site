Skegness Grammar School

Skegness

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 220 in the Lincolnshire 11+, or 210 if they get the Pupil Premium or Service Premium (declared when registering). Unusually, qualified children are not ranked by score: after looked-after children and siblings, places go to children in the designated free transport area, then by distance from the school. Score only breaks a tie for the last place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Sibling on roll Children with a sibling at the school when they start. The sibling must live at the same address, as part of the same family unit.
  3. 3. Lives in the designated free transport area Eligible children whose address is in the academy's designated area for free transport.
  4. 4. Distance from the school Everyone else, nearest first by straight-line distance.
  5. 5. Other children Any other eligible children. In practice criterion 4 already covers everyone left.

Good to know

  • Places are not given in score order: qualified children are separated by distance, and the 11+ score only breaks a tie for the last place.
  • The qualifying score is 220, or 210 for Pupil Premium or Service Premium children.
  • Twins and other multiple births are all admitted if the last place goes to one of them, even above the PAN.
  • A 2028 admissions policy is out for consultation, which may change things for later years.
