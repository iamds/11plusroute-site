Heckmondwike Grammar School

Heckmondwike

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child sits the school's own reasoning test in mid-September and must reach the 'standard required for selection'. If more children reach the standard than there are 210 places, places go first to looked-after children, then Pupil Premium children, then children living in the school's catchment area, then siblings, then children of staff, then up to 5% of places for children with a Grade 2 music certificate. All remaining places go in order of test score. Most places (144 of 210 in 2026) are filled by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children of selective ability who are in public care (in or outside England), or were and stopped being because of adoption, a residence order or a special guardianship order.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Children of selective ability who are eligible for the Pupil Premium. Proof must reach the school by noon on 26 June 2026.
  3. 3. Lives in the catchment area Children of selective ability whose permanent address on the day of the entrance exam is in the school's catchment area (map on the school website).
  4. 4. Sibling at the school Children of selective ability with a sibling at the school, including the Sixth Form, who will still be there when the child is admitted.
  5. 5. Child of a member of staff Children of selective ability whose parent has worked at the school for at least two years when the exam is sat, or was recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  6. 6. Grade 2 or above in music (up to 5% of offers) Children of selective ability with a Grade 2 or higher solo performance certificate. If more qualify than the 5% cap, the highest scorers in the entrance exam get these places.
  7. 7. All other qualifying children by test score Once criteria 1-6 have been applied, the remaining places go in order of overall performance in the entrance exam.

Good to know

  • Most places go by test score: the priority groups took 63-77 of the 210 places in 2024-2026.
  • Music places are capped at 5% of offers (about 10) and have been almost full each year.
  • The council offers the highest-preference school your child is eligible for, so put the school first if it's your top choice.
  • Appeals are heard in June. Details go on the school website after 1 March.
