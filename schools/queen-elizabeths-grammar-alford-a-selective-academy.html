Queen Elizabeth's Grammar Alford - A Selective Academy

Alford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must reach 220 in the Lincolnshire 11+. If more qualifiers apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after children, then children living in the designated free transport area (siblings, then Pupil Premium, then the rest), then children at the area's primary schools, then everyone else. Each group is ranked by score. Historically, every qualified child who lives in the area or attends one of its primary schools has got a place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified children in public care or previously in public care, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. In the transport area, with a sibling at the school Qualified children living in the designated free transport area (the traditional catchment) who have a sibling at the school.
  3. 3. In the transport area, Pupil Premium Qualified children living in the designated free transport area who are eligible for the pupil premium.
  4. 4. Lives in the designated free transport area All other qualified children living in the designated free transport area.
  5. 5. At a transport-area primary, with a sibling at the school Qualified children who live outside the transport area but attend one of its primary schools, and have a sibling at the school. They must be at the primary school by 14 December to count for National Offer Day.
  6. 6. At a primary school in the transport area Qualified children who live outside the transport area but attend one of its primary schools.
  7. 7. Outside the area, with a sibling at the school Qualified children who neither live in the transport area nor attend one of its primary schools, and have a sibling at the school.
  8. 8. Outside the area, Pupil Premium or Service Premium Qualified children living outside the transport area who are eligible for the pupil premium or service premium.
  9. 9. All other qualified children by score Qualified children who neither live in the transport area nor attend one of its primary schools, in score order.

Good to know

  • Historically, every qualified child living in the designated free transport area, or attending a primary school there, has got a place.
  • Lincolnshire still treats applications made by 14 December 2026 as on time.
  • Inside the transport area only Pupil Premium gives extra priority; outside it, Pupil Premium or Service Premium both count.
  • You get one overall standardised score; raw and per-paper scores aren't released.
  • Appeal forms come from the Headteacher's PA after offers in March. Return them by the end of March so the appeal is heard on time.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
