Stroud High School

Stroud

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Girls who reach the qualifying standard are offered places in this order: looked-after girls, then girls from families eligible for Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium at the time of the test, then everyone else in test rank order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the qualifying standard.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium Girls from families eligible for Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium (parent serving in the armed forces, killed in service, left through injury or on full-time reserve service) at the time of the test, who reach the required standard. No limit on numbers.
  3. 3. Rank order from the entrance test Everyone else who reaches the qualifying standard, highest rank first.

Good to know

  • Siblings, feeder schools and catchment count for nothing: after looked-after and Pupil Premium girls, places go by test rank.
  • Gloucestershire uses an equal-preference scheme: you're offered the highest preference you qualify for.
  • Naming the school is only useful if your daughter reached its qualifying standard.
  • In 2026 the last rank offered a place was 396.
  • From 2028 entry the test moves to the end of Year 5 (Saturday 3 July 2027) with a new provider.
