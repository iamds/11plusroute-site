The King's School, Grantham

Grantham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must score at least 220 in the Lincolnshire consortium 11+ (two GL reasoning papers). If more qualified boys apply than there are places, looked-after boys come first, then up to 20 Pupil Premium boys living within 30 miles, then boys living within 30 miles, then everyone else. Within each group places go in order of 11+ score. The lowest score offered on National Offer Day 2026 was 242.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including children adopted from state care outside England) who have qualified. Ranked by 11+ score.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium boys within 30 miles (max 20) Up to 20 boys eligible for the full Pupil Premium who live within 30 miles, in order of 11+ score. Used only for the main Year 7 transfer, not in-year places.
  3. 3. Lives within 30 miles Boys living within 30 miles (straight line), in order of 11+ score.
  4. 4. All other qualified boys Any remaining places go to other qualified boys in order of 11+ score.

Good to know

  • The 30-mile area is so large that almost all applicants compete on 11+ score.
  • Apart from looked-after children, the up-to-20 Pupil Premium places are the only way in below the score cut-off.
  • The lowest score offered on National Offer Day 2026 was 242.
  • The school leaves places empty rather than admit boys scoring below 220.
