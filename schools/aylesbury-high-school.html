Aylesbury High School

Aylesbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter needs 121 or more in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Test score gives no further priority. Among qualified girls, priority goes to looked-after and Pupil Premium girls living in catchment, sisters of AHS or Aylesbury Grammar pupils, staff daughters, catchment girls, then everyone else by distance. Up to 6 places go to catchment Pupil Premium or looked-after girls scoring 115-120.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after girls living in catchment Qualified looked-after, previously looked-after or internationally adopted (previously looked-after) girls who have lived in the catchment area since 1 September 2026. This covers only girls living in catchment.
  2. b. Pupil Premium girls living in catchment Qualified girls on Pupil Premium (including Service Premium) who have lived in the catchment area since 1 September 2026.
  3. c. Up to 6 places: Pupil Premium or looked-after girls in catchment scoring 115-120 A route for girls who did NOT qualify. Up to 6 places go to girls scoring 115-120 who are on Pupil Premium or looked after / previously looked after and have lived in catchment since 1 September 2026.
  4. d. Sisters of AHS students or of boys at Aylesbury Grammar School Qualified girls with a sister at Aylesbury High School or a brother at Aylesbury Grammar School.
  5. e. Children of staff Qualified daughters of staff with at least two years' permanent employment (or recruited to a skill-shortage post).
  6. f. Lives in catchment Qualified girls who have lived in the catchment area since 1 September 2026.
  7. g. All other girls, nearest first All other qualified girls, by straight-line distance.

Good to know

  • Looked-after girls living outside the catchment area get no specific priority and are ranked with all other out-of-catchment girls by distance.
  • If one twin, triplet or same-year sibling is offered a place, all are, as long as they qualify, even above the admission number.
  • The last distance offered was 12.739 miles in 2026, 13.507 miles in 2025 and 15.072 miles in 2024.
