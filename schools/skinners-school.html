The Skinners' School

Tunbridge Wells

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must pass the Kent Test. 140 places go to boys in the West Kent Area (3 miles plus named parishes around Tunbridge Wells, Tonbridge and Sevenoaks) and 20 to boys living elsewhere. After looked-after children, up to 16 West Kent places go to Pupil Premium boys by score, and 108 to boys scoring at least 40 above the Kent pass mark (385 for 2027), brothers and sons of staff first, then nearest. 16 Governors' Places go to the highest-scoring West Kent boys left. The 20 Outer Area places use the same +40 and distance rule.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in local authority care, and children previously looked after (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight from care), including those who left state care outside England by being adopted. Must be assessed suitable for grammar school.
  2. 2a. Pupil Premium, living in the West Kent Area (up to 16 places) Up to 16 boys in the West Kent Area who are registered for Pupil Premium. If more than 16 apply, the highest Kent Test aggregates get the places. Spare places go to criterion 2b. You must return the school's SIF by 31 October and name Skinners' on the SCAF.
  3. 2b(i). West Kent Area, score at least 40 above the Kent threshold Within up to 108 places (criterion 2b): West Kent Area boys scoring at least 40 points above KCC's threshold (385 for September 2027 entry). Brothers come first, then sons of staff, then distance.
  4. 2b(ii). West Kent Area, assessed suitable (if 2b(i) doesn't fill 108) Only if fewer than 108 boys qualify under 2b(i): other grammar-assessed West Kent Area boys, in the same order (brothers, sons of staff, distance).
  5. 3. Governors' Places: highest scorers in the West Kent Area (up to 16 places) Up to 16 places for the highest-scoring West Kent Area boys who haven't already got a place.
  6. 4a(i). Outer Area, score at least 40 above the Kent threshold (up to 20 places) Up to 20 places for boys living outside the West Kent Area (anywhere on the UK mainland) who score at least 40 points above the threshold (385 for 2027): brothers, then sons of staff, then distance.
  7. 4a(ii). Outer Area, assessed suitable (if 4a(i) doesn't fill 20) Only if fewer than 20 boys qualify under 4a(i): other grammar-assessed Outer Area boys, in the same order.

Good to know

  • Boys scoring at least 40 above the Kent Test threshold (385 for 2027 entry) are considered first for most places.
  • For 2025 admission places reached 11 miles in the West Kent Area and 18 miles in the Outer Area.
  • KCC offers only your highest-ranked qualifying school, so put Skinners' first if it's your favourite.
  • Up to 16 Governors' Places go to the highest scorers in the West Kent Area who wouldn't otherwise get a place.
  • Year 7 is boys only; the Sixth Form takes girls.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
