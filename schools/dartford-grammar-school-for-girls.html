Dartford Grammar School for Girls

Dartford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must pass the Kent Test. Places then go in Kent Test score order: looked-after girls first, then all Pupil Premium girls, then 100 places for girls living in Dartford Borough or 10 nearby parishes, then the rest to girls from any address. Distance only breaks tied scores. There is no sibling priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after girls, and girls previously looked after who were then adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Pupil Premium girls Girls eligible for Pupil Premium when they start (Ever 6 FSM, including Targeted FSM). There is no address requirement and no extra score bar. Send the SIF with proof by 1 November 2026.
  3. 3. Lives in Dartford Borough or a named parish (100 places) 100 places for girls living in the Borough of Dartford or the parishes of Ash-cum-Ridley, Crockenhill, Eynsford, Farningham, Fawkham, Hartley, Hextable, Horton Kirby and South Darenth, Swanley or West Kingsdown, in score order.
  4. 4. All other eligible girls, any address Remaining places go to eligible girls from any address in Kent Test score order.

Good to know

  • Pupil Premium girls get priority wherever they live, with no higher score needed beyond the Kent Test pass.
  • The Supplementary Information Form deadline, 1 November 2026, is a Sunday, the day before the council deadline.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
