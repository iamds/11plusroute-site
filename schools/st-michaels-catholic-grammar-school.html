St Michael's Catholic Grammar School

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A Catholic girls' grammar school. In practice only baptised Catholic girls from a practising Catholic family (with a Certificate of Catholic Practice) are invited to the entrance test on 11 September 2026. Looked-after girls and Pupil Premium girls (up to 32) in the top 175 come first. All other eligible girls follow in test rank order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked-after Catholic girls (practising family) in the top 175 Baptised Catholic girls from a practising Catholic family who are looked after or previously looked after (including those adopted from state care abroad), ranked in the top 175. A looked-after child in a Catholic family who would have been baptised but for her care status counts as Catholic.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Catholic girls (practising family) in the top 175 (max 32) Baptised Catholic girls from a practising Catholic family who receive Pupil Premium or Service Premium and rank in the top 175. Up to 32, in rank order. Girls beyond the 32 can still get in under criterion 3 on rank.
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic girls from a practising Catholic family, in rank order All other baptised Catholic girls from a practising Catholic family, highest test score first. This is where most places are decided.
  4. a. Fallback: baptised Catholic girls Baptised Catholic girls (without the practice certificate), in rank order. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.
  5. b. Fallback: catechumens and Eastern Christian girls Girls who are catechumens or members of an Eastern Christian Church. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.
  6. c. Fallback: other Christian denominations Girls of other Christian denominations, in rank order. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.
  7. d. Fallback: other looked-after girls Other looked-after or previously looked-after girls not in a-c. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.
  8. e. Fallback: other Pupil Premium girls Other Pupil Premium (or Service Premium) girls not in a-c. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.
  9. f. Fallback: any other girls Any other girls. Only used if fewer than 175 girls meet criteria 1-3 and other girls are invited to the test.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area: distance is used only as a final tie-break.
  • Catholic girls need both a Catholic baptism (or reception) certificate and a Certificate of Catholic Practice.
  • The Certificate of Catholic Practice needs at least five years' Sunday Mass attendance by a Catholic parent and your daughter.
  • The original paper certificate must reach the school by 4pm on 7 July 2026; scans and emails aren't accepted.
