Fort Pitt Grammar School

Chatham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test. If more grammar-assessed children apply than the 150 places, priority goes to looked-after children, then brothers and sisters, staff children, children living within 2 miles, and children in 11 named parishes (mainly the Hoo Peninsula). Everyone else is ranked by walking distance. Year 7 has been mixed since September 2026.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked after children, and previously looked after children who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order, if assessed as grammar.
  2. 2. Brother or sister at the school Children with a brother or sister who will still be at Fort Pitt when they start.
  3. 3. Children of staff Children of staff employed at Fort Pitt for at least three years, or recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  4. 4. Lives within 2 miles of the school Children living within 2 miles, measured by Medway Council's GIS.
  5. 5. Lives in a named Hoo Peninsula / Medway Valley parish Children living (at the time of application) in one of 11 named parishes, mostly on the Hoo Peninsula plus Cuxton and Halling. Nearest first by shortest safe walking distance.
  6. 6. All other eligible children, nearest first All other grammar-assessed children, nearest first by the shortest safe walking distance (Medway GIS).

Good to know

  • Year 7 has taken boys since September 2026. Other year groups, apart from the sixth form, stay girls only.
  • In September 2026 the furthest place offered was about 2.06 miles away by route distance, just beyond the 2-mile priority radius.
  • There is no alternative date for the Medway Test if it clashes with another test, and no resit.
  • Medway's online school directory shows each school's applications, offers and furthest distance offered last year.
