Marling School

Stroud

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Boys who reach the qualifying standard are offered places in this order: looked-after boys, then boys on Pupil Premium, then everyone else in test rank order. Equal ranks go first to boys from Cotswold Beacon Academy Trust primary schools, then to whoever lives closer.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) who meet the qualifying standard.
  2. b. Pupil Premium Boys who have had free school meals at any point in the six years before test day and meet the qualifying standard. No limit on numbers.
  3. c. Other qualifying boys in test rank order All other boys who meet the qualifying standard, highest rank first.

Good to know

  • Siblings and catchment count for nothing: places go by test rank after looked-after and Pupil Premium boys.
  • In 2026 the last boy offered a place was ranked 538.
  • Gloucestershire offers your highest-ranked preference you qualify for, so only name Marling if your son met its qualifying standard.
  • From September 2028 entry the Gloucestershire grammar schools switch to an FSCE test sat in July at the end of Year 5.
  • Answers go on separate answer sheets that are marked electronically, and rough paper is provided.
  • Pupil Premium boys can get free 11+ practice materials through Atom Learning.
