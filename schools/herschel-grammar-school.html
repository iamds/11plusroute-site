Herschel Grammar School

Slough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 111 or more in the Slough Consortium 11+. Looked-after children come first, then everyone living within 4 miles of the school (nearest first), then up to 10 Pupil Premium children living within 10 miles, then staff children. Any places left go in score order. In 2025 and 2026 every eligible applicant within 4 miles got a place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after children, and previously looked-after children (adopted, child arrangements or special guardianship orders, including those adopted from state care abroad).
  2. ii. Lives within 4 miles (catchment area) Children whose home is within 4 miles of the school (straight line), nearest first.
  3. iii. Pupil Premium, living within 10 miles (up to 10 places) Up to 10 more places for Pupil Premium children living within 10 miles, nearest first.
  4. iv. Children of staff Children of permanent staff employed at the school for 2+ years before the closing date.
  5. v. Everyone else, in score order Remaining places up to 150 in score order; equal scores are ranked nearest first.

Good to know

  • In 2025 and 2026 every eligible applicant living within 4 miles got a place.
  • There is no sibling priority and no supplementary form.
  • The Consortium moves to Quest Assessments for the 2027 test (2028 entry).
  • A free examination familiarisation booklet is published on the Consortium schools' websites.
  • Children eligible for the Pupil Premium can get free Atom Learning resources, which include some practice 11+ style tests.
