Spalding Grammar School

Spalding

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must reach the Lincolnshire 'county standard' of 220 in the consortium 11+ (verbal and non-verbal reasoning). After looked-after children, all qualified boys living in Spalding and 26 South Holland feeder parishes come first. If there are more than 150 of them, the pass mark is raised, so places go by score. Qualified boys from outside the area fill any places left, highest score first. There is no sibling priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after boys get first priority. Includes children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Lives in Spalding or the feeder parishes Qualified boys living in Lincolnshire County Council's designated transport area for the school (Spalding and the feeder parishes) are considered before anyone outside it. If there are still more than 150, the qualifying score is raised to contain the numbers.
  3. 3. Lives outside the area, by score If fewer than 150 qualify from the area, qualified boys from outside it are offered places in score order (highest first) until 150 is reached.

Good to know

  • There is no sibling priority.
  • A qualifying score of 220 or more stays valid for the rest of your son's secondary education.
  • A boy who doesn't qualify can sit a late-entry test for Year 8 onwards.
  • Families of UK service personnel with a confirmed posting, and returning Crown servants, can apply in advance using a unit or intended address.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
