Ilford County High School

Ilford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Places go by score in the Redbridge 11+ test (GL Assessment), shared with Woodford County High School (girls). He must score at least 104 to be considered. Looked-after children come first, then up to 45 places for Pupil Premium children, then children living in the Common Catchment Area in score order. Children outside the catchment are only considered once everyone inside has been placed, and none have been admitted in the last 10 years.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach 104 come first. There is no catchment requirement.
  2. b. Pupil Premium (45 places) 45 places go to Pupil Premium children who reach 104, highest scores first. Redbridge checks eligibility (including 'Ever 6') as at the date of application. You must apply under this criterion, and he must have been entitled to Pupil Premium in Year 5.
  3. c. Other children in the Common Catchment Area Everyone else living in the 11+ Common Catchment Area, highest score first. In practice this is where the last places are decided.
  4. d. Children outside the Common Catchment Area Children living outside the catchment are considered only after all requests from inside it have been met. Only catchment children have been admitted in the last 10 years.

Good to know

  • Only children living in the Common Catchment Area have been admitted in the last 10 years, so check the map before registering.
  • The last in-catchment score offered on National Offer Day has ranged from 106.02 (2026) to 111.38 (2023).
  • Children turned away on test day for not bringing the correct photo ID get no further test.
