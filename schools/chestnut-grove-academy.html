Chestnut Grove Academy

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Chestnut Grove is a comprehensive that takes up to 60 of its 210 places by aptitude: 30 for Art and Design and 30 for Modern Foreign Languages. Each is decided by the school's own test on 19 September 2026, needs at least 40%, and goes to the top 30 scorers. The other 150 places are not selective. Every applicant takes the Wandsworth Year 6 Test, which sorts them into five ability bands with equal places in each. Within each band, places go to looked-after children, then medical/social need, then siblings, then children of staff, then by distance. Children who don't apply for a specialist place, or don't get one, are considered for these general places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. S1. Art and Design aptitude (up to 30 places) The 30 highest-scoring applicants with at least 40% are offered places.
  2. S2. Modern Foreign Languages aptitude (up to 30 places) The 30 highest-scoring applicants with at least 40% are offered places. No previous knowledge of a language is needed.
  3. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children (within each band) Looked-after children and those who stopped being looked after because of adoption or a residence or special guardianship order.
  4. 2. Exceptional medical or social need (within each band) A sub-committee (Chair of the Local Academy Committee, Headteacher and Deputy) relies entirely on the professional evidence. Without it by the closing date, the application isn't considered under this criterion.
  5. 3. Siblings on roll (within each band) If a band has more siblings than places, siblings are admitted in distance order.
  6. 4. Children of staff (within each band) Children of long-serving staff.
  7. 5. Everyone else, nearest first (within each band) Straight-line distance from home to the centre of the Academy site (Wandsworth GIS). In 2026 the furthest offers ranged from 472 m (Band E) to 1383 m (Band A).

Good to know

  • There is no selection by general ability: the Wandsworth Year 6 Test only sorts applicants into bands so each band gets an equal share of places.
