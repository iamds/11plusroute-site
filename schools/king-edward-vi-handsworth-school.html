King Edward VI Handsworth School

Birmingham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One shared 11+ test (GL, 12 September 2026) for the Birmingham grammar schools. Your daughter must reach the qualifying score (205 for 2027). After looked-after children, Pupil Premium girls in the catchment are all offered places, with out-of-catchment Pupil Premium girls topping PP places up to 25%. Next come catchment girls with the priority score (224), by sibling then distance. Everyone else is ranked by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the qualifying score. Ranked by test score, then distance.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium, living in the catchment area Pupil Premium girls who reach the qualifying score and live in the catchment area. No cap: every such applicant has been offered a place in each year from 2023 to 2026. Ranked by distance if needed.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium, living outside the catchment area (top-up to 25%) Pupil Premium girls outside the catchment who reach the qualifying score, in score order, until Pupil Premium offers (categories 2 + 3) reach 25% of places. Equal scores: sibling first, then distance.
  4. 4. Priority score (224+) and living in the catchment area Girls in the catchment who reach the higher priority score (224 for 2027). Within this group the score doesn't matter: older siblings first, then nearest first.
  5. 5. Everyone else reaching the qualifying score, in score order All other girls who reach the qualifying score (in or out of catchment), in test-score order. Equal scores: sibling first, then distance.

Good to know

  • The catchment covers all of north and east Birmingham, including Sutton Coldfield, plus parts of Sandwell and Solihull.
  • Living in the catchment only helps if your daughter reaches the priority score (224) or is on Pupil Premium. Catchment applicants scoring 205-223 are ranked by score with everyone else.
  • Scores are on the Birmingham test's combined scale, so don't compare them with pass marks at other schools.
  • Pupil Premium priority only counts if the state primary school receives Pupil Premium funding for the child on the test date. Home-educated and independent-school children don't qualify.
  • Families living beyond a reasonable commuting distance may be asked for evidence, such as a house purchase, that they plan to move before the test.
  • Appeals rarely succeed: across the King Edward VI grammar schools, none of 60 appeals was upheld for 2025 entry.
