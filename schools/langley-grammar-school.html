Langley Grammar School

Langley

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 111 or more in the Slough Consortium 11+ to be considered. Looked-after children come first. Then up to 120 places go to Priority Area 1 (inner Langley postcodes SL3 0/7/8/9), Pupil Premium first and then by score. After that: Pupil Premium children in Priority Area 2 (wider Slough and nearby postcodes), staff children, other Priority Area 2 children by score, Priority Area 3, and finally everyone else by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 6. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children who score 111 or more take priority over all other applicants.
  2. 7a. Lives in Priority Area 1 (up to 120 places) Children living in Priority Area 1 (SL3 0, SL3 7, SL3 8, SL3 9), up to 120 places. If there are more eligible applicants than places, Pupil Premium children go first, then the rest in score order.
  3. 7b. Pupil Premium, living in Priority Area 2 Pupil Premium children living in Priority Area 2. Ties for the last place go to the nearest.
  4. 7c. Children of staff Children of permanent staff employed for 2+ years before the closing date, or recruited to fill a skill-shortage post.
  5. 7d. Lives in Priority Area 2 Other children living in Priority Area 2, in score order.
  6. 7e. Lives in Priority Area 3 Children living in Priority Area 3, in score order. If oversubscribed, Pupil Premium children go first (new for 2027 entry).
  7. 7f. Lives outside the Priority Admission Areas Everyone else, in score order. Applicants outside the priority areas have no realistic chance of a place.

Good to know

  • Applicants living outside the priority areas have no realistic chance of a place.
  • Priority Area 1 can now take up to 120 places, up from 100.
  • There is no sibling priority and no supplementary form.
  • The Consortium moves to Quest Assessments for the 2027 test (2028 entry); the 111 eligibility score stays.
  • A free examination familiarisation booklet is published on the Consortium schools' websites.
  • Children eligible for the Pupil Premium can get free Atom Learning resources, which include some practice 11+ style tests.
