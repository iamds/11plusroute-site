Wycombe High School

High Wycombe

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter needs 121+ in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Up to 12 places go to Pupil Premium or looked-after girls in the catchment who score 113-120. The score doesn't rank qualified girls. Places go by: looked after, Pupil Premium in catchment, sisters in catchment (including sisters of John Hampden boys), staff, medical/social, then Catchment Area A, Area B, and distance. In 2026 every catchment-A girl who qualified got in, and Area B girls got in up to 8.05 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls (incl. adopted from state care abroad) Qualified girls who are looked after, or were previously looked after (including girls adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Pupil Premium girls who qualify and live in the catchment Girls who qualify (121+ or via Selection Review), get the Pupil Premium and live in Catchment Area A or B.
  3. 3. Up to 12 disadvantaged catchment girls scoring 113-120 Up to 12 places for girls in the catchment who get the Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium, or are looked after/previously looked after, and score 113-120. They don't count as qualified for any other criterion, so a girl not placed here isn't considered further (unless she qualifies through Selection Review).
  4. 4. Sisters of girls at the school, living in the catchment Qualified girls with a sister at Wycombe High School who live in Catchment Area A or B. Sisters only count if they live in the catchment.
  5. 5. Sisters of boys at John Hampden Grammar School, living in the catchment Qualified girls whose brother attends John Hampden Grammar School (the neighbouring boys' grammar) and who live in the Wycombe High School catchment. These are called 'linked siblings'.
  6. 6. Daughters of staff Qualified daughters of staff employed at the school for two years or more, or recruited to a skill-shortage post. No catchment requirement.
  7. 7. Exceptional medical or social need Qualified girls whose exceptional medical or social needs can only be met at Wycombe High. A panel of independent education professionals decides who counts.
  8. 8. Living in Catchment Area A Qualified girls living in Catchment Area A on or before 1 September 2026, nearest first.
  9. 9. Living in Catchment Area B Qualified girls living in Catchment Area B on or before 1 September 2026, nearest first.
  10. 10. Everyone else by distance All other qualified girls, nearest to the school's main entrance gate first (straight line).

Good to know

  • One qualifying score (121+) counts for every Buckinghamshire grammar school.
  • Above 121 the score makes no difference: places go by catchment, siblings and distance.
  • In 2026 every qualified girl in Catchment Area A got a place, and Area B girls got in up to 8.05 miles.
  • Qualification lasts until the end of Year 9 for waiting-list purposes.
  • Use Buckinghamshire Council's Priority Area Catchment Checker to see whether your address is in Area A or B.
