St Joseph's College

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Not a grammar school. This is an all-ability Catholic boys' school in Upper Norwood (Croydon). The only test is an optional maths aptitude test for 10% of places. After EHCPs and looked-after children, those places go to the top scorers. Then come baptised Catholics, siblings, other Christians, other faiths and everyone else, each by distance. Boys who don't sit the test still apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys, including those adopted from state care abroad. Needs a letter from the local authority's children's services, plus the adoption / special guardianship order for previously looked-after boys.
  2. 2. Top 10% in the maths aptitude test 10% of places go to the boys with the highest scores in the maths aptitude test (the school is a Mathematics & Computing specialist college).
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic boys Baptised Catholic boys, by distance. No Mass-attendance certificate is required. The SIF asks for a priest's reference, but it isn't a condition.
  4. 4. Siblings Boys who will have a brother or sister at the College when they start.
  5. 5. Other Christian denominations Members of other Christian denominations.
  6. 6. Members of other faiths Children who are members of other faiths, churches and denominations.
  7. 7. Other faiths, not practising Children of other faiths who are not practising.
  8. 8. All other children Everyone else, by distance.

Good to know

  • This is an all-ability school, not a grammar school: the maths test only decides 10% of places.
  • Boys who don't return the Supplementary Information Form are considered only as 'other children'.
  • In 2026 the furthest distance offered was 3.109 miles.
  • The sixth form is mixed; Year 7 is boys only.
