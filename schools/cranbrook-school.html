Grammar School Admissions · Year 7 entry, school by school

Cranbrook School

Cranbrook

Year 7 is day places only (90). Your child must pass the Kent Test. After EHCPs, priority goes to looked-after children, then (all within the Priority Area: named parishes around Cranbrook, or within 8.5 km) Pupil Premium children, social need and siblings, then children of staff, then other Priority Area children, then everyone else, with places going by Kent Test score. Recent lowest offer scores were 337-349. Boarding starts in Year 9, through the school's own entrance exam.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in local authority care, and children previously looked after (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight from care), including those who left state care outside England by being adopted. Must be assessed suitable for grammar school. No address condition.
  2. b. Pupil Premium, living in the Priority Area Children in the Priority Area who are eligible for Pupil Premium (registered for free school meals at any time in the last 6 years). You must return the school's Supplementary Information Form to the Registrar by 31 October 2026 and name Cranbrook on the SCAF. There is no cap on the number of places.
  3. c. Social need, living in the Priority Area Children in the Priority Area with a demonstrable and significant need to attend Cranbrook, because of their own physical or mental impairment, or their parent's or guardian's health or social needs. Apply to the Registrar by 31 October 2026 with professional evidence linking the need to this school.
  4. d. Brother or sister at the school, living in the Priority Area Children in the Priority Area with a brother or sister already at the school when they join. Siblings must live in the same house, and include adopted, step and foster brothers and sisters. Siblings outside the Priority Area get no priority.
  5. e. Children of staff Children of teaching or support staff on at least half-time contracts who have worked at the school for two years or more when you apply, or who were recruited to a post with a demonstrable skill shortage. No address condition.
  6. f. Lives in the Priority Area Other grammar-assessed children whose permanent home is in the Priority Area (named parishes around Cranbrook, or within 8.5 km of the school) at the closing date for applications.
  7. g. Lives outside the Priority Area Grammar-assessed children living outside the Priority Area (anywhere in the UK; day applications aren't accepted from overseas).

Good to know

  • Cranbrook is the only Kent school with a normal entry point at Year 9 as well as Year 7.
  • Year 7 is 90 day places. Boarding starts in Year 9, with 52 boarding places for 2027 entry.
  • If your child was born outside 1 September 2015 - 31 August 2016 and you want Year 7 entry, ask the Registrar for a Supplementary Information Form as early as possible.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
