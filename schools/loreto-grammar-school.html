Loreto Grammar School

Altrincham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit Loreto's own entrance exam (GL English and verbal reasoning plus a school maths paper) on 18 September 2026 and must reach the school's expected standard. If more girls pass than there are places, baptised Catholic girls come first (looked-after, then sisters, then girls at an associated Catholic primary who live in a named parish area, then other Catholics), then other looked-after girls, then girls baptised in other denominations, then everyone else. Within each category Pupil Premium girls come first, then the highest marks.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Baptised Catholic looked after / previously looked after girls Baptised Roman Catholic girls who are looked after, previously looked after, or internationally looked after.
  2. 2. Baptised Catholic girls with a sister at the school Baptised Roman Catholic girls whose sister is at the school when she starts.
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic girls at an associated primary who live in a nominated Local Pastoral Area Baptised Roman Catholic girls who attend one of the 15 associated Catholic primary schools and live in one of the nominated Local Pastoral Areas (parishes in the St Ambrose Deanery, Diocese of Shrewsbury, and Our Lady's Deanery, Diocese of Salford). She must meet both conditions.
  4. 4. Other baptised Catholic girls All other baptised Roman Catholic girls who passed the exam. There's no practice or Mass-attendance requirement, just baptism.
  5. 5. Other looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after, previously looked-after and internationally previously looked-after girls who aren't baptised Catholics.
  6. 6. Girls baptised in other Christian denominations Girls baptised in any other denomination.
  7. 7. Other girls All other girls who passed the exam: Pupil Premium girls first, then by exam marks.

Good to know

  • Catholic priority needs only baptism before the exam registration date, not a certificate of Catholic practice or Mass attendance.
  • Within every category, Pupil Premium girls come before exam marks.
  • Category 3 needs both: attending an associated Catholic primary and living in a nominated parish area.
  • Offers have been slightly above the 150 places (159-165 a year).
  • Appeals go to the Clerk to the Governors by 31 March 2027, with 7 paper copies of all evidence.
