The Crossley Heath School

Halifax

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child sits the joint Halifax Grammar Schools test (FSCE: two English and maths papers plus creative writing), shared with North Halifax Grammar School. They must reach the qualifying standard in English, maths and creative writing. Looked-after children and Pupil/Service Premium children who qualify come first. All other places go in order of merit on the English and maths papers. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after / special guardianship Looked-after children, and children previously looked after who became subject to an adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Pupil Premium or Service Premium Qualifying children who attract the Pupil Premium (FSM in the last 6 years, or in or previously in care) or the Service Premium. Evidence received after the end of September counts only after the main allocation round.
  3. d. All other qualifying children in order of merit The remaining places go in order of merit for English and maths until the school reaches 180.

Good to know

  • About 1,300 children sit the test each year for 360 places across the two Halifax grammar schools (180 each).
  • Preference order matters: if you list a grammar school below a school whose criteria your child meets, you lose the grammar place whatever their order of merit.
  • The test content changes every year: the free FSCE Familiarisation Guide shows sample questions, and no commercial practice papers are endorsed.
