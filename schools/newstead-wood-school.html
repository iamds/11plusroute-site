Newstead Wood School

Orpington

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must reach the required standard in the school's own verbal and non-verbal reasoning test: normally 210, lowered only if needed to fill the places. After looked-after girls and Pupil Premium/Service Premium girls living within 9 miles, places go strictly in score order to girls within 9 miles (plus daughters of long-serving staff). In practice girls outside 9 miles aren't offered places. The lowest score offered on National Offer Day 2026 was 225.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 3.2.1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reach the required standard. Wherever they live.
  2. 3.2.2. Pupil Premium / Service Premium girls within 9 miles Girls living within 9 miles who receive Pupil Premium or Service Premium when they apply to sit the test, and who reach the required standard. No cap on numbers.
  3. 3.3.1. Within 9 miles, or daughters of staff (2+ years) living further away, in rank order Most places: girls whose permanent home is within a 9-mile radius, plus daughters of long-serving staff who live outside it, offered strictly in order of total test score.
  4. 3.3.2. Outside 9 miles, in rank order Girls living outside 9 miles, in score order. No places have been offered under this criterion because the school is heavily oversubscribed.

Good to know

  • Girls from anywhere can sit the test, but no girl living outside 9 miles has been offered a place.
  • The cut-off score on National Offer Day 2026 was 225, well above the required standard of 210.
  • The school does not sell past or practice papers. It links to CEM and GL familiarisation materials on its test page.
  • Pupil Premium, Service Premium and looked-after girls are offered about 10 hours of free online familiarisation material.
  • Check your test invitation for the exact test date.
