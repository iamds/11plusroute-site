Upton Court Grammar School

Slough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 111 or more in the Slough Consortium 11+. After children with an EHCP, looked-after children come first, then up to 20 children on free school meals now or in the last six years (Pupil Premium), then staff children and children at Foxborough Primary or Trevelyan Middle School. Places up to 120 in total go by test score, and the remaining 45 or so to those living nearest the school.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight after being looked after), including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. ii. Pupil Premium (up to 20 places) Up to 20 places for children eligible for free school meals now or in the last six years. If more apply, by score, then distance.
  3. iii. Children of staff Children of permanent staff working at Upton Court (employed by the trust for 2+ years at half-time or more, or in a skill-shortage post).
  4. iv. Attends Foxborough Primary School or Trevelyan Middle School Children attending Foxborough Primary School or Trevelyan Middle School (the designated feeder schools) when they apply.
  5. v. In score order (up to 120 places, less those above) Up to 120 pupils in score order. The number goes down by the number admitted with an EHCP and under criteria i-iv. Equal scores: nearest first.
  6. vi. Remaining places by distance The remaining places up to the PAN of 165 (at least 45) go to those living nearest the school, measured in a straight line using Slough's GIS.

Good to know

  • Results arrive on 15 October 2026, before the 31 October application deadline.
  • For 2026 entry, 992 of 1,274 applicants scored 111 or more. Score places went down to 121, and distance places to children within 1.263 miles.
  • There is no sibling priority.
  • No supplementary form is needed, but to claim the free school meals priority email your primary school's letter to the school by 31 October 2026.
  • You must have lived at your address since 1 May 2026.
  • To ask for Year 7 entry outside the normal age group, write to the school before the CAF deadline; there is no appeal against the decision.
