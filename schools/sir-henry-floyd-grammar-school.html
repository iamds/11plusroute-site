Sir Henry Floyd Grammar School

Aylesbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 121 or more in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Test score gives no further priority. Among qualified children, priority goes to looked-after children, Pupil Premium children in catchment, siblings, staff children, catchment, then distance from the nearest school entrance. Up to 6 extra places above the PAN go to FSM/Pupil Premium children scoring 110-120, nearest first.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium children living in catchment Qualified children eligible for Pupil Premium (autumn 2026 census) who have lived in the catchment area since 1 September 2026.
  3. 3. Siblings at the school Qualified children with a sibling at the school now who will still be there when they start.
  4. 4. Children of staff Qualified children of staff who have worked at the school for at least two years (or were recruited to a shortage post).
  5. 5. Lives in catchment Qualified children living in the catchment area at and continuously from 1 September 2026.
  6. 6. Everyone else, nearest first Remaining qualified children, by straight-line distance from home to the nearest of the school's three main entrances.

Good to know

  • Up to six extra places above the PAN go to children on Free School Meals or Pupil Premium scoring 110-120. They have filled every year since 2024, with the last offer between 1.3 and 1.5 miles.
  • The last distance offered under the main distance rule was 13.82 miles in 2026, 14.163 in 2025 and 13.216 in 2024.
  • A higher test score doesn't help: places depend on the admission rules and distance.
  • If the last place goes to one twin or triplet, all are admitted, even above the PAN.
