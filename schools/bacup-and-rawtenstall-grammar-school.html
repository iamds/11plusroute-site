Bacup and Rawtenstall Grammar School

Rossendale

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child sits the school's entrance exam in September of Year 6 and must reach the qualifying score. If more qualifying children apply than the 180 places, looked-after children come first. Next come children at named Rossendale primary schools: Area 1 schools (Bacup, Rawtenstall and nearby) before Area 2 schools (Haslingden, Whitworth and nearby), with siblings first in each group and then by exam mark. Then come staff children, then other siblings, then everyone else by exam mark.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who met the required standard.
  2. 2. Attends an Area 1 primary school (siblings first) Children who attend an Area 1 primary school for at least the whole of Year 6. Those with a brother or sister in Years 7-11 at BRGS come first, then the rest in order of overall exam mark.
  3. 3. Attends an Area 2 primary school (siblings first) Children who attend an Area 2 primary school for at least the whole of Year 6. Siblings of current Year 7-11 pupils come first, then the rest by overall exam mark.
  4. 4. Children of BRGS staff Children of staff employed at the school for two years or more (or recruited to fill a skill shortage).
  5. 5. Sibling at BRGS (Years 7-11) Other children with a brother or sister in Years 7-11 at the school (sixth formers don't count).
  6. 6. All other children, by exam mark Everyone else who met the required standard, in order of overall exam mark.

Important dates

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Saturday 31 October 2026: Council application deadline (results of the exam are sent before this)
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day

Good to know

  • In 2026/27, 96 of the 180 places went to children from outside the named Area 1 and Area 2 primary schools.
  • New for 2027/28: siblings now come first within the Area 1 and Area 2 groups, and there is a new sibling priority before 'all other children'.
  • Your child must attend the named primary school for at least the whole of Year 6 for it to count.
  • Free familiarisation materials can be downloaded from the GL Assessment website.
