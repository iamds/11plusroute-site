A two-round entrance test. The top 300 in Round One sit Round Two. Looked-after and Pupil Premium girls in the top 300 of Round One get top priority. Next come girls living within 3 miles who sat Round Two, then everyone else in order of combined Round One + Round Two score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children LAC/PLAC girls in Round One's top 300 are guaranteed a place if they apply. Ties are broken by distance.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Pupil Premium girls in Round One's top 300 are guaranteed a place if they apply, provided the evidence arrives by 1 July. Ties are broken by distance.
  3. 3. Lives within 3 miles Girls within 3 miles who sat Round Two come next. If there are more of them than places, places go in rank-score order.
  4. 4. All other candidates by rank Everyone else, in order of combined Round One + Round Two rank. Anyone who didn't sit the test is ranked after all who did.

Important dates

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Wednesday 1 July 2026: Entrance Test Entry Form deadline (5pm) and Pupil Premium evidence deadline
  • Monday 17 August 2026: Round One test details sent out (contact the school if nothing by 20 Aug)

  • Thursday 1 October 2026: Round Two test (one day in early October)
  • Tuesday 20 October 2026: Late October: letters to Round Two candidates (guaranteed place if LAC/PP, otherwise 'may be eligible')
  • Saturday 31 October 2026: CAF deadline (national)
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day

Good to know

  • The 3-mile priority only needs a Round Two sitting, not a high final rank, so local girls in the top 300 of Round One have a big advantage.
