The Royal Grammar School, High Wycombe

High Wycombe

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son needs 121+ in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Up to six places go to Pupil Premium or looked-after catchment boys scoring 115-120. The score doesn't rank qualified boys. Day places go by: looked after, Pupil Premium in catchment, siblings, catchment (Priority Area A then B), staff, then distance. In 2026 every qualified catchment boy got a day place, plus others to 13.5 miles. There are also 10 weekly-boarding places with their own criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified boys who are looked after or previously looked after (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Up to 6 places: Pupil Premium or looked-after catchment boys scoring 115-120 Up to six places for catchment boys (either Priority Area) who score 115-120 and have been eligible for the Pupil Premium continuously since 1 September 2026, or are looked after/previously looked after. Looked-after boys come first, then distance.
  3. 3. Qualified Pupil Premium boys living in the catchment Qualified boys (121+ or via Selection Review) who are eligible for the Pupil Premium at the deadline and live in either Priority Area.
  4. 4. Siblings of day students Qualified boys with a brother or sister at RGS as a day student (the sixth form is mixed). No catchment requirement.
  5. 5. Living in the catchment (Priority Area A, then B) Qualified boys living in the catchment from 1 September 2026 until they join. If this criterion is oversubscribed, Priority Area A comes before Priority Area B, each nearest first.
  6. 6. Children of staff Qualified sons of staff. This comes after the catchment, so in practice it only helps families outside the catchment.
  7. 7. Everyone else by distance All other qualified boys, nearest first (straight line to the nearest pupil entrance).

Good to know

  • Once your son scores 121 or more, a higher score makes no difference for day places: they go by catchment, siblings and distance.
  • In 2026 every qualified boy living in the catchment got a day place, plus others living up to 13.52 miles away.
  • Siblings don't need a catchment address and come before the catchment criterion.
  • Year 7 boarding is weekly only; full boarding starts in Year 9.
  • There is an unmarked practice test on 8 September 2026. Each real paper takes about 45 minutes (60 with the introduction and examples).
  • A familiarisation booklet with example questions and a guide to the answer sheets is posted home in early July; GL Assessment also offers free materials online.
