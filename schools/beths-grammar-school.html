Beths Grammar School

Bexley

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must be deemed selective in the Bexley Selection Test. Places go first to looked-after boys, then to the Bexley-wide top 180 scorers, brothers and sisters, and staff children, then up to 12 to Pupil Premium/FSM boys (nearest first). All other places go by straight-line distance; in 2026 the last offer was at 6.665 miles. After that, boys who passed the Kent (Dartford) test and are within 5 marks of the Bexley pass mark can be offered places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who are deemed selective.
  2. 2. Top 180 scorers in the Bexley Selection Test The 180 highest scorers across everyone who sat the Bexley Selection Test (for any of the four schools) are 'guaranteed' a place at their preferred Bexley grammar school. Bexley sets the cut-off each year, and it varies with that year's scores.
  3. 3. Siblings Boys with a brother or sister (including half, adopted and step siblings at the same address) at Beths, which has a mixed sixth form. Return the Supplementary Form by 30 October 2026.
  4. 4. Children of staff Sons of staff on a permanent contract when you apply.
  5. 5. Pupil Premium / Free School Meals (up to 12 places) Up to 12 places for deemed-selective boys eligible for Pupil Premium and/or Free School Meals, nearest first. Unlike at the other Bexley grammars, this comes after siblings and staff children.
  6. 6. Distance All other deemed-selective boys, nearest first, by straight-line distance to the school's east entrance in Hartford Road.
  7. 8. Kent (Dartford) 11+ selective, within 5 marks of the Bexley pass Only after all deemed-selective boys have been considered. Boys who passed the Kent test for Dartford grammar schools and scored within 5 marks of the Bexley pass mark. Order: looked after, siblings, staff children, Pupil Premium/FSM (if those places aren't used up), then distance. These offers come after the waiting list of selective boys and Bexley scores down to 212, and before the Bexley reserve list.
  8. Reserve list. Bexley reserve list (below the selective score) Once the waiting list of selective boys and criterion 8 are exhausted, places can go to boys on Bexley's reserve list. Those on the highest mark below the pass mark are offered first, then the next mark, and so on.

Good to know

  • About 5,900 children sit the Bexley test each year and about 30-35% are deemed selective (5,866 sat in 2025 and 2,070 were selective).
  • If grammar places remain after the first allocation, they go to boys one mark below the selective score, then two marks below, and so on.
  • Offers can go down to a Bexley score of 212, a mark below which places have historically not been offered.
  • A brother or sister counts if they will be at the school when your son enrols.
  • Both papers are sat in one session lasting two and a half to three hours including instructions. All working out goes in the test booklet, as no rough paper is given.
