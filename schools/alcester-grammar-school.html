Alcester Grammar School

Alcester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Children sit the Warwickshire 11+ (shared West Midlands test). Places go first to looked-after children and Pupil Premium (FSM) children in the priority area who reach the qualifying score or come within 20 marks of it, then to other children living in the priority area (a 16.9-mile circle from Stratford-upon-Avon) who reach the qualifying score, in score order. In recent years every place has gone to children inside the priority area, and in 2026 the in-area cut-off was a little above the qualifying score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the AQS or come within 20 marks of it (or the waiting-list score, if that is lower than 20 below). No residence condition. Below-AQS places in categories 1 and 2 together are capped at 20.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (FSM) children in the priority area Children living within the priority circle who attract the Pupil Premium through free school meals and reach the AQS or come within 20 marks of it. Below-AQS places in categories 1 and 2 together are capped at 20.
  3. 3. Lives in the priority area Children living within the 16.936-mile priority circle who reach the AQS, highest scores first. In recent years this is where places run out.
  4. 4. Outside the priority area, at or above the AQS Children living outside the priority area who reach the AQS. They normally get first-round offers only if there are not enough qualifying children inside the area.
  5. 5. Below the AQS but at or above the waiting-list score Children between the waiting-list score and the AQS. In practice this is a waiting-list category: children below the AQS are placed on the waiting list, and no first-round offers were made here in 2023-2026.

Good to know

  • The priority area is a 16.9-mile circle centred on the Fountain in Rother Street, Stratford-upon-Avon, not on Alcester.
  • For 2027 entry, looked-after and Pupil Premium children can get a place with a score up to 20 marks below the AQS (15 marks for 2026 entry), with at most 20 such places in total.
  • In 2024-2026 the in-area cut-off (221, 224, 227) was 1-4 marks above the AQS, so reaching the AQS doesn't guarantee a place.
  • If you live outside Warwickshire, apply through your own council. The priority circle extends into Worcestershire.
  • There is no special priority for twins.
  • A free familiarisation booklet with example questions is on the Warwickshire Admissions website, or on paper on request.
