Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Current family association (brother or sister at the school) A brother or sister attending the school when your son starts: natural, adopted, step or foster siblings, or children living as siblings in the same house.
  3. c. Health and special access reasons For boys whose (or whose parent's) physical or mental health or social needs give a demonstrable and significant need to attend this school, backed by professional evidence.
  4. d. Student Premium (free school meals) Registered for free school meals at any point in the last six years (Universal Infant Free School Meals alone don't count). Return the Supplementary Information Form to the school by 31 October and name the school on the SCAF.
  5. e. Nearness of home to school All other eligible boys, nearest first (straight line, measured by KCC).

Good to know

  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • Offers were below the 160 places in 2024 and 2025, but all 160 places were filled in 2026, so distance may start to matter.
  • A higher Kent Test score gives no extra priority: once a boy is assessed suitable, places go by the oversubscription criteria.
  • The school publishes no last-offer distances.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
