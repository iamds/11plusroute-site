The Blue Coat School

Liverpool

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child applies to the school to sit its own two-phase CEM entrance test (apply in Year 5 by 24 April). About 600 go on from Phase 1 to Phase 2. Places then go in score order to children who reach the eligible score, with up to 27 places for the highest-scoring children on Free School Meals. Looked-after children who reach the eligible score get priority. There are no faith, sibling or distance criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including children who appear to have been in state care outside England, who reach an eligible score.
  2. 2. Free School Meals (up to 27 places) Up to 27 places go to the highest-scoring children who are eligible for Free School Meals and reach the eligible score.
  3. 3. All other applicants in score order The remaining places go to the other highest-scoring applicants who reach the eligible score.
