Colchester County High School for Girls

Colchester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (English and maths). After EHCP girls scoring 320+, places go first to looked-after girls scoring 320+, then up to 20 Pupil Premium girls scoring 320+ (any address), then up to 10 Service Premium girls in the priority area scoring 320+. Then girls living within 25 miles of the school are taken in score order until 154 places are used, and the last 38 places go to the highest scorers wherever they live.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked-after / previously looked-after girls scoring 320+ Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who score 320 or more, wherever they live.
  2. 2. Up to 20 Pupil Premium girls scoring 320+ (any address) Up to 20 places go to the highest-scoring girls eligible for the Pupil Premium through free school meals (now or in the last 6 years) who score 320 or more, wherever they live.
  3. 3. Up to 10 Service Pupil Premium girls in the priority area scoring 320+ Up to 10 places go to the highest-scoring girls eligible for the Service Pupil Premium who live within the 25-mile priority area and score 320 or more.
  4. 4. Girls living within 25 miles, by score, until 154 places are filled Girls whose permanent home is within 25 miles of the school, highest score first, until 154 places have gone in total (EHCP plus criteria 1-4). She must live there continuously from 31 October 2026 until starting in September 2027.
  5. 5. Remaining 38 places: highest scorers, any address The last 38 places go to the highest scorers regardless of where they live, including priority-area girls who missed out under criterion 4.

Good to know

  • The Pupil Premium priority has no address condition, but the Service Pupil Premium priority needs a priority-area address.
  • The 154 places include girls admitted under criteria 1-3 and EHCPs, so the number of places for other girls within 25 miles depends on how many are used above it.
  • The 25-mile priority area was introduced for 2025 entry.
  • Last-offer scores leave out places awarded under special-considerations criteria and on appeal.
