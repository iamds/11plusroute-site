Queen Elizabeth's Grammar School, Horncastle

Horncastle

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must reach 220 in the Lincolnshire 11+. If more qualifiers apply than there are places, looked-after children come first, then Pupil Premium or Service Premium children, then children living in the school's designated free transport area in score order, then everyone else in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Service Premium Qualified children eligible for the pupil premium or service premium.
  3. 3. Lives in the designated free transport area Qualified children whose permanent address is in the school's designated area for free transport, in order of total test score.
  4. 4. All other qualified children by score Qualified children from all other areas, in order of total test score.

Good to know

  • A total standardised score of 220 or more makes your child eligible, but doesn't guarantee a place.
  • Lincolnshire still treats applications made by 14 December 2026 as on time.
  • Pupil premium children can get a free Atom Learning 11+ familiarisation account through the school.
  • The school is consulting on its 2028 admissions policy, which may change things for later years.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
