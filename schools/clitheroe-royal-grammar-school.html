Clitheroe Royal Grammar School

Clitheroe

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child registers with the school and sits its entrance exam (two FSCE papers, both English and maths). They must reach the 'required standard', which is set each year. If more children reach it than the 180 places, looked-after children come first. Next come children in the immediate catchment (named Ribble Valley parishes), then the wider catchment (most of Lancashire plus Blackburn with Darwen), then everyone else. In each area, Pupil Premium children come first, and places go in test-score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reached the required standard.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium, living in the immediate catchment area Pupil Premium children whose permanent home is in the immediate catchment area, in score order.
  3. 3. Lives in the immediate catchment area Other children whose permanent home is in the immediate catchment area, in score order.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium, living in the wider catchment area Pupil Premium children whose permanent home is in the wider catchment area, in score order.
  5. 5. Lives in the wider catchment area Other children whose permanent home is in the wider catchment area, in score order.
  6. 6. Pupil Premium, living outside the catchment area Pupil Premium children living outside both catchment areas, in score order.
  7. 7. Lives outside the catchment area Everyone else who reached the required standard, in score order. Families whose main home is outside the catchment areas, or who are only renting short-term or staying with friends or relatives in the area, count as outside the catchment.

Good to know

  • Most of Lancashire, plus Blackburn with Darwen, counts as the wider catchment, so 'outside the catchment' mainly means families from other counties.
  • If your child qualifies for a school you ranked higher on the council form, that school will be offered instead of CRGS.
  • Lancashire's four grammar schools normally test on the same day, but not all use the same test. LGGS uses the same FSCE papers.
  • Question types and style change every year: there are no past papers, and the free FSCE Familiarisation Guides are the only official preparation.
