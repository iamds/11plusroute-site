Townley Grammar School

Bexleyheath

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must be deemed selective in the Bexley Selection Test. Places go first to looked-after girls, then 20 to Pupil Premium girls in score order, which can reach up to 2% below the selective score. Then come the Bexley-wide top 180 scorers, sisters and staff children. All other places go by straight-line distance; in 2026 the last offer was at 7.076 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who are deemed selective.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium girls who are deemed selective (20 places) 20 places for Pupil Premium girls who reach the selective score, highest score first (distance breaks ties). Girls not placed here can still qualify under later criteria.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium girls up to 2% below the selective score (unfilled PP places) If fewer than 20 Pupil Premium places are filled under criterion 2, the rest go to Pupil Premium girls scoring up to 2% below the selective score, highest score first, then by distance.
  4. 4. Top 180 scorers in the Bexley Selection Test The 180 highest scorers across everyone who sat the Bexley Selection Test (for any of the four schools) are 'guaranteed' a place at their preferred Bexley grammar school. Bexley sets the cut-off each year, and it varies with that year's scores.
  5. 5. Siblings Girls with a brother or sister (including adopted, step and half siblings at the same address) at Townley when you apply.
  6. 6. Children of staff Daughters of staff with at least two years' service, or staff recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  7. 7. Distance All other deemed-selective girls, nearest first, by straight-line distance to the student entrance, whatever their score.
  8. Further allocations. Reserve list (below the selective score) Only if places are left after every deemed-selective applicant has been offered one. Places then go in descending order of aggregate test score under Bexley's reserve-list process.

Good to know

  • Most places go on distance: the last offers were at 7.076 miles in 2026 and 7.17 miles in 2025.
  • The 180 highest scorers across all Bexley candidates are guaranteed a place at their preferred Bexley grammar school.
  • Townley ranks its 20 Pupil Premium places by test score, unlike the other Bexley grammar schools, which use distance.
  • About 5,900 children sit the Bexley test each year and about 30-35% are deemed selective.
  • If places remain, they go to girls one mark below the selective score, then two marks below, and so on.
  • Both papers are sat in one session lasting two and a half to three hours including instructions. All working out goes in the test booklet, as no rough paper is given.
