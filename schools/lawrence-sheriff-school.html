Lawrence Sheriff School

Rugby

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Warwickshire 11+ (shared West Midlands test). After looked-after boys, up to 30 Pupil Premium and 5 Service Premium boys living locally get places if they reach the qualifying score, come within 20 marks of it, or reach the waiting-list score, whichever bar is lowest. Then come children of staff, then boys in the Eastern Area of Warwickshire (Rugby and surrounding parishes) at the qualifying score, then boys elsewhere in the 10-mile Priority Circle, in score order. In 2026 nearly all places went to Eastern Area boys, and Priority Circle boys needed 241.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the AQS, come within 20 marks of it, or reach the waiting-list score, whichever is lowest. No residence condition.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium boys in the Eastern Area or Priority Circle (up to 30 places) Up to 30 places for boys eligible for the Pupil Premium when they registered for the test and living in the Eastern Area or Priority Circle, at the lowest of the three bars. The 30 places are shared with criterion 7 (Pupil Premium boys from outside), including waiting-list re-offers.
  3. 3. Service Premium boys in the Eastern Area or Priority Circle (up to 5 places) Up to 5 places for boys eligible for the Service Premium (children of Armed Services personnel) when they registered for the test and living in the Eastern Area or Priority Circle, at the lowest of the three bars. The 5 places are shared with criterion 8.
  4. 4. Children of staff Sons of permanent staff who have worked at the school for at least two years and who reach the AQS. No residence condition.
  5. 5. Eastern Area of Warwickshire Boys living in the Eastern Area of Warwickshire (Rugby and surrounding parishes) who reach the AQS, highest scores first. In recent years almost all of these boys have been offered places.
  6. 6. Priority Circle (10.004 miles from Rugby Water Tower) Other boys living within the Priority Circle who reach the AQS, highest scores first. In recent years this is where places run out, at scores well above the AQS.
  7. 7. Pupil Premium boys from outside the catchment areas If the PAN hasn't been reached and fewer than 30 places went under criterion 2, the balance goes to Pupil Premium boys living outside both catchment areas, at the lowest of the three bars.
  8. 8. Service Premium boys from outside the catchment areas If the PAN hasn't been reached and fewer than 5 places went under criterion 3, the balance goes to Service Premium boys living outside both catchment areas, at the lowest of the three bars.
  9. 9. Outside the catchment areas, at or above the AQS Boys living outside both catchment areas who reach the AQS, highest scores first.
  10. 10. Anywhere, at or above the waiting-list score Boys inside or outside the catchment areas who reach the waiting-list score but not a higher bar. In practice a waiting-list category: no first-round offers went here in 2023-2026.

Good to know

  • In 2025 and 2026 almost all places went to Eastern Area boys at the AQS, leaving only 13 and then 3 places for the rest of the Priority Circle.
  • A Priority Circle boy outside the Eastern Area needs a much higher score than a Rugby boy: 241 in 2026, against an AQS of 213.
  • Pupil and Service Premium status counts when you register for the test.
  • There is no sibling priority.
  • A free familiarisation booklet with example questions is on the Warwickshire Admissions website, or on paper on request.
  • Children eligible for the Pupil Premium can get free Atom Learning 11+ preparation through the school.
