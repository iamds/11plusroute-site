Ashlawn School

Rugby

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Ashlawn is a bilateral school: most places are non-selective, but up to 31 go to children who reach Ashlawn's qualifying score in the Warwickshire 11+ (15 for the Eastern Area of Warwickshire, 16 for the 10-mile Priority Circle, Pupil Premium first), and up to 25 go to the top scorers in Ashlawn's own modern languages aptitude test. Unfilled selective and aptitude places become ordinary places. Everyone else, including children who don't sit either test, is placed by siblings, Ashlawn's priority area and distance. In 2026 non-selective places reached children in the priority area up to 1.324 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England. No test score is needed under this criterion.
  2. 2a(i). Selective: Eastern Area, Pupil Premium, at the AQS First call on the 15 Eastern Area selective places: children living in the Eastern Area of Warwickshire who would be eligible for the Pupil Premium and reach the AQS.
  3. 2a(ii). Selective: Eastern Area, at the AQS The rest of the 15 Eastern Area selective places, for other Eastern Area children who reach the AQS.
  4. 2b. Selective: Priority Circle, at the AQS (Pupil Premium first) Up to 16 selective places for children living in the 10.004-mile Priority Circle around the Rugby Water Tower who reach the AQS. The circle includes the Eastern Area, so Eastern Area children who missed out under 2a are considered here. Pupil Premium children go first, then other applicants, and finally children the Committee of Reference has judged to have met the minimum academic standard and put on the waiting list.
  5. 2c. Selective: outside the priority areas, at the AQS Only if the selective places are not filled from inside the areas: children living outside the priority area who reach the AQS. Any selective places still unfilled become non-selective places.
  6. 3. Modern languages aptitude (up to 25 places) Up to 25 places (about 10% of the intake) for children who meet the required standard in Ashlawn's own Modern Language aptitude test, in score order. They study two languages, on a curriculum similar to the selective stream. Children who miss out are considered under the later criteria. Unfilled places go to the next criterion.
  7. 4a. Same-year siblings of a child given a selective or aptitude place Twins and other same-year siblings of a child who has just been given a selective or aptitude place.
  8. 4b. Siblings living in Ashlawn's priority area Children living in Ashlawn's priority area who will have a sibling at the school when they start.
  9. 4c. Siblings living outside the priority area Children living outside the priority area who will have a sibling at the school when they start.
  10. 4d. Living in Ashlawn's priority area Other children living in Ashlawn's priority area, nearest first.
  11. 5. Children of TLET staff with a sibling at the school Children of permanent trust staff working mainly at Ashlawn (two years' service), who will have a brother or sister at the school.
  12. 6. Children of TLET staff (over two years' service) Other children of permanent trust staff with more than two years' service.
  13. 7. Siblings living outside the priority areas Other children living outside the priority areas who will have a sibling at the school.
  14. 8. All other children Everyone else, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Ashlawn's 11+ qualifying score (208) has been lower than Lawrence Sheriff's and Rugby High's (212-213) in every recent year.
  • In 2026 the lowest selective offers were above the qualifying score, for example 210 in criterion 2a(ii).
  • Siblings get no extra priority for the selective places.
  • The MFL aptitude test can be sat only once, even if your child doesn't finish it.
  • A free familiarisation booklet with example questions is on the Warwickshire Admissions website, or on paper on request.
  • No sample or practice questions are given for the MFL aptitude test; all instructions are given on the day.
