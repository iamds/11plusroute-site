The King John School

Benfleet

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A large mixed comprehensive academy (Zenith Multi Academy Trust) in Thundersley. Looked-after children come first, then siblings living in the catchment area or an SS postcode, then everyone in the catchment area, then staff children. Any places left go first to up to 52 SS-postcode children with the highest scores in the school's own test (a non-verbal reasoning test, sat on 3 October 2026 after applying by 7 September), then by walking distance. Children who don't sit the test apply in the normal way.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care or previously in care (including adopted from state care abroad).
  2. 2. Siblings living in the priority admission area or an SS postcode Children living in the catchment area or anywhere in the SS postcode area with a sibling at the school when you apply. A twin who gets a test place doesn't count as a sibling for the other twin.
  3. 3. Living in the priority admission (catchment) area Children living in the school's catchment area.
  4. 4. Children of staff Children of school staff.
  5. 5. Places by test of general ability (SS postcodes only) If places remain after criteria 1-4, up to 15% of the 350 places go to the children with the highest scores in the school's own test. Only children living in an SS postcode can sit it.
  6. 6. Distance Any remaining places, nearest first by walking route to the main reception on Shipwrights Drive. Children who sat the test can also get in here.

Good to know

  • In 2024-2026 the last places went by distance, so every catchment-area child who applied got a place.
  • Test places have been available to SS-postcode children from outside the catchment.
  • The school publishes no cut-off scores for the test places.
