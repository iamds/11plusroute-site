Spalding High School

Spalding

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must reach the Lincolnshire 'county standard' of 220 in the consortium 11+ (verbal and non-verbal reasoning). If more than 150 qualified girls apply, places go to looked-after children, then girls with a brother or sister at the school, then everyone else by straight-line distance. Once she has qualified, a higher score gives no extra priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reached the qualifying standard. Includes children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Brother or sister at the school Qualified girls with a brother or sister at the school, nearest first if there are too many.
  3. 3. Everyone else by distance All other qualified girls, nearest home first (straight line).

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area and no Pupil Premium priority: after looked-after children and siblings, places go by distance.
  • Twins, triplets and siblings in the same year group can all be admitted if one gets a place, as long as each has reached 220.
  • A child can sit the 11+ only once for Year 7 entry.
  • In each of the last three years the school made 149-150 offers for 150 places, from about 280-300 applications.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
