The Judd School

Tonbridge

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys need a grammar assessment from the Kent Test. 157 places go to boys living in the Inner Area (Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells, Sevenoaks and nearby postcodes) and 23 to boys living elsewhere in England. Each list is filled in order of total Kent Test score, after looked-after boys. Up to 5 Inner Area places are kept for boys on Free School Meals or Pupil Premium who would otherwise miss out.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. Inner 1. Inner Area: looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England) living in the Inner Area who have a grammar assessment.
  2. Inner 2. Inner Area: Kent Test score Other Inner Area boys, in order of total Kent Test score. Equal scores are split by distance (nearest first), then by drawing lots.
  3. Inner PP 1. Inner Area Pupil Premium places: Free School Meals Inner Area boys on Free School Meals who haven't got a place in the main allocation, in Kent Test score order. You must send the school's SIF by 31 October 2026.
  4. Inner PP 2. Inner Area Pupil Premium places: other Pupil Premium Any of the 5 places not taken by FSM boys go to other Inner Area Pupil Premium boys, in Kent Test score order. Unused places go back to the Inner Area main allocation.
  5. Outer 1. Outer Area: looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys living outside the Inner Area (in England) who have a grammar assessment.
  6. Outer 2. Outer Area: Kent Test score Other boys living in England outside the Inner Area, in order of total Kent Test score, then distance, then drawing lots.

Good to know

  • In 2026 the lowest Inner Area offer went to a score of 389, and the lowest Outer Area offer to 403.
  • If your son scored above the lowest Inner Area offer, an appeal panel doesn't need convincing that he meets the school's academic standard.
  • About 20 offers a year have recently come from the waiting list, mostly at reallocation and mostly from the Inner Area list.
  • You can join the waiting list wherever you ranked Judd on your application.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
  • The school offers free 11+ preparation resources for Pupil Premium children.
