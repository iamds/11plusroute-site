Lancaster Girls' Grammar School

Lancaster

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must register for and sit the school's entrance test (FSCE, English and maths) and reach the pass mark, which is set each year. If more girls pass than there are 145 places, priority goes to looked-after girls, then Pupil Premium girls living in the Lancaster district, then all girls living in the Lancaster district, then staff children, then Pupil Premium girls from outside the district, then everyone else, in test-rank order within each group. Looked-after and Pupil Premium girls can score up to 5% below the pass mark.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reach the pass mark or score up to 5% below it.
  2. b. Pupil Premium, living in the City of Lancaster Pupil Premium girls whose home is in the City of Lancaster (the Lancaster City Council district) at the time of application, who reach the pass mark or score up to 5% below it.
  3. c. Lives in the City of Lancaster Girls who reached the pass mark and whose home is in the City of Lancaster district at the time of application.
  4. d. Children of LGGS staff Daughters of staff who have worked at the school for two years or more, who reached the pass mark.
  5. e. Pupil Premium, living outside the City of Lancaster Pupil Premium girls living outside the Lancaster district who reach the pass mark or score up to 5% below it.
  6. f. All other applicants Every other girl who reached the pass mark, in test-rank order.

Good to know

  • The 'City of Lancaster' means the whole Lancaster City Council district, not just the town.
  • The pass mark is set each year after the test, so a score that passed one year may not pass the next.
  • Girls eligible for Pupil Premium can get free online 11+ preparation through Atom Learning.
  • If one twin or triplet gets a place, the others are offered places too.
  • Question types and style change every year: there are no past papers, and the free FSCE Familiarisation Guides are the only official preparation.
