Wilmington Grammar School for Girls

Dartford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must pass the Kent Test. Places then go to looked-after children, siblings at either Wilmington grammar school, staff children, up to 18 'Governor places' for the top Kent Test maths scorers within 5 miles, then girls living within 1.5 miles or in about 30 named local parishes (Pupil Premium first), then other Pupil Premium girls within 3 miles, then everyone else by distance. Apart from Governor places, the score doesn't matter once you pass.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Children in local authority care, or previously in care and then adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order (including children adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Sibling at WGSB or WGSG A sibling at this school or at Wilmington Grammar School for Boys (the brother/sister school on the same campus) when she starts.
  3. 3. Children of staff Children of staff employed at the school for at least two years, or recruited to fill a skill-shortage post.
  4. 4. Governor places: top 18 Kent Test maths scores, within 5 miles (up to 18 places) Up to 18 places (10% of PAN) for girls living within 5 miles who are in the top 18 selective places on the Kent Test mathematics paper. Ties go to the nearer home, then independent random selection.
  5. 5. Within 1.5 miles or in the named parishes/wards (Pupil Premium first) Girls living within 1.5 miles of the school or in one of 30 named parishes/wards around Dartford, Sevenoaks and Gravesham. Within this group, Pupil Premium children come first, then by distance.
  6. 6. Other Pupil Premium girls within 3 miles Pupil Premium girls living within 3 miles who are not already covered by criterion 5.
  7. 7. All other eligible girls, by distance All other girls with a grammar assessment, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Apart from the 18 Governor places, your daughter's score doesn't matter once she passes the Kent Test.
  • Governor places rank girls on the Kent Test maths score only.
  • A brother or sister at Wilmington Grammar School for Boys counts as a sibling.
  • If one twin or triplet gets a place, the others are offered places too, as long as each passed the Kent Test.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
