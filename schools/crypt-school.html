The Crypt School

Gloucester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Children who reach the qualifying standard are offered places in this order: looked-after children, then Pupil Premium children, then children of staff (each of these groups by distance from the school), then everyone else in test rank order. Pupil Premium and looked-after children have a lower qualifying standard.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the (lower) qualifying standard. Nearest first; equal distance goes to the higher score.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Children with free school meals at any point in the six years before test registration closed, who reach the lower qualifying standard. No limit on numbers. Nearest first; equal distance goes to the higher score.
  3. 3. Children of staff Qualifying children of staff employed for two years or more, or recruited to a skills-shortage post. Nearest first; equal distance goes to the higher score.
  4. 4. All other qualifying children in test rank order Everyone else who reaches the Qualifying Standard, highest score first; equal scores go to whoever lives closer.

Good to know

  • Gloucestershire offers the highest-ranked school on your form that your child qualifies for, so only name the Crypt if your child met its qualifying standard.
  • Unusually, looked-after, Pupil Premium and staff children are placed by distance from the school, not by test score.
  • From September 2028 entry the Gloucestershire grammar schools switch to an FSCE test on Saturday 3 July 2027 (end of Year 5), with registration from 8 February to 19 March 2027.
  • The Crypt is the only mixed grammar school in Gloucester.
  • The admission number rose to 155 for September 2026 entry.
  • Exceptionally gifted children can be admitted early with a headteacher's recommendation, but a child who sits the test early can't sit it again the following year.
  • Answers go on separate answer sheets that are marked electronically, and rough paper is provided.
