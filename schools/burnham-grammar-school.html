Burnham Grammar School

Slough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after children, up to 6 places go to looked-after or catchment Pupil Premium children scoring 115-120. Then come qualified catchment Pupil Premium children, siblings and former siblings (wherever they live), medical/social cases, staff children and the catchment, then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify.
  2. 2. Up to 6 places: looked-after or catchment Pupil Premium children scoring 115-120 Up to 6 places for children scoring 115-120 who are looked after (living anywhere) or who live in catchment and are eligible for Pupil Premium. Looked-after children first, then the nearest.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium in catchment, qualified Qualified children living in catchment since 1 September 2026 who are eligible for Pupil Premium.
  4. 4. Siblings Qualified siblings of children who will be at the school in September 2027, wherever they live.
  5. 5. Former siblings Qualified children whose sibling used to attend the school.
  6. 6. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified children with exceptional medical or social needs.
  7. 7. Children of staff Qualified children of eligible staff.
  8. 8. Catchment Qualified children living in catchment continuously since 1 September 2026, nearest first.
  9. 9. All other children Remaining places to other qualified children, nearest first.

  • Brothers and sisters, including former siblings, rank above the catchment area and don't need to live in it.
