Kendrick School

Reading

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One test day (two papers covering English, maths, verbal and non-verbal reasoning), ranked by total standardised score. Looked-after girls, and Pupil/Service Premium girls living in the designated area, get first priority with a qualifying score 5 points lower. Up to 75% of places then go in score order to girls in the designated area (Priority Areas 1 and 2). The last 25% go in score order to Priority Area 1 first, then Priority Area 2, then everyone else. Nobody from outside the designated area has been offered a place since 2013.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reach the lower qualifying score are offered a place, even if not in the top 128. There is no address requirement.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Service Premium, living in the designated area Pupil Premium or Service Premium girls whose permanent home is in Priority Area 1 or 2 on 31 August 2026 (and still at admission) are offered a place if they reach the lower qualifying score, even if not in the top 128.
  3. 3. Lives in the designated area (Priority Area 1 or 2) - up to 75% of places Girls whose permanent home (shared with parent/carer) is in Priority Area 1 or 2 on 31 August 2026, and still at admission, get places in score order until 75% (96) of places are filled across criteria 1-3. Priority Areas 1 and 2 are treated equally here.
  4. 4. Lives in Priority Area 1 (remaining 25%) The remaining 25% (32) places go first to qualifying girls in Priority Area 1 (the nearer postcodes) not yet offered a place, in score order.
  5. 5. Lives in Priority Area 2 Then qualifying girls in Priority Area 2 not yet offered a place, in score order.
  6. 6. Lives outside the designated area Girls living outside the designated area are considered only if places remain. None has been offered a place since the designated area was introduced in 2013.

Good to know

  • Nobody living outside the designated area has been offered a place since 2013, and the school strongly advises out-of-area families not to register.
  • For 2027 entry, 698 girls sat the test and 352 reached the qualifying score of 99.00, for 128 places.
  • The qualifying score is a total of two papers, so don't compare it directly with other schools' pass marks.
  • Address checks are strict. You must declare every property any parent owns, and renting in Priority Area 1 while owning in Priority Area 2 means the Priority Area 2 address counts.
  • Pupil Premium includes 'Ever 6' free school meals, and the status must hold on 31 August 2026.
