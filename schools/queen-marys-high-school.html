Queen Mary's High School

Walsall

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test (the West Midlands grammar schools' GL test, run locally by the Shropshire, Walsall and Wolverhampton consortium). Girls must reach the qualifying score set by the governors (200 for 2027 entry). Looked-after girls come first. Next come up to 45 Pupil Premium girls, with those at Walsall state primaries first. Then everyone else in score order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the qualifying score, in score order.
  2. b. Pupil Premium (up to 30% = 45 places), Walsall primaries first Up to 45 Pupil Premium girls who reach the qualifying score. Girls at Walsall state primaries come first. Others are still considered under criterion c on their score.
  3. c. All other girls by score Everyone else who reached the qualifying score, in score order, until 150 places are filled. Where she lives doesn't matter.

Good to know

  • Pupil Premium girls who reached the qualifying score have all been offered places in recent years.
  • The lowest score offered to girls not on Pupil Premium was 216 in 2026, 213 in 2025 and 216 in 2024.
  • Where she lives doesn't matter: after looked-after and Pupil Premium girls, places go purely in score order.
  • Scores are on the West Midlands Grammar Schools scale (median 200). Birmingham reports the same test on its own scale, where 205 qualifies, so check which scale you're reading.
  • Pupil Premium evidence must arrive before test day; nothing is accepted on or after it.
