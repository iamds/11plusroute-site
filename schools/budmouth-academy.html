Budmouth Academy

Weymouth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

An all-ability academy in Weymouth that offers 30 places to the highest scorers in its own abilities test, sat on a Saturday in early October. After EHCPs and looked-after children, those 30 places come first. The rest go to siblings, some staff children, children linked to Portesham Primary, then everyone else by distance. Your child doesn't have to sit the test: children who don't sit it, or don't make the top 30, are still considered under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Top 30 in the abilities test The 30 children who show the highest ability in the test are offered places automatically. Children who don't get one of these places are still considered under the other criteria.
  3. 3. Siblings Children with a brother or sister at the academy when they start.
  4. 4. Children of staff (skill-shortage posts) Children of staff recruited to a post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  5. 5. Portesham Primary link Children who live in the catchment area of Portesham Primary School or who attend it.
  6. 6. All other children, by distance Everyone else, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Budmouth is an all-ability academy, not a grammar school: only 30 of its 260 places (about 12%) go by the abilities test.
  • Children who win an ability place can join the 'high ability group' or follow the normal curriculum pathway.
  • Budmouth was not oversubscribed for September 2026.
  • Up to two places in the Autism Base are filled separately under the council's policy for specialist bases.
