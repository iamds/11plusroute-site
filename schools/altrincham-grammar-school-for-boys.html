Altrincham Grammar School for Boys

Altrincham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the shared Trafford Grammar School Consortium test (GL Assessment: verbal, non-verbal and maths, two papers) on 14 September 2026. The normal pass mark is a total standardised score of 334; looked-after boys and Pupil Premium boys need 324. If more boys qualify than there are places, looked-after boys come first, then up to 20 Pupil Premium boys living in the priority area (WA13, WA14, WA15, M33 and M23 postcodes within Trafford) who attend a Trafford state primary, then up to 10 more Pupil Premium boys at Trafford state primaries, then boys in the priority area, then everyone else. Within each group, places go in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys scoring 324+ Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) with a total standardised score of 324 or more.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium boys in the priority area at a Trafford state primary (up to 20 at 324-333, plus all at 334+) Pupil Premium boys whose permanent home is in the priority admission area and who attend a Trafford state-funded primary school. Up to 20 with scores of 324-333 are offered places in score order. Those scoring 334 or more are offered places as well, on top of the 20.
  3. 3. Up to 10 more Pupil Premium boys at a Trafford state primary, scoring 324+ Up to 10 Pupil Premium boys who attend a Trafford state-funded primary school, wherever they live, with a score of 324 or more, in score order.
  4. 4. Lives in the priority admission area, scoring 334+ Boys whose permanent home is in the priority admission area (WA13, WA14, WA15, M33 and M23 postcodes within Trafford), with a score of 334 or more, in score order.
  5. 5. Lives outside the priority admission area, scoring 334+ Eligible boys living outside the priority admission area with a score of 334 or more, in score order.

Good to know

  • Looked-after and Pupil Premium boys need a total standardised score of 324; everyone else needs 334.
  • The priority area covers only the parts of WA13, WA14, WA15, M33 and M23 that lie inside Trafford.
  • Address checks are strict: home owners must prove ownership, and the sale of a previous home if they've owned this one for under 2 years.
  • From 2028 entry the consortium moves to a curriculum-based FSCE test, and from 2029 entry the test moves to the summer term of Year 5.
  • If AGSB is your first choice, the school strongly advises putting it as your highest preference. The council offers the highest-preference school your son qualifies for.
  • A free familiarisation guide has practice questions and a sample answer sheet, and practice papers are on the GL website.
