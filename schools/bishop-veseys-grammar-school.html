Bishop Vesey's Grammar School

Sutton Coldfield

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test (the Birmingham grammar schools' GL test). Boys must reach the qualifying score (205 for 2027 entry). New for 2027, the school has distance zones and nearly all priority goes to Zone 1 (within 12 miles). Looked-after boys in Zone 1 come first, then up to 38 Pupil Premium boys in Zone 1, then other Zone 1 boys in score order. Only if places remain do boys 12-20 miles away (Zone 2), then beyond 20 miles (Zone 3), get places in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children in Zone 1 (incl. internationally adopted) Looked-after and previously looked-after boys living in Zone 1 (within 12 miles) who reach the qualifying score, in score order. Those living further away are ranked with other boys in their zone.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium in Zone 1 (up to 38 places) Up to 38 Pupil Premium boys living in Zone 1 who reach the qualifying score, in score order. Any not placed here are still considered under criterion 3. Pupil Premium boys outside Zone 1 get no extra priority.
  3. 3. Other boys in Zone 1 (up to 12 miles) Other qualifying boys who live within 12 miles (straight line), in score order.
  4. 4. Other boys in Zone 2 (12-20 miles) Only if places remain: qualifying boys living between 12 and 20 miles, in score order.
  5. 5. Other boys in Zone 3 (beyond 20 miles) Only if places remain after Zones 1 and 2: qualifying boys living more than 20 miles away, in score order.

Good to know

  • The distance zones are new for 2027 entry. Before that, places went to looked-after boys, then up to 38 Pupil Premium boys, then everyone else in score order, wherever they lived.
  • Looked-after and Pupil Premium boys living outside Zone 1 get no special priority: they are ranked by score with other boys in their zone.
  • Scores are on the Birmingham test's combined scale (qualifying score 205), so don't compare them with single-paper scores of around 100 at other schools.
  • Families living beyond a reasonable commuting distance may be asked for evidence that they plan to move before their son is tested.
  • Only the separate answer sheet is marked; the question booklet has space for working out.
  • Free familiarisation questions are published on the West Midlands Grammar Schools website.
