Simon Langton Girls' Grammar School

Canterbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Pass the Kent Test. If more qualifying girls apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after children, then sisters of current pupils, then girls with medical/social needs, then Pupil Premium girls, then everyone else by distance (nearest first). There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or previously in care (including adopted from state care abroad), who have a grammar assessment.
  2. 2. Current family association (sibling) Girls with a brother or sister at the school at the time of entry.
  3. 3. Health and special access reasons Girls (or whose parents) have a significant medical, health, social or access need to attend this particular school, applied in line with the Equality Act 2010.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium Girls eligible for Pupil Premium. Return the supplementary form to the school by 31 October and name the school on the application.
  5. 5. Nearness of home to school All other qualifying girls, nearest first.

Good to know

  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • A grammar assessment makes your daughter eligible but doesn't guarantee a place: offers follow the oversubscription criteria, with nearest first within each.
  • If your daughter lives at two addresses equally, the one closer to your first-preference school is used.
  • Girls joining later in Year 7 or above are tested by the school (GL Progress Tests: aggregate 330, no score below 110).
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
