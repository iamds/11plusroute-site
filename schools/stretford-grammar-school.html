Stretford Grammar School

Manchester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 334 in the Trafford Consortium test (Pupil Premium children scoring 324-333 are automatically raised to a pass, and others in that band can ask for a Local Review). Qualifiers are ranked: looked-after children, Pupil/Service Premium children in the catchment, siblings, then catchment (M32, M33, M41, M15, M16, M17, M21), then Pupil/Service Premium outside it, then everyone else by distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or special guardianship.
  2. 2. Adopted from state care outside England Children who were in state care abroad and were adopted.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium / Service Premium in the catchment area Pupil Premium or Service Premium children living in the catchment area (criterion 5), nearest first.
  4. 4. Sibling at the school Children with a brother or sister who will be in Years 7-11 at the school in September 2027. No address requirement.
  5. 5. Lives in the catchment area Qualifying children living in M32, M33, M41, M15, M16, M17 or M21, nearest first.
  6. 6. Pupil Premium / Service Premium outside the catchment area Pupil Premium or Service Premium children living outside the catchment, nearest first.
  7. 7. Everyone else, by distance All other qualifying children, nearest first. Score doesn't matter once your child has qualified.

Good to know

  • Once your child has qualified, the score doesn't matter: places go by criteria and distance.
  • If you move into the catchment after registering, your registration address is used until after the first-round offers.
  • Moving further away counts straight away.
  • For 2028 entry the test changes to one based on the KS2 curriculum up to the end of Year 5.
  • A free familiarisation guide has practice questions and a sample answer sheet, and practice papers are on the GL website.
