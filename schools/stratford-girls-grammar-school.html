Stratford Girls' Grammar School

Stratford-upon-Avon

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Sit the Warwickshire 11+ test (shared with the West Midlands grammar schools). Girls who reach the school's Automatic Qualifying Score and live within the 16.936-mile priority circle around Stratford-upon-Avon are offered places in score order. Looked-after girls, and up to 19 Pupil Premium (free school meals) girls in the circle, can get in with up to 20 marks below that score. Girls outside the circle have not been offered places in recent years.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the AQS or score up to 20 marks below it.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (free school meals) girls in the priority circle - up to 19 places Girls in the priority circle who attract the Pupil Premium through free school meals and reach the AQS or score up to 20 marks below it, up to 19 places. A girl in this group who reaches the full AQS also qualifies under category 3 anyway.
  3. 3. Lives in the priority circle and reaches the AQS Girls living within the 16.936-mile priority circle who reach the AQS, highest scores first. In 2024-2026 this category filled the rest of the school, and in 2025 and 2026 the lowest score offered was 232, five or six marks above the AQS.
  4. 4. Lives outside the priority circle and reaches the AQS Girls outside the circle who reach the AQS. They are offered places on National Offer Day only if there are not enough qualified girls in the circle. Otherwise they usually wait for later rounds. No offers went to this category in 2023-2026.
  5. 5. Below the AQS but at or above the waiting-list score Girls between the waiting-list score and the AQS (inside or outside the circle). In practice this is the waiting list: girls below the AQS are placed on the waiting list.

Good to know

  • In 2024-2026 every place except the looked-after and Pupil Premium places went to girls inside the priority circle.
  • Reaching the AQS hasn't been enough on its own: in 2025 and 2026 offers in the circle stopped at 232.
  • Pupil Premium priority is for girls on free school meals, not the Service Premium.
  • In-year applications and appeals are usually unsuccessful, even for girls at the qualifying standard.
  • A 13-mile inner priority circle has been proposed for 2028 entry; it doesn't apply for 2027.
  • A free familiarisation booklet with example questions is on the Warwickshire Admissions website, or on paper on request.
  • Children eligible for the Pupil Premium can get free Atom Learning 11+ preparation through the school.
