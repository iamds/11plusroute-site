Bournemouth School

Bournemouth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the shared Bournemouth and Poole consortium test (GL maths, English and verbal reasoning) in late September. Those who reach the required standard are 'eligible' and are ranked: looked-after boys, then Pupil Premium boys, then boys living in the priority area (Bournemouth postcodes BH1-BH11 plus parts of BH12 and BH23), then sons of long-serving staff, then everyone else. Each group is ranked by total test score. In 2026 only 3 boys from outside the priority area got places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after children Eligible looked-after, previously looked-after and internationally adopted previously looked-after boys, in order of test score.
  2. ii. Pupil Premium Remaining eligible boys eligible for the Pupil Premium Grant (FSM now or in the last six years, or a child of UK Armed Forces personnel in the last six years), in order of test score. There is no address requirement.
  3. iii. Lives in the priority area Remaining eligible boys living in the priority area, in order of test score.
  4. iv. Sons of staff (employed 2+ years) Eligible sons of staff who have worked at the school for at least two years, in order of test score. This comes after the priority-area group, so it only matters for staff living outside the area.
  5. v. All other eligible boys Remaining eligible boys (outside the priority area, not Pupil Premium), in order of test score.

Good to know

  • The four Bournemouth and Poole grammar schools share one test, so one sitting lets your son be ranked for both Bournemouth School and Poole Grammar.
  • From September 2028 entry (tests in September 2027) the consortium test is set by Quest Assessments: maths, English and verbal reasoning, 40 minutes each, with nothing beyond the Year 5 curriculum.
  • GL Assessment publishes free 11+ familiarisation papers in maths, English and verbal reasoning. Answers go on a pre-printed answer sheet, and time limits are strict.
