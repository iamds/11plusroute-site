Borden Grammar School

Sittingbourne

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son must pass either the Kent Test or the optional Borden Test (which he can only sit if he also sits the Kent Test). Both routes count the same. After EHCP and looked-after boys, up to 15% of places go to boys on free school meals in ME9-ME12, then brothers, medical/social need, then boys in ME9-ME12, then ME8/ME13, then anywhere else, nearest first within each group. In 2025 and 2026 the school had spare places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Free school meals in ME9-ME12 (up to 15% of places) Boys living in ME9, ME10, ME11 or ME12 who were eligible for free school meals at any time in Years 3-6. Up to 15% of places. Return the SIF by 31 October 2026.
  3. 3. Current family association (brother or sister at the school) A brother (or a sister in the sixth form) attending the school when your son starts: natural, adopted, step or foster siblings, or children living as siblings in the same house.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons For boys whose (or whose parent's) physical or mental health or social needs give a demonstrable and significant need to attend this school, backed by professional evidence.
  5. 5a. Traditional school area: ME9, ME10, ME11, ME12 Boys living in postcode areas beginning ME9, ME10, ME11 or ME12, nearest first.
  6. 5b. Traditional school area: ME8, ME13 Then boys in postcode areas beginning ME8 or ME13, nearest first.
  7. 5c. Any other area Then boys living anywhere else, nearest first.

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Monday 1 June 2026: Kent Test registration opens
  • Wednesday 1 July 2026: Kent Test registration and special-arrangements requests close (midnight)
  • Wednesday 8 July 2026: Late Borden Test registrations (evidenced exceptional circumstances only)
  • Tuesday 21 July 2026: Borden Test special-arrangements requests close
  • Tuesday 1 September 2026: Secondary applications open
  • Thursday 10 September 2026: Kent Test in Kent primary schools
  • Saturday 12 September 2026: Borden Test at the school (also Kent Test centre weekend for out-of-county children)
  • Thursday 15 October 2026: Kent Test decisions emailed after 4pm
  • Saturday 31 October 2026: Free school meals SIF deadline
  • Monday 2 November 2026: Application deadline for Kent residents (midnight)
  • Monday 7 December 2026: Last day to change your application details in time for offer day
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day (emails after 4pm)
  • Monday 15 March 2027: Accept/decline the offer; deadline for late applications and waiting-list requests for KCC's reallocation
  • Tuesday 13 April 2027: Deadline to lodge an appeal to be heard before September
  • Tuesday 27 April 2027: KCC reallocates places from waiting lists; schools then run their own waiting lists

Good to know

  • The Borden Test gives 'near miss' boys a route to a place without waiting for appeals.
  • Borden filled its 150 places in 2024 but offered only 125 in 2025 and 2026, so in those years every eligible applicant was offered a place.
  • The free school meals priority counts eligibility at any time in Years 3-6, and only for boys living in ME9-ME12.
  • No Supplementary Information Form is needed to sit the Borden Test; it is only needed for the free school meals priority.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
