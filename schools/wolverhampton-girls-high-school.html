Wolverhampton Girls' High School

Wolverhampton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test (the West Midlands grammar schools' GL test, run locally by the Shropshire, Walsall and Wolverhampton consortium). Looked-after girls and the top 25 Pupil Premium girls who reach a qualifying score (set in October) get automatic offers. All other places go strictly in score order. There is no catchment area. In 2026 the last place went to a score of 208.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Stage 1: looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) are offered a place if they score at or above the qualifying score.
  2. 2. Stage 1: top 25 Pupil Premium girls The 25 highest-scoring Pupil Premium girls who reach the qualifying score are offered places. Other Pupil Premium girls go back into the main merit order for Stage 2.
  3. 3. Stage 2: everyone else in strict score order All remaining places go in strict order of merit by test score. The council receives the merit table and makes the offers.

  • Saturday 31 October 2026: Home LA preference form deadline
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day
  • Friday 31 December 2027: Waiting list closes (end of autumn term 2027)

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area: apart from looked-after and Pupil Premium places, offers go strictly by score.
  • In 2026 the last place went to a score of 208.
  • Results are shared across the Shropshire, Walsall and Wolverhampton grammar schools; tick the boxes at registration if you also want Birmingham or Warwickshire schools to see them.
  • No allowance is made for performance affected by illness, so don't let your daughter sit the test if she's unwell.
