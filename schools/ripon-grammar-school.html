Ripon Grammar School

Ripon

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child sits a two-paper GL Assessment test run by North Yorkshire Council (12 September 2026). The cut-off is set so that about the top 28% of Year 6 children living in the Ripon area pass; it was 191 for 2027 entry. Of the 117 places, 14 are for boarders. Qualifying day applicants are then ranked: looked-after children, medical/social need, children living in the catchment area, children of staff, then children living outside the catchment. Within each group, siblings come first and then the shortest route distance. In each of the last three years every group was filled up to out-of-catchment children, with the last day place going at 7-10.5 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children who reached the cut-off, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Special social or medical reasons Children the council accepts have special social or medical reasons for needing this school.
  3. 3. Lives in the catchment (normal) area Qualifying children who live in the Ripon catchment area. In each of the last three years the last day place went to an out-of-catchment child, so every qualifying catchment applicant was offered a place.
  4. 4. Children of staff Qualifying children of staff who live outside the catchment area.
  5. 5. Lives outside the catchment area All other qualifying children: those with a sibling at the school first, then by shortest route distance. The last day place went at 10.48 miles in 2026, 9.86 miles in 2025 and 7.13 miles in 2024.

Good to know

  • The cut-off mark is set so that the top 28% of Year 6 children living in the Ripon area reach it. Children from outside the area need the same mark but get lower priority for day places.
  • Past cut-off marks: 191 for 2027 entry, 203 for 2026 and 196 for 2025. The test papers changed for 2026 testing, so 191 can't be compared directly with earlier years.
  • In recent years the last day places have gone to children living outside the catchment area.
  • Tuition is free for boarders. 2026-27 boarding fees are £5,033 a term (weekly) and £5,422 a term (full), plus a refundable £1,750 bond.
  • Day, weekly boarding and termly boarding places count as separate preferences on your application.
