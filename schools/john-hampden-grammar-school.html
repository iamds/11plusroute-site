John Hampden Grammar School

High Wycombe

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son needs 121+ in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). 12 places are kept for looked-after or Pupil/Service Premium boys in the catchment who score 113-120. The score doesn't rank qualified boys. Places go by: looked after, the 12 Pupil Premium places, other Pupil Premium boys in catchment, brothers and Wycombe High sisters in catchment, staff, Priority Area A, Priority Area B, medical/social, then distance. In 2026 all qualified Area A boys got in and Area B boys got in up to 11.5 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys (incl. adopted from state care abroad) Qualified boys who are looked after or previously looked after, including boys adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. 12 places: looked-after or Pupil/Service Premium catchment boys scoring 113-120 12 places for catchment boys who are looked after or previously looked after, or eligible for the Pupil Premium or Service Premium, and score 113-120. If more than 12 apply, the nearest get the places. A boy who isn't placed here isn't qualified for any other criterion (unless he qualifies through Selection Review).
  3. 3. Other Pupil/Service Premium boys who qualify (121+) and live in the catchment Boys in the catchment who are eligible for the Pupil Premium or Service Premium and score 121 or more.
  4. 4. Brothers of boys at the school, living in the catchment Qualified boys with a brother at John Hampden who live in the catchment.
  5. 5. Boys with a sister at Wycombe High School, living in the catchment Qualified boys whose sister attends Wycombe High School (the neighbouring girls' grammar) and who live in the John Hampden catchment. The council calls these 'linked siblings'.
  6. 6. Sons of staff Qualified sons of permanent staff employed for two years or more, or recruited to a skills-shortage post. No catchment requirement.
  7. 7. Living in Priority Area A Qualified boys living in Priority Area A (High Wycombe and surroundings), nearest first.
  8. 8. Living in Priority Area B Qualified boys living in Priority Area B (Maidenhead area and parts of South Bucks), nearest first.
  9. 9. Exceptional medical or social need Qualified boys with exceptional medical or social needs that only this school can meet. Unusually, this comes after the catchment areas, so in practice it only helps boys from outside the catchment.
  10. 10. Everyone else by distance All other qualified boys, nearest first.

Good to know

  • In 2026 every qualified boy in Priority Area A got a place, and Priority Area B places reached 11.5 miles.
  • Medical and social need ranks below both catchment areas, which is unusual. In practice it only helps boys from outside the catchment.
  • You must be living at your home address by 1 September 2026.
  • Up to 12 catchment boys who are looked after or on Pupil/Service Premium can get in with a score of 113-120.
