Weald of Kent Grammar School

Tonbridge

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls qualify by passing either the Kent Test or the school's own Weald Grammar Assessment, which count equally. Test scores don't affect who gets in. Qualified girls are ranked by priority group: looked-after girls, 40 Pupil Premium places, sisters/brothers, medical/social need, staff children, girls in TN9-TN12 and listed local parishes, then everyone else. Within each group, the nearest to the Tonbridge campus go first.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (40 places) 40 places for girls eligible for Pupil Premium, nearest to the Tonbridge campus first. You must send the SIF by 31 October.
  3. 3. Sister or brother at the school Girls with a sister or brother at the school when she starts. Twins and triplets are all offered places even if that takes the school over 270.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons Claims must reach the school by 31 October. The Admissions Committee decides.
  5. 5. Children of permanent staff Daughters (including adopted and foster children) of permanent staff who live with them.
  6. 6. Lives in the listed postcodes or parishes (by distance) Girls whose permanent home is in postcodes TN9-TN12 or one of the listed parishes in Tonbridge & Malling and Sevenoaks district, nearest to the Tonbridge campus first.
  7. 7. All other postcodes Everyone else, nearest to the Tonbridge campus first.

Good to know

  • Passing either the Kent Test or the school's own Weald Grammar Assessment is enough; passing both gives no advantage.
  • Test scores don't affect who gets in: places go by priority group, then distance to the Tonbridge campus.
  • Which campus (Tonbridge or Sevenoaks) your daughter attends is decided after offers.
  • Only grammar-assessed girls can join the waiting list.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
