Saint Ambrose College

Altrincham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the College's own entrance exam (GL verbal reasoning plus in-house maths and English, all multiple choice) on 18 September 2026 and need a total age-standardised score of 320. If more boys qualify than there are places, baptised Catholic boys come first (looked-after, then brothers, then boys living in the nominated pastoral-area postcodes, then other Catholics), then other looked-after boys, catechumens and Eastern Christians, then other Christians, then brothers, then everyone else. Within each category Pupil Premium boys come first, then the nearest. Test score only breaks ties.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Baptised Catholic looked after / previously looked after boys Baptised Catholic boys who are looked after or previously looked after (including those adopted from state care abroad).
  2. 2. Baptised Catholic boys with a brother at the College Baptised Catholic boys whose brother will be at the College when he starts.
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic boys living in a nominated Local Pastoral Area Baptised Catholic boys whose home postcode is in WA14, WA15, M31, M33, M16, M21, M32 or M41.
  4. 4. Other baptised Catholic boys All other baptised Catholic boys who qualified. There's no Mass-attendance requirement, just baptism or reception.
  5. 5. Other looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys who aren't baptised Catholics.
  6. 6. Catechumens and members of an Eastern Christian Church Catechumens of a Catholic Church, and members of Eastern Christian (including Orthodox) Churches.
  7. 7. Baptised Christian boys with a brother at the College Baptised Christian boys whose brother will be at the College when he starts.
  8. 8. Practising Christian boys of other denominations (minister's reference) Christian boys practising in other denominations, with a minister's reference confirming they are a full and active member of the Church (attending more than once a month).
  9. 9. Other boys with a brother at the College Other boys whose brother will be at the College when he starts.
  10. 10. Other boys All other boys who qualified: Pupil Premium boys first, then nearest home.

Good to know

  • Within each category, distance (after Pupil Premium) decides places, not the test score. The score only separates boys living the same distance away.
  • Catholic priority needs only baptism or reception into full communion before the 6 July 2026 closing date. There's no Mass-attendance requirement.
  • Other Christians need a minister's reference showing church attendance more than once a month.
  • A large share of places go to families outside Trafford: 77 of 149 offers in 2026.
  • The qualifying score is 320.
