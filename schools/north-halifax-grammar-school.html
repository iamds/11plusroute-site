Grammar School Admissions · Year 7 entry, school by school

The North Halifax Grammar School

Halifax

Your child sits the joint Halifax Grammar Schools test (FSCE: two English and maths papers plus creative writing), shared with Crossley Heath. They must reach the qualifying standard in English, maths and creative writing, which means being in the top 510 in order of merit. Looked-after children and Pupil/Service Premium children who qualify come first. Next, up to 10% of places (18) go to qualifying children living in the Illingworth & Mixenden and Ovenden wards. All other places go in order of merit on the English and maths papers.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after / special guardianship Looked-after children, and children previously looked after who became subject to an adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Pupil Premium or Service Premium Qualifying children who attract the Pupil Premium (FSM in the last 6 years, or in or previously in care) or the Service Premium. Evidence received after the end of September counts only after the main allocation round.
  3. c. Lives in the Priority Admissions Area (up to 10% of places) Up to 18 places for qualifying children whose permanent home address is in the Illingworth and Mixenden ward or the Ovenden ward by the test registration closing date (26 June 2026). If more than 18 qualify, places go in order of merit.
  4. d. All other qualifying children in order of merit Once places under (a)-(c) are allocated, the remaining places go in combined order of merit for English and maths until the school reaches 180.

Good to know

  • About 1,300 children sit the joint test each year for 360 places across the two Halifax grammar schools.
  • Preference order matters: if your child is eligible at a school ranked higher on your application, that is the one offered.
  • The priority area for Illingworth & Mixenden and Ovenden wards was added from September 2026 entry, when non-verbal reasoning was dropped and creative writing added.
  • Creative writing is only marked for children who meet the English and maths standard.
