The Norton Knatchbull School

Ashford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must be assessed suitable for grammar school through the Kent Test. If more qualified boys apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after boys, then Pupil Premium boys (supplementary form needed), then brothers of current pupils, then health/special access reasons, then everyone else by straight-line distance. There is no catchment area and test scores don't rank qualified boys.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England) who have passed come first.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Boys receiving Pupil Premium, if the supplementary form reaches the school by 31 October.
  3. 3. Current family association (brother/sister at the school) Boys with a brother or sister at the school when they start.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons Boys (or families) with a significant medical, health, social or access need that only this school can meet, backed by professional evidence sent before the closing date.
  5. 5. Nearness of home to school All other eligible boys, nearest first (straight-line distance).

Good to know

  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • Test scores don't rank qualified boys: places go by the priority criteria and then distance.
  • Offers have been below the 210 places in recent years.
  • Twins and triplets are all admitted, even if this takes the school over its 210 places.
  • Kent's application deadline for 2027 entry is Monday 2 November 2026, and offers come on Monday 1 March 2027.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
