Poole High School

Poole

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A comprehensive school in Poole (Twynham Learning trust). 10% of places go to its 'Expressly Academic Class', in test-score order, to children who sit its own English, maths and verbal reasoning tests in early October. The rest go to looked-after children and staff children, then (for everyone else) in-catchment siblings, older siblings, catchment, out-of-catchment siblings and finally others, by walking distance. Children who don't sit the test, or aren't offered an Expressly Academic place, are automatically considered for a standard place.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Children of staff Children of staff who have worked at the school for at least two years, or who fill a skill-shortage post.
  3. 3. Expressly Academic 10% Children selected by the tests, in score order. Children not offered an Expressly Academic place are automatically considered for a standard place.
  4. 4a. In catchment, with a sibling Children living in the catchment area with a brother or sister at the school.
  5. 4b. Sibling on roll since 6 January 2020 Other children with a sibling at the school who was already on roll on 6 January 2020. This is a protection from a past catchment change, so very few children now qualify.
  6. 4c. In catchment Children living in the catchment area.
  7. 4d. Out of catchment, with a sibling Children living outside the catchment area with a brother or sister at the school.
  8. 4e. Out of catchment Everyone else, nearest first by walking route.

Good to know

  • Poole High is a comprehensive academy. The only selection is the Expressly Academic Class, 10% of the intake.
  • One preference on your council application covers both an Expressly Academic place and a standard place.
  • Children not offered an Expressly Academic place are automatically considered for a standard place.
  • Late applicants can't sit the Expressly Academic tests.
  • Places in the Expressly Academic Class go to the 32 highest scorers not offered a higher preference.
  • The school doesn't provide practice papers or sample tests.
