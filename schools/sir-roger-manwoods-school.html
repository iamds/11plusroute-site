Sir Roger Manwood's School

Sandwich

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must pass the Kent Test. If more qualified children apply than the 150 places, priority goes to looked-after children, then siblings, then Pupil Premium/Service Premium children living in CT3, CT11-CT16 postcodes within 8.5 miles, then children of staff, then everyone else by distance. In recent years the school has offered fewer places than its 150.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, and children previously in care (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order straight after care), including those adopted from state care outside England. No address requirement.
  2. 2. Brother or sister at the school A sibling on roll when your child starts: children living as brother or sister in the same house, including adopted, step- and foster siblings.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium (incl. Service Premium) in CT3, CT11-CT16 within 8.5 miles Pupil Premium or Service Premium children who live in a CT3, CT11, CT12, CT13, CT14, CT15 or CT16 postcode and within 8.5 miles (straight line) of the school. The area matches where students offered places have lived since the school moved to five forms of entry.
  4. 4. Children of staff Children of staff employed at the school for two or more years when you apply, and/or recruited to a vacant post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  5. 5. Distance from home to school Everyone else, nearest first, by straight-line distance from home to school.

Good to know

  • In 2024-2026 the school offered 113-124 places against 150, so every qualified applicant who named it has in practice been offered a place.
  • Places are not ranked by Kent Test score: the test only decides whether a child is suitable for grammar school.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • Children applying after Christmas of Year 7 sit the school's own online test instead (standardised score of 110+ needed).
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
