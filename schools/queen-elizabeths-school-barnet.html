Queen Elizabeth's School, Barnet

Barnet

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test in September: two multiple-choice papers, English and maths, sat in one session. Boys with a combined standardised score of 225 or more qualify, and all 180 places go to the highest-scoring qualifying boys who name the school on their CAF. There is no catchment area and no priority for looked-after children, siblings or Pupil Premium. Equal scores are split by distance from the school gate, then by an independent random ballot.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Qualifying applicants in order of combined score Boys who reach 225 and name the school are ranked by combined score, and the top 180 are offered places. Equal scores are split by distance (nearest first), then by an independent random ballot for the last place.

Good to know

  • Places are decided purely on test score. Where you live only matters if two boys have exactly the same score.
  • For 2027 entry the minimum combined score is 225.
  • Putting Queen Elizabeth's first on your application doesn't hurt your chances at your other schools.
  • No supplementary information form is needed.
  • Joining in Years 7-11 later is possible only if a vacancy occurs and your son passes the school's entrance tests.
