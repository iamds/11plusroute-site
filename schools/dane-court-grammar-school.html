Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, and children previously in care (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order straight after care), including those adopted from state care outside England. No address requirement.
  2. b. Health and special access reasons Applied in line with the Equality Act 2010. You need written professional evidence linking the need to Dane Court specifically.
  3. c. Pupil Premium (free school meals in the last 6 years) Children registered for free school meals at any time in the last 6 years. You must send the school's Supplementary Information Form with proof by 31 October as well as naming Dane Court on your application.
  4. d. Brother or sister at the school A brother or sister on roll who will still be at the school when your child starts, including adopted, step- and foster siblings living in the same house.
  5. e. Distance from home to school Everyone else, nearest first, by straight-line distance from home to school.

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Wednesday 1 July 2026: Kent Test registration closes at midnight; deadline for primary schools to request special arrangements
  • Tuesday 21 July 2026: Last date for late Kent Test registrations (paper form, children at KCC-maintained primary schools only)
  • Tuesday 1 September 2026: Secondary application (SCAF) opens
  • Thursday 10 September 2026: Kent Test in Kent primary schools
  • Saturday 12 September 2026: Kent Test at test centres for children at schools outside Kent (12/13 September)
  • Thursday 15 October 2026: Kent Test decisions and scores emailed after 4pm
  • Saturday 31 October 2026: Supplementary Information Form (Pupil Premium) due at the school
  • Monday 2 November 2026: SCAF closing date (midnight) for Kent residents
  • Monday 7 December 2026: Last date for changes to on-time applications
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day (emails after 4pm)
  • Monday 15 March 2027: Accept or decline the offer; deadline to join waiting lists for the reallocation
  • Tuesday 13 April 2027: Deadline for lodging appeals to be heard in the main round
  • Tuesday 27 April 2027: KCC reallocation of vacant places; schools run their own waiting lists after this

Good to know

  • Dane Court has been full every year recently (165 offers for 165 places in 2024, 2025 and 2026), so passing the Kent Test alone doesn't guarantee a place.
  • Equal cases are settled by random allocation.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
