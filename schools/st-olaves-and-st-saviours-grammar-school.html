St Olave's and St Saviour's Grammar School

Orpington

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Entry is purely on test performance. There is no catchment, sibling, feeder or faith priority. Boys sit a one-hour multiple-choice test (the SET) in September, and the top 450 are invited to a Stage 2 test in English and maths in November. Looked-after boys in the top 450 who sit Stage 2 are admitted. Up to 10 places go to Pupil Premium boys, ranked separately. All other places go in rank order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) are all admitted if they reach the top 450 in the SET and sit Stage 2.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (up to 10 places) Up to 10 places go to Pupil Premium boys who passed the SET and sat Stage 2. They are ranked separately from the main pool, which makes an offer much more likely.
  3. 3. All other candidates by rank Remaining places go in rank order of combined SET + Stage 2 score, subject to parental preference on the CAF. Boys who didn't sit the test are ranked after all who did.

Good to know

  • Places depend only on test rank: there is no catchment, sibling, feeder-school or faith priority.
  • A typical intake comes from about 90 primary schools.
  • Around 1,600 boys apply each year.
  • Up to 4 extra places go to choristers at the King's Chapel of the Savoy, who apply in Year 5.
