The Latymer School

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must live in the Inner Area, a fixed list of north and east London postcode districts (looked-after children excepted). There is one test day: GL maths and verbal reasoning, plus the school's own English paper. Children in the top 700 on maths and verbal reasoning have their English marked and are re-ranked. After looked-after children, up to 20 Free School Meals / Universal Credit places (top 900) and up to 20 music places (top 700) are filled. Everyone else in the Inner Area is then ranked by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children in the top 900 are admitted, with no limit. They don't have to live in the Inner Area.
  2. 2. Free School Meals / Universal Credit (up to 20 places) Up to 20 places for Inner Area children receiving Free School Meals or Universal Credit who are in the top 900, offered in rank order. If one withdraws, the next-ranked child under this criterion is considered.
  3. 3. Music (up to 20 places) Up to 20 places for musical Inner Area children in the top 700. There is no music test, but the school may audition up to 32. Many music applicants get in on score alone anyway.
  4. 4. Other Inner Area applicants in rank order Everyone else living in the Inner Area, by final (re-standardised) score.
  5. 5. Outside the Inner Area: not considered Applicants living outside the Inner Area are not considered until they live inside it. If they move in, they can be reconsidered for the waiting list.

Good to know

  • You must live in the Inner Area by 7 January 2027. Applications from outside it aren't considered until the family has moved in.
  • The last rank offered in the first round was 306 in 2024 and 293 in 2025.
  • Siblings get no priority.
  • From 2028 entry the school plans to switch to a Quest Assessments test (maths, English comprehension and writing).
