St Bernard's Catholic Grammar School

Slough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 111 or more in the Slough Consortium 11+. Places then go by faith category: looked-after children, practising Catholics (with a priest's reference or Certificate of Catholic Practice), other baptised Catholics, other Christians, pupils at four Slough Catholic primaries, other faiths, then everyone else. Within each category, Pupil Premium children come first, then by score. You must fill in the school's Supplementary Form by 31 October.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad).
  2. 2. Practising baptised Catholics Baptised Catholic children with a Priest's reference or a Bishops' Conference Certificate of Catholic Practice. Includes a looked-after child living with a family where a parent is Catholic.
  3. 3. Other baptised Catholics Other children baptised (or received) Catholic, including Eastern Catholic Churches.
  4. 4. Other Christians with a religious leader's reference Children baptised or dedicated in another Christian church (e.g. members of Churches Together in England) with a religious leader's reference.
  5. 5. Other baptised or dedicated Christians Other children baptised or dedicated in another Christian church.
  6. 6. Attends a St Peter's Pastoral Area Catholic primary and lives in Slough Children on roll at Holy Family, Our Lady of Peace, St Anthony's or St Ethelbert's Catholic Primary School when applying, who live in Slough (council tax paid to Slough Borough Council).
  7. 7. Attends a St Peter's Pastoral Area Catholic primary Children on roll at one of those four Catholic primaries, wherever they live.
  8. 8. Other faiths with a religious leader's reference Children of other (non-Christian) faiths with a religious leader's reference.
  9. 9. Other faiths Other children of other faiths.
  10. 10. Any other children Everyone else, including anyone who didn't return the Supplementary Form.

Good to know

  • There is no sibling priority.
  • From 2028 entry the test changes to Quest Assessments.
  • Within every category, children receiving the Pupil Premium or Service Premium come first.
  • Without the Supplementary Form your child goes in the last category, whatever their faith.
