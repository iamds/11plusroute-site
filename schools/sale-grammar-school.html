Sale Grammar School

Sale

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must score 334 or more in the Trafford Consortium test. Qualifiers are then ranked: looked-after children, then up to 15 Pupil Premium children, then everyone in the priority area (M33, plus Trafford residents in M23, WA14 and WA15) by distance, then everyone else in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reach the qualifying score.
  2. 2(I). Pupil Premium, living in the priority admission area (within the 15 PP places) Up to 15 places go to Pupil Premium children. They go first to Pupil Premium children living in the priority admission area, nearest first.
  3. 2(II). Pupil Premium, at a Trafford primary, living in Trafford outside the priority area (within the 15 PP places) Any of the 15 Pupil Premium places left go to Pupil Premium children who attend a Trafford state primary and live in Trafford but outside the priority area, in score order.
  4. 3. Lives in the priority admission area Qualifying children living in M33, or living in Trafford with an M23, WA14 or WA15 postcode, nearest first.
  5. 4. Everyone else, in score order Qualifying children from outside the priority admission area, highest score first.

Good to know

  • A score of 334 or more qualifies, but doesn't guarantee a place.
  • If you move nearer after registering but before 1 March 2027, your original address is used until after first-round offers. Moving further away counts straight away.
  • Pupil Premium children get priority for up to 15 places; Service Premium children don't get priority.
  • Each consortium school gives its own standardised score, and results can't be shared with other schools after registration.
  • For September 2028 entry the consortium test changes to an FSCE test based on the KS2 curriculum, and from 2029 entry it moves to the summer term of Year 5.
