Shoeburyness High School

Southend-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A mixed comprehensive academy (SECAT) with a small selective intake. After looked-after children, up to 28 places go to children in the selective priority area who score 303+ in the CSSE 11+, highest first, and any left go to borderline scores of 295-302.99. Then come siblings, children of staff, the catchment area, six feeder primaries and everyone else by distance. Children who don't sit the test apply under the other criteria, and a child who sits it but misses a selective place is still considered under them.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care or previously in care (including adopted from state care abroad).
  2. 2. Selective places (CSSE 303+), priority area Up to 28 places for children scoring 303 or more in the CSSE 11+ who live in the selective priority area, highest scores first.
  3. 3. Borderline selective places (CSSE 295-302.99), priority area If any of the 28 selective places are left, the governors may offer borderline places to children who scored 295 to 302.99 and live in the selective priority area, highest scores first.
  4. 4. Siblings Children with a brother or sister at the school.
  5. 5. Children of staff Children of certain staff. New in this position for 2027 entry.
  6. 6. Living in the catchment area Children living in the school's catchment area.
  7. 7. Year 6 at a feeder primary Children in Year 6 at one of six feeder primaries.
  8. 8. Living outside the catchment area Everyone else, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Selective places are under-used: in 2026 only 6 selective and 7 borderline places were offered out of 28.
  • Borderline places go to children scoring 295 to 302.99 if selective places are left; 7-20 have been offered each year.
  • The priority area for selective places is much wider than the catchment area.
  • Ticking the selective box on Southend's form uses only one preference, and your child is still considered under the other criteria.
  • From 2027 entry siblings are one criterion wherever they live, and children of staff move up to criterion 5.
  • Free English and maths familiarisation papers can be downloaded from the CSSE website. Shop-bought practice books are at a similar level but not in the same format as the real papers.
