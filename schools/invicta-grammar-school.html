Invicta Grammar School

Maidstone

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must pass the Kent Test. There is no catchment and no extra credit for a high score. After EHCP and looked-after girls, places go to sisters, medical/social need, Pupil Premium girls, staff children, then everyone else by straight-line distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. b. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. c. Current family association (brother or sister at the school) A sister (natural, adopted, half, step or foster, or living as sisters in the same house; not cousins) attending Invicta when your daughter starts, living at the same address.
  3. d. Health and special access reasons Girls whose own (or whose parent's) health or social needs give a demonstrable and significant need to attend this school. The evidence must show it is the only school that can meet her needs.
  4. e. Pupil Premium Registered for free school meals at any point in the last six years (Universal Infant Free School Meals alone don't count). Return the school's Supplementary Information Form by 31 October and name Invicta on the SCAF.
  5. f. Children of staff Daughters of full-time staff with two years' continuous service, or staff recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  6. g. Nearness of home to school All other eligible girls, nearest first (straight line, measured by KCC).

Good to know

  • Invicta is not super-selective: any Kent Test pass is enough, and places then go mostly by distance.
  • There is no catchment area.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
