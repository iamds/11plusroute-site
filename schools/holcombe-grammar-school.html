Holcombe Grammar School

Chatham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test. If more grammar-assessed children apply than the 180 places, priority goes to looked-after children, then brothers and sisters, children at four Thinking Schools Academy Trust primaries, staff children and health reasons. Everyone else is ranked by distance. Year 7 has been mixed since September 2026.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked after and previously looked after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who are assessed as grammar come first. If this group alone fills the school, distance decides.
  2. 2. Brother or sister at the school Children with a brother or sister at Holcombe when they start. Twins/multiple births: if only one place is left, the other(s) are admitted above PAN.
  3. 3. Attends a Thinking Schools Academy Trust Medway primary Children at one of the trust's four Medway primary schools.
  4. 4. Children of staff Children of staff who have worked at Holcombe for at least two years, or who fill a skills-shortage post.
  5. 5. Health reasons Children with a specific health reason to attend this school, backed by a GP or consultant.
  6. 6. Nearest to the school All other grammar-assessed children, nearest first (Medway GIS route distance). Equal distances for the last place: both are admitted above PAN.

Good to know

  • Year 7 has taken girls since September 2026. Other year groups, apart from the sixth form, stay boys only.
  • In September 2026 the furthest place offered was about 23 miles away by route distance.
  • There is no alternative date for the Medway Test if it clashes with another test, and no resit.
  • Medway Council publishes a free familiarisation booklet showing the format and layout of the test papers.
