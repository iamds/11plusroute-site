Churston Ferrers Grammar School

Brixham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child sits the shared Torbay 11+ (GL English and maths papers) on 19 September 2026 and must reach Churston's Qualifying Score. Places then go to looked-after children, then anyone scoring at or above the 75th-ranked applicant, then children on Free School Meals / Pupil Premium / Service Pupil Premium, then siblings, then children of staff, then everyone else by score. Each group is ordered by score. Feeder-school priority was removed for 2027 after a Schools Adjudicator ruling.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reach the Qualifying Score.
  2. B. Highest qualifying children (top 75 scores) Anyone scoring at least as high as the 75th-ranked applicant. The ranking counts every applicant who named Churston at any preference, so many top scorers may take places at their first-preference school instead (in 2026, when the bar was the 50th-ranked child, 14 places went this way).
  3. C(a). Free School Meals, Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium Eligible children who qualify for FSM, Pupil Premium or Service Pupil Premium at the time of the test, in score order.
  4. C(b). Siblings Reinstated for 2027 after the Schools Adjudicator ruling. In score order.
  5. C(c). Children of staff In score order.
  6. D. All other eligible children by score Everyone else at or above the Qualifying Score, by Selection Test score.

Good to know

  • Priority for 15 named feeder primary schools was removed for 2027 after a Schools Adjudicator ruling, and sibling priority was reinstated.
  • For 2026 entry the top-scorer band reached only the 50th-ranked child and just 14 children got places that way, mostly because high scorers took places at their first-choice grammar. For 2027 it is the 75th.
  • The school plans to consult in December 2026 and January 2027 on arrangements for 2028 entry.
  • GL's free familiarisation material has sample questions; only the English and maths sections apply.
