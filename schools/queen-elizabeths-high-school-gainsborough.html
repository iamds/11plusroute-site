The Queen Elizabeth's High School, Gainsborough

Gainsborough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must reach 220 in the Lincolnshire 11+. If oversubscribed, priority goes to looked-after children, siblings and staff children, then 'local' children living 9 miles or less by road (nearest first). Next come Ever-6 free school meals children, then everyone further away in score order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A. Looked after / previously looked after children Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. B. Brother or sister at the school Qualified children with a brother or sister at QEHS when you apply.
  3. C. Children of staff Qualified children of staff employed at the school.
  4. D. Local children (9 miles or less by road) Qualified children whose home is 9 miles or less from the school by driving distance, nearest first. Ties for the last place go to the higher 11+ score, then the nearer home in a straight line, then a lottery.
  5. E. Ever 6 free school meals Qualified children registered as Ever 6 FSM when you apply.
  6. F. More than 9 miles, by score Qualified children living more than 9 miles away by road, highest 11+ score first. Ties go to the nearer home by road, then in a straight line, then a lottery.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area: local priority depends only on living 9 miles or less from the school by road.
  • More than 188 children qualify in most years, so qualifying doesn't guarantee a place.
  • Brothers and sisters in the same year group who all qualify are treated as one application, and the school may go over its PAN to admit them all.
  • The school runs two free familiarisation days (27 June and 4 July 2026) and says these are enough preparation.
  • Lincolnshire still treats applications made by 14 December 2026 as on time.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
