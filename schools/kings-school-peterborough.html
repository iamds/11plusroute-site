The King's School, Peterborough

Peterborough

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A Church of England comprehensive school (7-18) with no catchment area. 135 Year 7 places are open to new applicants, because 15 children move up from its Junior Department. Up to 120 go by faith (Church of England / Methodist worship scores, up to 74 places), staff, siblings, other faiths and distance. Up to 15 go by selection: 12 to the top scorers (at least 40/58) in the school's English, maths and verbal reasoning exam, and 3 to the best music auditions. Your child can apply under every category they qualify for, so children who don't sit the exam still apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. A2. Church of England / Methodist worshippers (up to 74 places) Children of worshipping Church of England, Church in Wales, Scottish Episcopal or Methodist parents, in order of worship mark. Weekly-plus attendance for more than 8 years scores the maximum of 45 (5 x 9). Fortnightly attendance for 3-4 years scores 8.
  3. A3. Children of staff Children of school staff who have worked there for at least two years, or who fill a skill-shortage post.
  4. A4. Siblings Children with a brother or sister at the school. Those whose sibling is in Years 7-11, or was there in Years 7-11 before the sixth form, come first. Then distance. A sibling place is not guaranteed.
  5. A5. Other Christian denominations and other major faiths Children of worshipping members of other Christian churches or of other major faiths, ranked by lottery.
  6. A6. All other applicants, by distance Everyone else, nearest first (straight line from the main school reception entrance). Historically, no places have been offered in this category.
  7. B1. Academic ability: top 12 in the entrance exam The 12 highest scorers who reach the pass mark of 40/58. Equal scores are split by distance. Unfilled places go to B2. If B1 and B2 together can't be filled, the extra places go to Group A criteria 3-6.
  8. B2. Musical ability: top 3 at audition The three children with the highest audition marks. Unfilled places go to B1, and then to Group A criteria 3-6.

Good to know

  • Historically no places have gone by distance alone, so most children get in through faith, staff, sibling or selection places.
  • About 15 of the 150 places go to children moving up from the school's Junior Department, leaving 135 for new applicants.
  • The school has been oversubscribed for at least 20 years.
  • The Church of England / Methodist SIF must cover at least the last eight years of worship.
