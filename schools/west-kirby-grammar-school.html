West Kirby Grammar School

Wirral

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

She must reach the Wirral Consortium 11+ standard (one test day in September, run for the four non-Catholic Wirral grammar schools). If more qualifying girls apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after girls, medical need, up to 18 girls on Free School Meals (nearest first), sisters and staff children. Of the places left, 60% go by distance and the rest by test score. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after girls, and girls previously looked after who were adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Valid medical reason Girls with a valid medical reason, backed by a doctor's letter and given on the preference form.
  3. 3. Free School Meals (up to 18 places) Up to 18 places for girls on targeted (low-income) Free School Meals when you apply who reach the qualifying score, nearest to the school first.
  4. 4. Sister at the school Girls with a sibling at the school when you apply (same parents, or a half, adopted or step-sibling living in the same household). The school has a mixed sixth form.
  5. 5. Children of staff Daughters of staff employed at the school for at least two years when you apply, or recruited to a post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  6. 6. 60% of remaining places by distance Of the places left after criteria 1-5, 60% go to the girls who live nearest the school (shortest road route to the nearest school gate). There is no catchment area.
  7. 7. Remaining places by test score The rest of the places go in order of age-standardised total score, highest first.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area: after the priority groups, 60% of places go by distance and 40% by test score.
  • Free School Meals priority needs the same qualifying score as everyone else.
  • In 2026 the last distance offered was 6.095 miles and the lowest score offered was 238.
  • In 2024 and 2025 every on-time applicant was offered a place.
  • Free Quest familiarisation booklets for each subject include a practice OMR answer sheet.
  • Pupil Premium children can get free Atom Home access for 11+ practice.
