Wallington County Grammar School

Wallington

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must pass the shared Sutton SET (English and maths, multiple choice) for Wallington County Grammar, then sit the shared written Second Stage. Their combined score (SET 2/5, Second Stage 3/5) must reach the school's qualifying standard. Looked-after boys and staff children come first, then the highest scorers from anywhere, up to 110 places in total. Up to 25 places then go to Pupil Premium boys and up to 15 to boys in the local Sutton postcodes, both in score order. Unused places go back to the open score list.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reach the combined qualifying standard.
  2. 2. Children of permanent staff Boys who reach the standard and whose parent has worked permanently at the school for at least two consecutive years, or was recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  3. 3. Entrance test rank (criteria 1-3 up to 110 places) The highest-scoring boys from any address, in rank order, until criteria 1 to 3 have filled 110 places.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium boys (up to 25 places) Up to 25 places for boys eligible for Pupil Premium through their current school on 31 October 2026, in score order. They must still reach the full qualifying standard.
  5. 5. Sutton postcodes (up to 15 places) Up to 15 places for the highest-scoring remaining boys whose permanent home on 31 October 2026 is in SM1-SM7 or the KT4 8, CR0 4 or CR4 4 sectors.

Good to know

  • Pupil Premium and local postcode places come after the main score list, so they go to boys who just missed out on rank.
  • Pupil Premium places have no address restriction.
  • Every boy must reach the same combined qualifying standard, with no lower bar for disadvantaged boys.
  • The local postcode area is SM1-SM7 plus only the KT4 8, CR0 4 and CR4 4 sectors.
