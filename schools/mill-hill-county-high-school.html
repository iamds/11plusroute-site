Mill Hill County High School

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Mill Hill is a comprehensive school that takes up to 60 of its 243 places by aptitude tests: 24 for technology (a GL verbal and non-verbal reasoning test), 24 for music and 12 for dance. All other places go to looked-after children, at least 90 by distance, then siblings and children of staff, with any places left over also going by distance. Children who don't sit an aptitude test can still apply for the non-selective places through the normal council application.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after children and previously looked-after children (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order), including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. 90 places by distance The 90 children living nearest the school (straight line to the Headteacher's Office). Any places left over at the end are also offered by distance.
  3. 3. Siblings All applicants with a brother or sister on the school roll at the closing date who lives at the same address are offered places. Twins and other multiple births are all admitted if one gets a place (counted as sibling places).
  4. 4. Children of staff Children of eligible staff. The Governors' Admissions Committee decides whether the staff member qualifies.
  5. 5a. 'Technological aptitude' places, ranked on a reasoning test (24 places) Places for the highest combined scores in the verbal and non-verbal reasoning test. Equal scores are separated by distance.
  6. 5b. Musical aptitude (24 places) Places for the highest musical aptitude scores. Grade exams are not needed or taken into account. Children offered a music place are expected to join at least one school music group, rehearsing weekly, until the end of Year 9.
  7. 5c. Dance aptitude (12 places) Places for the highest dance aptitude scores (contemporary dance). Children offered a dance place are expected to take part in the school's extra-curricular dance until the end of Year 9.

Good to know

  • The 24 'technological aptitude' places are decided by a GL verbal and non-verbal reasoning test, which works like a general ability test.
  • No supplementary information form is needed: book the aptitude tests on the school website and apply for the place through your home council.
  • If parents share care, the closer of the two addresses is used for distance.
  • Places left over after the aptitude places become extra distance places: in 2025 there were 118 distance offers (90 plus 28 extra).
  • You get your aptitude test scores before the council application deadline.
  • Example listening test questions are published in the school's admissions procedures document.
