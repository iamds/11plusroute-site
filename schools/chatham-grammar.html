Chatham Grammar

Chatham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your child must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test or the Kent Test (Chatham Grammar is the only Medway grammar that accepts the Kent Test). Year 7 is mixed: 150 places for boys and girls. Looked-after children and children with an EHCP naming the school are admitted first; then, if more qualified children apply than places, priority goes to brothers and sisters, then staff children, then distance (shortest safe walking route).

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. LAC. Looked after and previously looked after children (automatic admission) Admitted automatically, before criteria a-c, if assessed as grammar. This includes children who were in care but stopped being so because they were adopted, or became subject to a residence order or special guardianship order.
  2. a. Brother or sister at the school Grammar-assessed children with a brother or sister at Chatham Grammar when you apply who will still be there when your child starts. Whole and half siblings (by blood or adoption) count; step and foster siblings count if they live permanently with your child at the date of application.
  3. b. Children of staff Grammar-assessed children of staff employed at the school for at least a year when you apply, or recruited to a post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  4. c. Distance from home Everyone else assessed as grammar, nearest first, measured along the shortest available safe walking route by Medway Council's GIS.

Good to know

  • Chatham Grammar was a girls' school. From September 2027 boys join Years 7 and 8, rising by one year group each year to Year 11 in 2030.
  • In September 2026 the furthest distance offered was about 18 miles, so most qualified applicants who named the school got a place or a higher preference.
  • A brother or sister must be at the school both when you apply and when your child starts.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
  • Medway Council publishes a free familiarisation booklet showing the format and layout of the test papers.
