Denmark Road High School

Gloucester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Girls must reach the Qualifying Standard; girls living in Gloucester postcodes GL1-GL4 can also get in with the lower Priority Standard. Looked-after girls come first, then Pupil Premium girls (GL1-GL4 first) up to the national Pupil Premium percentage of places, then daughters of staff, then GL1-GL4 girls, then sisters of current pupils, then everyone else by test score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the Qualifying Standard.
  2. ii. Pupil Premium girls living in GL1-GL4 Pupil Premium girls with a GL1-GL4 postcode who reach the Qualifying Standard, by test score, up to the national Pupil Premium percentage of the PAN.
  3. iii. Pupil Premium girls outside GL1-GL4 (to top up the PP share) If criterion ii doesn't fill the Pupil Premium share, Pupil Premium girls living outside GL1-GL4 who reach the qualifying score are offered places, by test score, until the share is met.
  4. iv. Daughters of staff Girls who reach the Qualifying Standard whose parent/carer has worked at the school on a permanent contract for two or more years, or was recruited to a skill-shortage post.
  5. v. Girls living in GL1-GL4 (Qualifying or Priority Standard) Girls with a GL1, GL2, GL3 or GL4 postcode who reach either the Qualifying Standard or the lower Priority Standard. Within this group girls with a sibling at the school come first, then test score (changed from distance for 2027).
  6. vi. Girls with a sibling at the school Girls who reach the Qualifying Standard and have a sibling at the school, by test score.
  7. vii. All other qualifying girls by test score Everyone else who reaches the Qualifying Standard, by test score.

Good to know

  • Gloucestershire offers the highest-ranked school on your form that your daughter qualifies for, so only name Denmark Road if she met its standard.
  • There is no catchment area, and girls from outside Gloucestershire can apply.
  • For 2027, Pupil Premium places rise to the national Pupil Premium percentage, and GL1-GL4 girls are ranked by test score instead of distance.
  • From September 2028 entry the Gloucestershire grammar schools switch to an FSCE test on Saturday 3 July 2027 (end of Year 5), with registration from 8 February to 19 March 2027.
