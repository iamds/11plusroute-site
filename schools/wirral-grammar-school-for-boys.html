Wirral Grammar School for Boys

Wirral

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys must reach the Wirral 11+ standard (register with Wirral Council by 31 May in Year 5). Up to 30 places go first to looked-after boys and boys with Pupil or Service Premium, who may score up to 5 marks below the normal standard. Then come medical need, siblings, children of staff, and finally shortest distance from the school.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. A. Looked after, Pupil Premium or Service Premium (up to 30 places, lower score allowed) Up to 30 places for looked-after or previously looked-after boys, or boys with Pupil or Service Premium, who score at the eligible score or up to 5 marks below it.
  2. B. Medical reason Boys with a valid medical reason for this particular school. A senior health care professional's letter must explain why only this school meets his medical needs.
  3. C. Siblings Boys with a brother at the school when the application is made. Step, foster and adopted siblings and other children living permanently at the same address count.
  4. D. Children of staff Sons of staff who have worked at the school for at least two years, or who were recently recruited to a post with a demonstrable skills shortage.
  5. E. Shortest distance All other qualified boys, by shortest distance from home to school on Wirral Council's computer mapping system.

Good to know

  • Looked-after boys and boys with Pupil or Service Premium can get one of up to 30 places with a score up to 5 marks below the normal standard.
  • A higher score gives no extra priority once your son reaches the standard.
  • In 2026 the last place went on distance at 9.211 miles; in 2025 every on-time applicant was offered a place.
  • The assessment can be taken only once.
  • Free Quest familiarisation booklets for each subject include a practice OMR answer sheet.
  • Pupil Premium children can get free Atom Home access for 11+ practice.
