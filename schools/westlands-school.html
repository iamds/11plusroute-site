Westlands School

Sittingbourne

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

An all-ability academy in Sittingbourne, not a grammar school. Up to 29 places (10%) go to the children who score highest in the school's optional maths aptitude test, after looked-after children and those with medical or social need. The rest go to siblings, staff children, pupils at Westlands Primary and Regis Manor Primary, children in ten nearby parishes, and then by distance. Your child doesn't have to sit the test to apply under those criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Children in care, and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Medical, health, social and special access reasons Children whose own or whose parent's physical or mental health or social needs mean they need to attend this particular school.
  3. 3. Highest scorers in the maths aptitude test Up to 29 places go to the children who score highest in the maths aptitude test. Children who miss out can still get a place under the later criteria.
  4. 4. Brother or sister at the school Children who will have a brother or sister at the school when they start.
  5. 5. Children of staff Children of staff who have worked at the school for at least two years, or who fill a skill-shortage post.
  6. 6. Pupils at Westlands Primary or Regis Manor Primary Children in Year 6 at Westlands Primary School or Regis Manor Primary School.
  7. 7. Living in the named parishes Children living in Bobbing, Borden, Bredgar, Hartlip, Iwade, Lower Halstow, Newington, Stockbury, Tunstall or Upchurch parishes.
  8. 8. Nearness of home to school Everyone else, by straight-line distance, nearest first.

Important dates

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Friday 23 October 2026: Deadline to book the maths aptitude test (online form)
  • Monday 2 November 2026: Kent SCAF closing date
  • Saturday 7 November 2026: Maths aptitude test at Westlands School
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day (emails after 4pm)

Good to know

  • Westlands is an all-ability school and doesn't use the Kent Test.
  • Your child doesn't need to sit the maths test to apply for the other places.
  • The maths test is after the application deadline, so you apply without knowing the result.
