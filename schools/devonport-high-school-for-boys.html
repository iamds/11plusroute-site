Devonport High School for Boys

Plymouth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Plymouth 11+ (Quest English and maths papers on two Saturdays in September), plus DHSB's own English writing paper, which they must pass. Boys are ranked by their Quest score. After looked-after boys, 174 places go in rank order, then up to 6 to qualifying Pupil Premium boys, then the rest in rank order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. i. Looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after and previously looked-after boys who reach the qualifying marks.
  2. ii. Adopted from state care outside England Boys who appear to have been in state care outside England and left it by being adopted, if they reach the qualifying marks.
  3. iii. 174 places in rank order The next 174 qualified boys in rank order.
  4. iv. Up to 6 Pupil Premium boys Up to 6 qualified boys receiving the pupil premium when tested who weren't placed in the 174, taken in rank order. They only need the qualifying marks, not a top-180 score.
  5. v. Other qualified boys in rank order Any remaining places go to other qualified boys in rank order.

Good to know

  • The published admission number is 180, but the school admitted 210 boys for 2026 entry.
  • Rank order uses only the two Quest papers (averaged). The DHSB writing paper is pass/fail, and is also used as a tie-break.
  • The Pupil Premium priority covers pupil premium only; service premium counts only in the tie-break.
  • Free familiarisation sessions for Year 5 boys run in June, and the practice papers and mark schemes then go on the school website.
