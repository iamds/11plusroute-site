St Thomas More High School

Westcliff-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A Catholic boys' comprehensive (Assisi Catholic Trust) with a selective stream. Up to 30 places go to boys who pass the CSSE 11+ and live in SS0-SS9. The other places go first to Catholic looked-after boys, then baptised Catholics in ten local parishes, other baptised Catholics, boys at Assisi Trust primaries, other looked-after boys, catechumens and Eastern Christians, other Christians and everyone else. Within each group, brothers/sisters at St Thomas More or St Bernard's come first, then the nearest. Boys who don't sit the test apply under the faith criteria, and a boy who sits it but misses a selective place is still considered under them.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Selective places (CSSE 11+), SS0-SS9 residents Up to 30 places for boys who pass the CSSE 11+ and live in a postcode starting SS0 to SS9. Boys who don't get a selective place are still considered under criteria 2-9.
  2. 2. Catholic looked after / previously looked after boys Looked-after boys from Catholic families and boys adopted from care (including from state care abroad) by Catholic families.
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic boys living in the named parishes Baptised Catholic boys living in one of ten local parishes.
  4. 4. Other baptised Catholic boys All other baptised Catholic boys.
  5. 5. Boys at an Assisi Catholic Trust primary Boys (of any faith) attending one of the eight Assisi Catholic Trust primary schools.
  6. 6. Other looked after / previously looked after boys All other looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including adopted from state care abroad).
  7. 7. Catechumens and members of an Eastern Christian Church Boys preparing to become Catholics (catechumens) and members of Eastern Christian Churches, including Orthodox.
  8. 8. Boys of other Christian denominations Boys of other Christian denominations whose membership is confirmed by a minister.
  9. 9. Any other boys whose families want a Catholic education Any other boys whose families want a Catholic education for their son.

Good to know

  • In 2025 and 2026 the lowest selective score offered was 303, the CSSE minimum, so every boy in SS0-SS9 who passed and applied seems to have been offered a place.
  • 18 selective places were taken in 2026.
  • A boy who sits the 11+ but misses a selective place is still considered under the faith criteria.
  • Brothers or sisters at St Bernard's High School count as siblings, as well as those at St Thomas More.
  • Free English and maths familiarisation papers can be downloaded from the CSSE website. Shop-bought practice books are at a similar level but not in the same format as the real papers.
