Mayfield Grammar School, Gravesend

Gravesend

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls qualify by passing the Kent Test or, if that doesn't work out, the school's own optional 'Mayfield procedure' test (CEM computer test plus a marked English writing paper) on 12 September 2026. Places go to looked-after girls, then siblings, then girls living in the inner postcode area (Gravesend and nearby postcodes such as DA3, DA9-DA13 and ME3), then the outer area, then everyone else. In the two area criteria, girls on free school meals come first, then highest Kent Test score, then distance.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after girls An eligible looked-after girl, or a girl previously looked after who was then adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. b. Sister or brother at the school An eligible girl with a brother or sister attending the school when she starts (children living as siblings in the same house).
  3. c. Lives in the inner postcode area (FSM girls first, then by score) Girls whose permanent home is in DA2 6, DA2 8, DA3, DA4, DA9, DA10, DA11, DA12, DA13, ME3, TN15 7 or the postcodes TN15 6AR, 6AS and 6AT. Girls currently receiving free school meals come first; then places go by highest Kent Test aggregate, then distance.
  4. d. Lives in the outer postcode area (FSM girls first, then by score) Girls whose permanent home is in DA1, DA2 7, ME2, TN13, TN14 or TN15 (except the TN15 parts in criterion c). Free school meals girls first, then highest Kent Test aggregate, then distance.
  5. e. All other eligible girls, by score Girls living outside both postcode areas: highest Kent Test aggregate first, then distance. There is no free school meals priority in this criterion.

Good to know

  • The Mayfield test is a second chance for girls who don't pass the Kent Test, but she must take part in the Kent Test too.
  • The Mayfield test puts more weight on English, so results can differ from the Kent Test.
  • Free school meals priority means girls currently receiving free school meals, not the wider Pupil Premium group.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
  • CEM publishes no practice papers, but a familiarisation website shows the look and feel of the computer test.
