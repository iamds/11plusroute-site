Pate's Grammar School

Cheltenham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+), and places go by test rank. The usual pass is the top 250. Disadvantaged children only need to score within 15% of the 150th-ranked child. Places go first to looked-after children, then up to 15 'Priority Places' for Pupil Premium children and children from deprived Cheltenham postcodes. Next come children from Gloucestershire/GL postcodes, until 105 places are filled. At least 45 places are open to children from anywhere in the UK, in rank order.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Every looked-after, previously looked-after or internationally adopted previously looked-after child who reaches the qualifying standard (top 250, or within 15% of the 150th-ranked score) is offered a place. There is no limit on numbers.
  2. 2. Priority Places (Pupil Premium / deprived Cheltenham postcodes), max 15 Up to 15 places for disadvantaged children, filled in sub-group order I to V and by rank within each sub-group. Pupil Premium here means FSM at any point in the 6 years before test registration closed, or adopted from / left care, or looked after.
  3. 3. Non-Priority Places (Local): Gloucestershire or GL postcode Qualifying children who live in Gloucestershire or have any GL postcode, in rank order, until groups 1-3 together reach 105 places.
  4. 4. Non-Priority Places (UK wide), minimum 45 At least 45 places for qualifying children with a home postcode anywhere in the UK (local children included), in rank order.

Good to know

  • Siblings, feeder schools and distance (except as a tie-break) count for nothing: entry depends only on test rank and which group your child falls into.
  • Other test results, such as SATs, CATs or other areas' 11+ tests, are not considered.
  • Name Pate's high enough on your application to prefer it over any other school your child qualifies for.
  • Children from outside Gloucestershire compete for at least 45 UK-wide places and any local places left over. Any GL postcode, even outside the county, counts as local.
  • Places are split into up to 15 priority places, 90-105 local places and at least 45 UK-wide places.
  • The school may admit above its PAN to treat equally qualified children fairly.
