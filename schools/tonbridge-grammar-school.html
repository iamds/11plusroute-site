Tonbridge Grammar School

Tonbridge

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls need a grammar assessment from the Kent Test. Looked-after girls come first. Then 135 places go to girls living in Tonbridge & Malling, Tunbridge Wells or Sevenoaks and 30 to girls living elsewhere, each in Kent Test score order. A further 15 go to Pupil Premium girls who scored below the local cut-off, nearest first.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who name TGS and have a grammar assessment come before everyone else. Their places come out of the Area places.
  2. 2 Area. Area places: lives in Tonbridge & Malling, Tunbridge Wells or Sevenoaks Girls living in, and paying Council Tax to, Tonbridge & Malling, Tunbridge Wells or Sevenoaks, in order of combined Kent Test score. Equal scores are split by distance, then by drawing lots. If there aren't enough Area applicants by offer day, spare places go to the Trustee list.
  3. 2 Trustee. Trustee places: lives outside those three council areas Girls living outside the three council areas, in order of combined Kent Test score, then distance, then drawing lots. Spare places go to the Area list.
  4. 3. Pupil Premium (15 places) 15 places for grammar-assessed girls eligible for Pupil Premium who score below the Area cut-off, nearest to the school first. There's no address requirement. You must return the SIF by 31 October.

Good to know

  • Kent uses an equal-preference scheme: where you put TGS doesn't affect your chances at other schools.
  • Pupil Premium places are for girls registered for targeted free school meals, not 'expanded' free school meals.
  • Pupil Premium places have no address requirement and go to girls below the Area cut-off, nearest first.
  • First-round cut-offs in 2026 were 378 for Area places and 400 for Trustee places.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
