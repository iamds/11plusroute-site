St Bernard's High School

Westcliff-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A Catholic girls' comprehensive with a selective stream. Up to 30 places go to girls who pass the CSSE 11+ and live in SS0-SS9, in score order. The other places go first to Catholic looked-after girls, then Catholic girls at the eight Catholic feeder primaries, other Catholics, other looked-after girls, catechumens and Eastern Christians, other Christians, non-Catholic girls at the feeder primaries, other faiths and everyone else. Within each group, sisters/brothers at St Bernard's or St Thomas More come first, then the nearest. Girls who don't sit the test apply under the faith criteria, and a girl who sits it but misses a selective place is still considered under them.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Selective places (CSSE 11+), SS0-SS9 residents Up to 30 places for girls who pass the CSSE 11+ and live in a postcode starting SS0 to SS9, in order of their test performance. Girls who don't get a selective place are still considered under criteria 2-10.
  2. 2. Catholic looked after / previously looked after girls Catholic girls who are in care or were previously in care (including adopted from state care abroad).
  3. 3. Catholic girls at a Catholic feeder primary Catholic girls attending one of the eight Catholic feeder primaries.
  4. 4. Other Catholic girls All other Catholic girls.
  5. 5. Other looked after / previously looked after girls Girls who are in care or were previously in care, of any faith or none.
  6. 6. Catechumens and members of an Eastern Christian Church Girls preparing to become Catholics (catechumens) and members of Eastern Christian Churches.
  7. 7. Girls of other Christian denominations Girls of other Christian denominations.
  8. 8. Non-Catholic girls at a Catholic feeder primary Girls who may not be Catholic but attend one of the eight Catholic feeder primaries. New for 2027 entry.
  9. 9. Girls of other faiths Girls of other faiths whose membership is confirmed by a minister of religion or other religious leader.
  10. 10. Remaining applications All other girls, including those with no SIF.

Good to know

  • Very few selective places are taken up: none in 2026 and fewer than 5 in each of 2022-2025.
  • The lowest selective score offered for 2024 entry was 307.
  • A girl who sits the 11+ but misses a selective place is still considered under the faith criteria.
  • If you live near the edge of SS0-SS9, check with the school whether you qualify for a selective place.
  • Sisters or brothers at St Thomas More High School count as siblings, as well as those at St Bernard's.
  • Free English and maths familiarisation papers can be downloaded from the CSSE website. Shop-bought practice books are at a similar level but not in the same format as the real papers.
