Shirley High School

Croydon

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Not a grammar school. Shirley High is a comprehensive (all-ability) mixed school in Croydon with a performing arts specialism. Up to 20 places go to children who show an aptitude for dance, music and/or drama at a scholarship workshop. Before those come looked-after children, siblings and serious medical cases; after them, children of staff in shortage posts, then everyone else by distance. Children who don't apply for the scholarship still apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after children and children who were looked after until adopted or placed under a child arrangement or special guardianship order, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Siblings Children with a brother or sister at the school who will still be there when they start. Siblings come before medical cases and before the scholarship places at this school.
  3. 3. Serious medical reasons Children with serious medical grounds. The evidence has to go in with the application.
  4. 4. Performing Arts Scholarship (up to 20 places) Up to 20 places for children who show an aptitude in the school's specialism, performing arts. You must apply on the Scholarship Application Form by 5 October 2026 and attend the workshop on 10 October 2026. Scholars get extra performing-arts opportunities and trips.
  5. 5. Children of permanent staff in shortage posts Priority 'may be given' to children of permanent staff recruited to a post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  6. 6. Distance from school Everyone else, nearest first, measured in a straight line to the main entrance.

Important dates

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Thursday 1 October 2026: Open evening (5pm to 7.30pm)
  • Monday 5 October 2026: Performing Arts Scholarship Application Form deadline; open-morning week begins (by appointment)
  • Saturday 10 October 2026: Performing Arts Scholarship workshop
  • Saturday 31 October 2026: CAF deadline
  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day (scholarship offers made the same day)
  • Monday 15 March 2027: Accept or decline the place in writing
  • Wednesday 17 March 2027: Deadline to lodge an appeal with the Chair of Governors

Good to know

  • Shirley High has no academic test. The only selection is up to 20 Performing Arts Scholarship places.
  • Siblings come before medical cases and the scholarship places, which is an unusual order.
  • In 2026 places were offered to children living up to 6.4 miles away.
