St Clement Danes School

Chorleywood

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A mostly non-selective school. After looked-after children, siblings and staff children, up to 24 places go on the SW Herts Consortium music aptitude test and up to 24 on the consortium academic test, in score order. Everything left goes by straight-line distance, WD3 postcodes first. Children who don't sit the tests can still apply for the sibling, staff and distance places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Includes children adopted after being in state care outside England and Wales.
  2. 2. Siblings at the school No test needed. Sibling applicants don't usually sit the tests.
  3. 3. Children of staff Needs the school's SIF by 31 October 2026.
  4. 4. Musical aptitude (up to 10%, 24 places) Places in order of the round 2 performance score. In 2026, children who scored 44+ in round 1 were invited to round 2, and the lowest performance score offered was 39 (out of 45).
  5. 5. Academic ability (up to 10%, 24 places) Places strictly in descending order of the standardised test score, with no minimum. In 2026, 662 applied under this criterion for 24 places and the lowest score offered was 215.
  6. 6. Distance: WD3 postcodes Remaining places go to children living in a WD3 postcode, nearest first. In 2026 the furthest offer was 2,483 m (about 1.54 miles).
  7. 7. Distance: outside WD3 Any places still left go to children outside WD3, nearest first. None were offered in 2026.

Good to know

  • Selective places come after siblings and staff children, and each type is capped at 24 places.
  • In 2026, 662 children applied for the 24 academic places; the lowest score offered was 215.
  • In 2026 no places went to children living outside WD3.
  • The school will educate summer-born children who started Reception late outside their normal year group.
  • The consortium publishes free familiarisation papers for verbal reasoning and maths, and there is a short break between the two tests.
