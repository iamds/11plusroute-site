Burntwood School

London

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Burntwood is a partially selective girls' comprehensive. Up to 60 of its 240 places (25%) go to the girls with the highest total scores in the Wandsworth Year 6 Test (non-verbal plus verbal reasoning). The other 180 places ignore the test score and go to looked-after girls, then exceptional medical/social need, then sisters, then children of staff, then by distance. Your daughter can still get one of the 180 places without sitting the test. Every year from 2024 to 2026, every girl who applied for an open place was offered one or a higher preference.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. Category 1. Ability places: highest Wandsworth Year 6 Test scores (up to 60 places) Up to 60 girls with the highest total NVR + VR scores are offered places. Distance only splits equal scores.
  2. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Girls in public care, and girls who stopped being looked after because of adoption or a residence, child arrangements or special guardianship order, including girls adopted from state care outside England.
  3. 2. Exceptional medical or social need Without supporting letters by the closing date, the application isn't considered under this criterion.
  4. 3. Sisters at the school Girls not admitted under Category 1 who have a sibling attending Burntwood when you apply.
  5. 4. Children of staff At most one place per form of entry.
  6. 5. Everyone else, nearest first Remaining places by straight-line distance from home to school, nearest first.
