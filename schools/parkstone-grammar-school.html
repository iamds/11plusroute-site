Parkstone Grammar School

Poole

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the shared Bournemouth and Poole consortium test (GL maths, English and verbal reasoning) in late September. Girls who reach the required standard are ranked by test score within these groups: looked-after girls; Pupil Premium girls living in the Poole priority area; other girls in the priority area; Pupil Premium girls from outside it; then everyone else. The priority area is the historic Borough of Poole or postcodes BH12-BH18 and BH21 3.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Eligible looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. b. Pupil Premium, in the priority area Eligible girls in the historic Borough of Poole or Poole postcodes who are entitled to the Pupil Premium on 31 October 2026.
  3. c. Lives in the priority area Eligible girls living in the historic Borough of Poole or Poole postcodes, in score order.
  4. d. Pupil Premium, outside the priority area Eligible Pupil Premium girls living outside the priority area.
  5. e. Lives outside the priority area Other eligible girls living outside the priority area, in rank order of test score.

Good to know

  • The four Bournemouth and Poole grammar schools share one test, but each sets its own oversubscription criteria.
  • Every recent year, girls from outside the priority area got 30-40 places, so living outside Poole doesn't rule out a high-scoring girl.
  • Within any oversubscribed criterion, girls with higher scores come first.
  • The test provider changes to Quest Assessments for September 2028 entry (tests in September 2027).
  • GL Assessment publishes free 11+ familiarisation papers in maths, English and verbal reasoning. Answers go on a pre-printed answer sheet, and time limits are strict.
  • Children who have had free school meals in the last six years can get free online 11+ preparation through the school's partnership with Atom Learning.
