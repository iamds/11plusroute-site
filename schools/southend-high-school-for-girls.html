Southend High School for Girls

Southend-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (English and maths) and must pass the school's pass mark (in practice at least 303). After EHCP and looked-after girls, up to 179 places go to girls living in postcodes SS0-SS9 in score order, with up to 17 of these kept for girls on free school meals or the Pupil Premium. At least 45 places go to girls living elsewhere, in score order, with up to 4 of these kept for free school meals or Pupil Premium girls.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who pass Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who pass the selection tests, wherever they live.
  2. 2. Priority area (SS0-SS9): preferential consideration (FSM / Pupil Premium) Up to 17 places for girls living in SS0-SS9 who pass and qualify for preferential consideration, highest mark first.
  3. 3. Priority area (SS0-SS9): everyone else by mark Girls living in SS0-SS9 who pass, highest mark first. The category is full when its quota is reached, when there are no more passing priority-area girls who named the school, or when the PAN is reached.
  4. 4. Outside SS0-SS9: preferential consideration (FSM / Pupil Premium) Up to 4 places for girls living outside SS0-SS9 who pass and qualify for preferential consideration, highest mark first.
  5. 5. Outside SS0-SS9: everyone else by mark (at least 41 places) All remaining places up to 224 go to girls living outside SS0-SS9 who pass, highest mark first.

Good to know

  • 224 girls were offered places in both 2025 and 2026.
  • A girl living in SS0-SS9 who misses out on a priority-area place is not considered for the places for girls living outside SS0-SS9.
  • The lowest scores offered in 2026 were 303 inside SS0-SS9 and 318 outside.
  • Results arrive on 12 October, before the 31 October application deadline.
  • The published lowest scores leave out places given for preferential consideration and on appeal.
