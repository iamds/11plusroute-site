Altrincham Grammar School for Girls

Altrincham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the shared Trafford Grammar School Consortium test (GL Assessment: verbal, non-verbal and maths, two papers) on 14 September 2026. The pass mark is a total standardised score of 334, or 324 for looked-after girls and girls receiving Pupil Premium. Every girl who qualifies is treated equally, so a higher score doesn't help. If more girls qualify than there are 204 places, looked-after girls come first, then girls living within 8 miles of the school (Pupil Premium girls, then sisters, then everyone else by distance), then girls outside the 8 miles in the same order. No girl from outside the catchment area got a place for 2026, and the last offer was at 7.346 miles.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 4.1. Looked after / previously looked after girls (324+) Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad), wherever they live, with a total standardised score of 324 or more.
  2. 4.2. Pupil Premium girls in the catchment area (324+) Girls living within the 8-mile catchment area who receive Pupil Premium, with a score of 324 or more.
  3. 4.3. Sisters in the catchment area Girls living within the catchment area with a sister who will be at the school when they start.
  4. 4.4. Lives in the catchment area (within 8 miles) All other qualified girls living within the 8-mile catchment area, nearest first.
  5. 4.5. Pupil Premium girls outside the catchment area (324+) Girls living outside the catchment area who receive Pupil Premium, with a score of 324 or more, nearest first.
  6. 4.6. Sisters outside the catchment area Girls living outside the catchment area with a sister who will be at the school when they start, nearest first.
  7. 4.7. Lives outside the catchment area All other qualified girls living outside the catchment area, nearest first.

Good to know

  • For 2026 entry, 1470 girls sat the test (plus 49 at the March late test) and 376 reached the qualifying score.
  • For 2026 entry, all 210 offers went to girls in the catchment area. 26 appeals were heard and 1 succeeded.
  • Pupil Premium girls outside the catchment area rank above sisters outside the catchment area, and qualify with a score of 324.
  • The consortium test changes to the curriculum-based FSCE test for 2028 entry.
  • Bright Futures Educational Trust is consulting until 14 December 2026 on adding priority for designated Trust primary schools from September 2028.
  • A free familiarisation guide has practice questions and a sample answer sheet, and practice papers are on the GL website.
