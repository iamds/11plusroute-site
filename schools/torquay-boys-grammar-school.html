Torquay Boys' Grammar School

Torquay

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son sits the shared Torbay 11+ (GL English and maths papers) in September of Year 6. Boys who reach the school's pass threshold are offered places strictly in score order, highest first, until the 168 places are filled. Looked-after and Pupil Premium status only counts when boys tie on the same score for the last place.

Important dates

Add these dates to your calendar

  • Friday 4 September 2026: Test registration deadline (noon)

  • Monday 1 March 2027: National Offer Day

Good to know

  • Places go purely by score above the pass threshold: there are no catchment, feeder-school or sibling priorities.
  • Looked-after and Pupil Premium status only matter when boys tie on the same score for the last place.
  • Scores are never released, not even on offer day.
  • In 2026 every place was filled on National Offer Day.
  • Boys registered by 30 June are invited to a free familiarisation session at the school in July.
  • Pupil Premium boys get free Atom Learning resources and may be able to sit up to two free mock exams.
