Queen Mary's Grammar School

Walsall

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test (the West Midlands grammar schools' GL test, run locally by the Shropshire, Walsall and Wolverhampton consortium). Boys must reach a minimum qualifying score set by the governors each November (200 in recent years). Looked-after boys come first. Next come up to 54 Pupil Premium boys, with those at Walsall state primaries first. Then everyone else in score order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the qualifying score, in score order.
  2. b. Pupil Premium (up to 30% = 54 places), Walsall primaries first Up to 54 Pupil Premium boys who reach the qualifying score. Boys at Walsall state primaries come first. Others are still considered under criterion c on their score.
  3. c. All other boys by score Everyone else who reached the qualifying score, in score order, until 180 places are filled. Where he lives doesn't matter.

Good to know

  • Where you live doesn't matter: after looked-after and Pupil Premium boys, places go purely in score order.
  • The lowest score offered to boys not on Pupil Premium was 225 in 2026 and 224 in 2025.
  • Scores are on the West Midlands Grammar Schools scale (median 200). Birmingham reports the same test on its own scale, where 205 qualifies, so check which scale you're reading.
  • Pupil Premium evidence must arrive before test day; anything later is only considered after the main allocation.
  • The qualifying score is only confirmed in November, after the application deadline.
  • Pupil Premium children (boys and girls) can get free Atom Learning 11+ preparation through the school.
