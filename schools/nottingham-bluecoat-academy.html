Nottingham Bluecoat Academy

Nottingham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Not a grammar school. This is an all-ability Church of England academy on Aspley Lane (now called Bluecoat Aspley Academy). There is no academic test. Up to 18 places go to the top scorers in an optional written technology aptitude test. Other places are reserved for regular worshippers (Christian and other faiths), Bluecoat Primary and Church of England primary pupils. At least 44 go to siblings and then by distance. Children who don't sit the test still apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (adoption, child arrangements or special guardianship order), including those previously in state care outside England.
  2. b. Children of staff Children (biological, adopted or stepchildren in the same household) of staff at the academy.
  3. c. Christian worshippers (up to 82 places) Children whose family worships at a Church of England parish or another Christian church in Churches Together in England or the Evangelical Alliance. 'Immediate family' means one or both parents/carers.
  4. d. Bluecoat Primary Academy pupils (up to 10 places) Children attending Bluecoat Primary Academy.
  5. e. Church of England primary school pupils (up to 11 places) Children attending Church of England primary schools.
  6. f. Other world faiths (up to 15 places) Children whose family worships in a faith other than Christianity.
  7. g. Aptitude in technology (up to 18 places) Up to 18 places to the highest scorers in the technology aptitude test. Children placed this way follow the same curriculum as everyone else. Unfilled places go to h, and children not placed here are considered under h.
  8. h(i). Siblings (remaining places, at least 44) Of the remaining places (at least 44, plus any unfilled under c-g), siblings come first.
  9. h(ii). Distance (remaining places) Everyone else, nearest first.

  • It is not a grammar school: the only selection is up to 18 of 180 places (10%) for aptitude in technology.
  • A child not offered a place under the criterion they chose is considered again under the distance criterion.
