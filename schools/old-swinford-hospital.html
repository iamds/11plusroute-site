Old Swinford Hospital

Stourbridge

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A state boarding and day school (Church of England, mixed since 2021 in Year 7). This entry covers the 46 free day places, which have no test or interview. After looked-after children they go to pupils at three local CofE feeder primaries, then service-premium and pupil-premium children, siblings, staff children, and finally by straight-line distance. All selection is for the fee-paying flexi-boarding places: 48 by an online ability test and 16 by music or sport aptitude, each split equally between boys and girls. Those are shown here too, and tailored boarding is listed under additional routes. Children who don't sit the tests can still apply for day or tailored-boarding places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 9.6. Day: looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 9.7.1. Day: attending a feeder primary school Children attending one of the three Church of England feeder primaries, all within 1.5 miles, when the application is made. In recent years there hasn't been room for all feeder-school applicants.
  3. 9.7.2. Day: eligible for service (armed forces) premium Children eligible for the service pupil premium.
  4. 9.7.3. Day: eligible for pupil premium Children eligible for the pupil premium.
  5. 9.7.4. Day: siblings Children with a brother or sister at the school.
  6. 9.7.5. Day: children of staff Children of staff.
  7. 9.8. Day: by distance Everyone else, nearest first by straight line (in metres) to the main entrance on Heath Lane.
  8. 6.1. Flexi-boarding: academic ability (OSHOT rank) Fee-paying flexi-boarding places, offered by the council in rank order to families who named OSH. Flexi-boarders arrive from 7am, take meals and do prep at school, and stay overnight up to about four times a term. Offers usually go well below 24th place (to children ranked in the mid-40s in recent years), because some high scorers don't name OSH or take a different place type.
  9. 6.7. Flexi-boarding: music or sport aptitude Fee-paying flexi-boarding places for musical or sporting aptitude, offered in rank order.

Good to know

  • Old Swinford Hospital is fully co-educational: girls have joined Year 7 since 2021.
  • The 46 day places are free and have no test or interview.
  • Boarding fees cover boarding only, as education is free: in 2026/27, £14,205 a year for tailored boarding and £7,102.50 for flexi-boarding.
  • Academic flexi-boarding places have gone to children ranked in the mid-40s in recent years, well below 24th place.
  • The assessments are held in early October.
