Highsted Grammar School

Sittingbourne

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must pass either the Kent Test or the optional Highsted Test (she must also be registered for the Kent Test). Both routes count the same. After EHCP places, the order is looked-after girls, Pupil Premium girls living in ME9-ME12, sisters, then distance. Recent years have had spare places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, or who left care through adoption, a child arrangements order or a special guardianship order, including children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Pupil Premium (living in ME9-ME12) Registered for free school meals at any point in the last six years (Universal Infant Free School Meals alone don't count). Only girls living in postcode areas ME9, ME10, ME11 or ME12. Return the Supplementary Information Form with evidence by 30 October 2026 and name the school on the SCAF.
  3. c. Current family association (brother or sister at the school) A sister (or a brother in the sixth form) attending the school when your daughter starts: natural, adopted, step or foster siblings, or children living as siblings in the same house.
  4. d. Nearness of home to school All other eligible girls, nearest first (straight line, measured by KCC).

Good to know

  • A pass in the Highsted Test has equal status with a Kent Test pass.
  • Highsted has offered well below its 150 places in recent years, so every eligible applicant has been offered a place.
  • Pupil Premium priority applies only to girls living in ME9-ME12. Pupil Premium girls elsewhere rank by sibling and distance.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
