Homewood School

Tenterden

September 2025 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

An all-ability academy in Tenterden (Tenterden Schools Trust), not a grammar school. After looked-after children, 20% of places go to the top scorers in the school's own assessment, the 'Homewood Test' (5 December 2026 for 2027 entry). The rest go to siblings, then by distance. Your child doesn't have to sit the test: children who aren't assessed are considered under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after and previously looked after children Children in local authority care, and previously looked-after children, including those in care outside the UK before adoption.
  2. b. 20% by ability or aptitude (Homewood Test) The highest scorers in the school's assessment fill 20% of the intake. Children not selected (or not assessed) are considered under c) and d).
  3. c. Brother or sister at the school Children who will have a brother or sister at the school when they start. Twins and other multiple-birth siblings are all offered places, even if this takes the school over its PAN.
  4. d. All other children by distance Everyone else, by straight-line distance, nearest first.

Good to know

  • The Homewood Test is held after the SCAF deadline, so you apply before knowing the result.
  • Homewood doesn't use the Kent Test or a supplementary form.
  • Appeals for 2027 entry must be lodged by 13 April 2027 and are heard by 10 June 2027.
