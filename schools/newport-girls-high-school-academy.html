Newport Girls' High School Academy

Newport

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter sits the West Midlands Grammar Schools test and must reach the qualifying score (192 for 2027 entry). Places go first to looked-after girls, then up to 15 Pupil Premium girls at listed Telford & Wrekin primary schools (both need only 95% of the qualifying score, 183), then qualifying girls living in the Newport boundary area by score, then everyone else by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Stage One: looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) who score at least 95% of the qualifying score.
  2. 2. Stage Two: up to 15 Pupil Premium girls at listed Telford & Wrekin primaries Girls on Pupil Premium who attend one of the listed Telford & Wrekin primary schools at the time of the test, and who score at least 95% of the qualifying score.
  3. 3. Stage Three: girls living in the Newport boundary area Qualifying girls who live inside the Newport boundary area. If there are more than the places left, highest scores first.
  4. 4. Stage Four: all other qualifying girls by score Girls living anywhere else in the UK or abroad, in order of standardised score.

Good to know

  • The 15 Pupil Premium places are only for girls at the 55 listed Telford & Wrekin primary schools. Pupil Premium girls at other schools can still get in under Stages 3 and 4 with the full qualifying score.
  • Roughly one third of each recent intake have been girls from the Newport boundary area.
  • The test is two multiple-choice papers.
  • In 2026, 111 of the 120 places went to on-time first preferences.
  • GL publishes free sample materials online.
