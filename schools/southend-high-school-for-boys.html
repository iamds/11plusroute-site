Southend High School for Boys

Southend-on-Sea

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (English and maths) and must reach the school's pass mark (in practice at least 303). After EHCP and looked-after boys, up to 150 places go to boys living in postcodes SS0-SS9 in score order (15 of them first to boys on free school meals or the Pupil Premium), and the remaining places (30 if the local quota fills) go to boys living elsewhere in score order (3 of them first to boys on free school meals or the Pupil Premium).

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. (i) Looked-after and previously looked-after boys who pass Looked-after and previously looked-after boys who have passed the selection tests, wherever they live.
  2. 2. (ii) Priority area (SS0-SS9): preferential consideration (FSM / Pupil Premium) Boys living in SS0-SS9 who pass and are entitled to preferential consideration, highest mark first.
  3. 3. (ii) Priority area (SS0-SS9): everyone else by mark Other boys living in SS0-SS9 who pass, highest mark first, until the 150 priority-area places are filled. Boys in SS0-SS9 who miss out are not considered for the places for boys outside SS0-SS9.
  4. 4. (iii) Outside SS0-SS9: preferential consideration (FSM / Pupil Premium) Boys living outside SS0-SS9 who pass and are entitled to preferential consideration, highest mark first.
  5. 5. (iii) Outside SS0-SS9: everyone else by mark All remaining places up to the PAN of 180 go to boys living outside SS0-SS9 who pass, highest mark first.

Good to know

  • Results arrive on 12 October 2026, before the 31 October application deadline.
  • In 2026 the lowest score offered was 310 for boys living in SS0-SS9 and 346 for boys living elsewhere.
  • A boy living in SS0-SS9 who misses out on a priority-area place can't get one of the places for boys outside SS0-SS9.
  • Free school meals priority means 'targeted free school meals' at the time of test registration, and you must say so when you register for the test.
  • The waiting list is run by Southend City Council, not the school, until 21 August 2027.
