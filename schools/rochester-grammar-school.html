The Rochester Grammar School

Rochester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must be assessed as grammar in the Medway Test. If more grammar girls apply than the 205 places, priority goes to looked-after girls, then Pupil Premium/forces families (SIF needed), sisters, girls at four Thinking Schools Academy Trust primaries, staff children and health reasons. Everyone else is ranked by distance. Test score gives no priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked after and previously looked after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who are assessed as grammar come first. If this group alone fills the school, distance decides.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (FSM now or in last 6 years, or services families) Girls eligible for the Pupil Premium or Service Premium, in the order i-v (current FSM first, then FSM in the last six years, then forces families). Within each, nearest first. You must return the school's SIF.
  3. 3. Sister at the school Girls with a sister at RGS when she starts. Twins/multiple births: if only one place is left, the other(s) are admitted above PAN.
  4. 4. Attends a Thinking Schools Academy Trust Medway primary Girls at one of the trust's four Medway primary schools.
  5. 5. Children of staff Daughters of staff who have worked at RGS for at least two years, or who fill a skills-shortage post.
  6. 6. Health reasons Girls with a specific health reason to attend this school, backed by a GP or consultant.
  7. 7. Nearest to the school All other grammar-assessed girls, nearest first (Medway GIS route distance). Equal distances for the last place: both are admitted above PAN.

Good to know

  • In September 2026 the furthest distance offered was 18,311.80 m (about 11.4 miles) by route.
  • All Medway grammar schools are oversubscribed and places go mainly by distance. In 2026, 2,379 children from outside Medway registered for the test, 1,426 were assessed as grammar and only 184 got a Medway grammar place.
  • There is only one test date: no alternative for clashes, no resit and no remark.
  • If two girls live exactly the same distance away for the last place, both are admitted.
  • Medway Council publishes a free familiarisation booklet showing the format and layout of the test papers.
