Tunbridge Wells Girls' Grammar School

Tunbridge Wells

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must pass the Kent Test. There is no extra score bar for most places. 157 places go by priority: looked-after girls, then (for girls in the priority area: within 4 miles or in named West Kent parishes) Pupil Premium and sisters, then health reasons, daughters of staff, priority-area girls by distance, and everyone else by distance. The last 17 are Governors' Places for the highest Kent Test scorers left in the priority area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in local authority care, and children previously looked after (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, straight from care), including those who left state care outside England by being adopted.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium, living in the priority area Girls eligible for Pupil Premium who live in the criterion 6 area. Return the school's SIF with evidence by 31 October 2026 and name TWGGS on the SCAF. There is no cap on numbers.
  3. 3. Sister at the school, living in the priority area Girls in the criterion 6 area with a sister who will still be at TWGGS when she starts.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons Applied in line with the Equality Act 2010. Evidence must link the need to TWGGS specifically.
  5. 5. Daughters of staff Daughters of permanent staff with two years' service (or a skill-shortage post).
  6. 6. Lives within 4 miles or in a named parish Grammar-assessed girls living within 4 miles of the school (including East Sussex) or in one of the 39 named parishes. Distance decides who gets in.
  7. 7. Distance from home to school All other grammar-assessed girls, nearest first.
  8. 8. Governors' Places: highest scorers in the priority area (17 places) Once 157 places are filled under criteria 1-7, a line is drawn. All girls left who live in the criterion 6 area are re-ranked by Kent Test score, and the top 17 get Governors' Places.

Good to know

  • Distance is the main factor for most places; only the 17 Governors' Places go by score.
  • In 2026 the last distance offered on offer day was 1.961 miles, and the lowest Governors' Place score was 375.
  • Applicants from outside Kent are accepted, but may not get a first-round place and should think about the journey.
  • If the last place falls to a twin or other multiple-birth child, each of them is offered a place.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
