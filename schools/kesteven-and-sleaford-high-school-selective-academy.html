Kesteven and Sleaford High School Selective Academy

Sleaford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must score at least 220 in the Lincolnshire consortium 11+ (two GL reasoning papers). If more qualified children apply than there are places, looked-after children come first, then Pupil Premium children (free school meals in the last six years), then siblings at a Community Inclusive Trust secondary school, then everyone else by distance. Test score doesn't affect priority once qualified.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including children adopted from state care outside England) who have qualified.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Qualified children registered for free school meals at any point in the last six years. No cap on numbers.
  3. 3. Sibling at a Community Inclusive Trust secondary school Children with a sibling at any of the Community Inclusive Trust's secondary schools (which include KSHS and Carre's) at the time of admission.
  4. 4. Distance from home All other qualified children, nearest first.

Good to know

  • Pupil Premium priority has no cap on numbers.
  • A sibling at any Community Inclusive Trust secondary school, including Carre's Grammar School, counts for sibling priority.
  • For 2025 entry the school made 103 offers for 124 places.
  • Official familiarisation papers go to primary schools in the summer of Year 5. If you buy GL Assessment practice packs, choose the multiple-choice format.
  • The consortium gives free Atom Learning Home access to all Pupil Premium children.
