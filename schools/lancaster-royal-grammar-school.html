Lancaster Royal Grammar School

Lancaster

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys register with LRGS for its 11+ (GL Assessment: maths, English and verbal reasoning) and must reach a minimum level set each year. Qualifying boys are ranked by aggregate score. Looked-after boys come first, then boys living in the Lancaster district (Pupil Premium boys first), then boys in a wider 'residual' area around it (Pupil Premium boys, then staff sons, then the rest), and finally boys from further away. Looked-after and Pupil Premium boys can score up to 5% below the minimum. There are also 12 state boarding places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England) at or up to 5% below the minimum level.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium, living in the priority catchment area (City of Lancaster) Boys in the Lancaster district eligible for Pupil Premium through free school meals (at any time in the last six years) or for Service Pupil Premium, at or up to 5% below the minimum level.
  3. 3. Lives in the priority catchment area (City of Lancaster) Boys living in the City of Lancaster district who reach the minimum level. All boys from this area who reach the qualifying standard are offered places.
  4. 4. Pupil Premium, living in the residual catchment area Pupil Premium boys living in the residual catchment area, at or up to 5% below the minimum level.
  5. 5. Sons of permanent LRGS staff, living in the residual catchment area Sons of permanent staff who live in the residual catchment area and reach the minimum level.
  6. 6. Lives in the residual catchment area Other boys in the residual catchment area who reach the minimum level.
  7. 7. Lives outside the residual catchment area (day places) Day applicants from outside both catchment areas who reach the minimum level. They are admitted only if places remain after categories 1-6. Families whose main home is outside the catchment areas, or who are only in short-term rented or temporary accommodation inside them, are treated as category 7.

Good to know

  • Every boy living in the City of Lancaster district who reaches the qualifying standard is offered a place.
  • The City of Lancaster area is the whole district, including Morecambe, Heysham and Carnforth, not just the town.
  • If your son qualifies for more than one school you're offered the one you ranked highest, so put LRGS first if it's your top choice.
  • There is no distance tie-break. Exact ties for the last place are settled at random.
