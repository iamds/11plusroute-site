Dame Alice Owen's School

Potters Bar

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A comprehensive with two selective routes. Up to 65 places go by rank in the school's own two-stage entrance exam (GL verbal reasoning, then English and maths for the top 325), and up to 10 by a two-stage music aptitude test. Only children living in the Local Priority Areas (parts of Hertfordshire, Barnet, Enfield and all of Islington) or at school in Islington can sit the tests. The rest of the places go to looked-after children, the 22 children living nearest, siblings, staff children and then by distance. You don't need to sit a test to apply for those. At least 20 places in all go to Islington children.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. The 22 children living nearest the school The 22 children whose permanent home is nearest the school on the closing date (31 October 2026). A home moved to within 36 months while a previous home within 50 miles was kept is treated as temporary and refused.
  3. 3. Siblings Children with a sibling at the school. Twins and other multiple-birth children are all admitted if one of them gets a place under any criterion (counted as sibling places, above PAN if needed).
  4. 4. Musical aptitude (up to 10 places) Up to 10 places in rank order of combined music test marks.
  5. 5. Academic ability (up to 65 places) Up to 65 places in rank order of total combined mark. Equal totals are split by verbal reasoning, then English, then maths.
  6. 6. Children of staff Children of staff. Ties are broken by the parent's length of service.
  7. 7. Everyone else, nearest first Remaining places go to the children living nearest the school. In practice no places, or very few, are left (0 in 2026, 4 in 2025).

Good to know

  • In 2026 the 22 places for children living nearest went to homes within about 338 metres (0.21 miles) of the school.
  • In 2026 academic places went up to ranked position 107 and music places up to position 12.
  • For 2027 entry, 1,058 children registered for the test; 969 sat the verbal reasoning round and 325 went on to English and maths.
  • Islington children: the lowest academic rank offered by 1 September was 17 in 2026 and 30 in 2025 (7 and 14 children admitted on the academic criterion).
  • Address checks for the 22 nearest places are strict: a home moved to within 36 months of the closing date while an earlier, further home (within 50 miles) is kept is treated as temporary and refused, even if the earlier home is let out.
  • Warning signs in address checks include a home more than 3 miles from the primary school, or low energy use.
  • Moving further away after an offer under criterion 2 or 7 normally means the place is withdrawn.
  • Brothers and sisters can't sit the tests: they are always placed under the sibling criterion first.
  • Free familiarisation papers for verbal reasoning, English and maths are on the school website; extra English and maths papers can be bought.
