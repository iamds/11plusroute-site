The Winston Churchill School

Woking

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

An all-ability 11-16 school in Woking, not a grammar school. Children who live outside its admission area can apply for up to 38 'specialist' places: up to 23 for those who reach the pass mark in the school's academic test and up to 15 by music audition. Most places go to siblings, children with exceptional need, service and staff children, and then children inside the admission area by distance. Your child doesn't have to sit any test to apply under those criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after and previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Siblings at the school Children with a sibling at the school when you apply.
  3. 3. Major medical, social, compassionate or family need Exceptional medical or social need, with written professional evidence sent with the application.
  4. 4a. Specialist academic places (outside the admission area) Children living outside the admission area who reach the threshold in the academic test. If more qualify than there are places, those living nearest come first. They are not ranked by score. Children who don't get one are still considered under the later criteria.
  5. 4b. Specialist music places (outside the admission area) Children living outside the admission area with an aptitude for music, judged at an audition. If more qualify, the nearest come first.
  6. 5. Service Children in Education Premium Children of serving UK regular armed forces personnel, or of parents who died in service and get an Armed Forces Compensation Scheme or War Pension Scheme pension.
  7. 6. Children of staff Children of staff who have worked at the school for over two years, or who fill a skill-shortage post.
  8. 7. Living in the admission area Children living inside the admission area shown on the school's map, nearest first.
  9. 8. Living outside the admission area Everyone else, nearest first.

Good to know

  • This is an all-ability school; the test and audition only matter for children living outside the admission area.
  • Few specialist places are taken up: 14 in 2024, 13 in 2025 and 7 in 2026, out of 41.
  • Academic specialist places go to the nearest children who reach the threshold, not to the highest scorers.
  • The school has no sixth form (it is 11-16).
