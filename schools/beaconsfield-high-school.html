Beaconsfield High School

Beaconsfield

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after girls and Pupil Premium girls in catchment (plus up to 10 places for catchment Pupil Premium girls scoring 115+), priority goes to catchment sisters, staff daughters, then all catchment girls by distance. Sisters outside catchment and then other girls by distance come after.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium / Service Premium in catchment, qualified Qualified girls in catchment entitled to Pupil Premium or Service Premium at the time of application.
  3. 3. Up to 10 places: catchment Pupil Premium girls scoring 115-120 Up to 10 places for catchment girls entitled to Pupil Premium/Service Premium who scored at least 115 but did not qualify. If more apply, the ten nearest get places. Places not applied for by 31 October 2026 pass to the next rule.
  4. 4. Sisters in catchment Qualified girls in catchment with a sister at the school.
  5. 5. Daughters of staff Qualified daughters of eligible staff.
  6. 6. Catchment, by distance Qualified girls living in catchment (by 1 September 2026), nearest first.
  7. 7. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified girls with exceptional medical or social needs.
  8. 8. Sisters outside catchment Qualified girls outside catchment with a sister at the school, nearest first.
  9. 9. Girls outside catchment, by distance Remaining places to other qualified girls, nearest first.

Good to know

  • The admission number rose from 180 to 186 for 2027 entry.
  • Medical or social need comes after all catchment girls in the priority order, which is unusual.
  • A sister only counts if she is in Years 7-11 at the time of application (October 2026), not the sixth form.
