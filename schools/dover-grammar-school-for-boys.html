Dover Grammar School for Boys

Dover

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son qualifies by passing either the Kent Test or the school's own Dover Boys Test (he can sit both; passing one is enough). If more qualified boys apply than the 150 places, priority goes to looked-after boys, then Pupil Premium boys, siblings, health/special access needs and staff children, then distance. In recent years all eligible boys who preferred the school have been offered places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Children in care, and children previously in care (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order straight after care), including those adopted from state care outside England. Parents must still name the school on the application.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium Boys registered for (targeted) free school meals at any time in the last 6 years. Send the school's form with evidence by 31 October 2026 and name DGSB on your application.
  3. 3. Brother or sister at the school A brother or sister (including adopted, step- and foster siblings living in the same house) already at the school when your son starts.
  4. 4. Health and special access reasons Applied in line with the Equality Act 2010. You need written professional evidence linking the need to DGSB specifically.
  5. 5. Children of staff Children of staff employed at the school for two or more years when you apply, and/or recruited to fill a vacant post with a demonstrable skill shortage.
  6. 6. Distance from home to school Everyone else, nearest first, by straight-line distance from home to school.

Good to know

  • In 2024-2026 the school offered 155-160 places against an admission number of 150.
  • Passing both tests gives no advantage.
  • The Dover Boys Test is paper-based and needs no extra preparation; registered boys get a link to familiarisation material.
  • Pupil Premium priority means 'targeted' free school meals; eligibility only under the newer 'expanded' free school meals doesn't count.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
