Boston High School

Boston

September 2026 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your daughter must reach the Lincolnshire consortium 11+ qualifying standard (a total of 220 across verbal and non-verbal reasoning). If more qualified girls apply than there are places, priority goes to looked-after children, then Pupil Premium girls (free school meals in the last six years), then sisters and brothers of current pupils, then distance. The school has always been able to take every girl who qualified.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reached the standard. Includes children adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (free school meals in the last six years) Qualified girls registered for Pupil Premium, i.e. registered for free school meals at any time in the last six years.
  3. 3. Brother or sister at the school Qualified girls with a brother or sister at the school when she starts.
  4. 4. Everyone else by distance All other qualified girls, nearest home first (straight line).

Good to know

  • For September 2026 the school made 119 offers against an admission number of 108.
  • If twins (or two siblings in the same year) both qualify and only one place is left, they are considered together and the school can go one over its admission number.
  • The Designated Transport Area decides where girls sit the test and whether the council pays for transport. It doesn't affect admission.
  • Girls can join in later years: those who qualified in the Lincolnshire 11+ at the governors' discretion, others by sitting CAT4 tests (75th national percentile or above, from Year 8).
