Wallington High School for Girls

Wallington

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must pass the shared Sutton SET and then both papers of the written Nonsuch and Wallington Second Stage (NWSSEE). Looked-after and Pupil Premium girls may score up to 20% below each pass mark. After EHCP and looked-after girls, 100 places go to the highest scorers from anywhere. Up to 35 go to Pupil Premium girls by score, and up to 110 (less EHCP, looked-after and Pupil Premium places) to the highest scorers living within 6.7 km of the school. Any remaining places go by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care outside England) who reach the pass marks or are up to 20% below each of them. Evidence must arrive before the SET. A girl who becomes looked after later, or whose evidence was late, can join the ranked looked-after waiting list after the initial allocation.
  2. 2. 100 places by score, irrespective of address 100 places for the highest total scores, wherever she lives. This comes before the Pupil Premium and catchment criteria.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium girls (up to 35 places) Up to 35 places for girls registered as Pupil Premium at their primary school, in score order, wherever they live. They may be up to 20% below each pass mark. Universal Credit free school meals alone don't count unless she is registered as Pupil Premium. A girl registered later, or whose form was late, can join the ranked Pupil Premium waiting list after the initial allocation.
  4. 4. Catchment: within 6.7 km (up to 110 places, less EHCP, 1 and 3) The highest-scoring girls whose permanent home on 31 October 2026 is within 6.7 km (4.16 miles) in a straight line of the main entrance to the school building.
  5. 5. Remaining places by score Any places left after criterion 4, up to the PAN of 210, go in order of highest score from any address, continuing the criterion 2 ranking.

Good to know

  • The 100 score-only places are filled before the Pupil Premium and catchment places, so catchment girls who miss those then compete only with other catchment girls.
  • Looked-after and Pupil Premium girls get both a lower bar (up to 20% below each pass mark) and priority, but the evidence must come from the primary school before the SET.
  • One registration and one set of results covers both Wallington High School for Girls and Nonsuch High School for Girls.
  • The SET can be sat only once.
  • Sample SET questions for English and maths are published, but there are no specimen papers.
