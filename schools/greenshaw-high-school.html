Greenshaw High School

Sutton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Not a grammar school. Greenshaw is a large comprehensive (all-ability) mixed school in Sutton that offers up to 60 'ability' places through the Sutton Selective Eligibility Test (SET), in score order to children who reach Greenshaw's pass mark. These come after looked-after children, exceptional medical/social cases and staff children. After that come siblings, then everyone else by distance (recently out to about 0.7 miles). Children who don't sit the SET still apply under the other criteria.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after children, and children who were looked after until adopted or placed under a child arrangements or special guardianship order, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Exceptional medical / social need Children with exceptional medical or social grounds for this school in particular. The Trust's Head of Admissions and the Headteacher decide jointly.
  3. 3. Children of permanent staff Children of permanent staff with two or more years' service, or recruited to a shortage post.
  4. 4. Academic ability (SET), up to 60 places Up to 60 places go to children who sat the SET and reached Greenshaw's pass mark, in rank order of their performance. Every year more children pass than there are places, so passing doesn't guarantee an offer.
  5. 5. Siblings Children who will have a brother or sister at the school. Cousins and friends' children living in the same household don't count.
  6. 6. Distance All remaining places, nearest first. In 2024 to 2026 the furthest offer was 1,108 to 1,242 metres (about 0.7 to 0.8 miles).

Good to know

  • Greenshaw uses only the first-stage SET. There is no second-stage test, unlike at the Sutton grammar schools.
  • Every year more children pass Greenshaw's SET mark than there are ability places.
  • In 2024 to 2026 the furthest distance offered was between 1,108 and 1,242 metres (about 0.7 to 0.8 miles).
  • Children who don't sit the SET can still apply under the other criteria.
  • Sample SET questions for English and maths are published, but there are no specimen papers.
