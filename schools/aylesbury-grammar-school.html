Aylesbury Grammar School

Aylesbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son needs 121 or more in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review). Test score gives no further priority. Among qualified boys, priority goes to looked-after boys, then Pupil Premium boys in catchment, siblings (including sisters at Aylesbury High School), staff children, medical/social need, catchment, then distance. Up to 6 places go to looked-after or catchment Pupil Premium boys scoring 115-120.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after boys Qualified looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England).
  2. 2. Up to 6 places: looked-after boys, or Pupil Premium boys in catchment, scoring 115-120 A route for boys who did NOT qualify. Up to 6 places go to boys scoring 115-120 who are either looked after / previously looked after (from anywhere) or on Pupil Premium and living in catchment. Looked-after boys come first, then nearest first.
  3. 3. Pupil Premium boys living in catchment Qualified boys on Pupil Premium who have lived in the catchment area since 1 September 2026.
  4. 4. Brothers/sisters at Aylesbury Grammar School Qualified boys with a sibling who will be at Aylesbury Grammar School when they start.
  5. 5. Sisters/brothers at Aylesbury High School (linked school) Qualified boys whose sister will be at Aylesbury High School when they start.
  6. 6. Siblings of former AGS pupils Qualified boys whose brother used to attend Aylesbury Grammar School.
  7. 7. Children of staff Qualified sons of staff with at least two years' permanent employment (or recruited to a skills-shortage post).
  8. 8. Exceptional medical or social needs Qualified boys whose exceptional medical or social needs can only be met at AGS, backed by professional evidence.
  9. 9. Lives in catchment Qualified boys living in the catchment area since 1 September 2026.
  10. 10. All other boys, nearest first All other qualified boys, by straight-line distance from the school.

Good to know

  • If one twin, triplet or same-year sibling gets a place, all are offered places, as long as they qualify.
