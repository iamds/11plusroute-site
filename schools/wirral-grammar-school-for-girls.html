Wirral Grammar School for Girls

Wirral

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls must reach the Wirral 11+ standard (register with Wirral Council by 31 May in Year 5). After girls whose EHCP names the school, priority goes to looked-after girls, then medical need, then up to 15 girls on Free School Meals (by distance), then sisters, then daughters of staff, then shortest road distance. A higher test score gives no extra priority.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls, including girls adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Medical reason Girls with a valid medical reason supported by a doctor's letter.
  3. 3. Free School Meals (up to 15 places) Up to 15 places for girls eligible for (Targeted) Free School Meals when the application is made, nearest first.
  4. 4. Sisters Girls with a sister at the school when applying who is expected to still be there at the start of the new year. Half- and step-sisters count if they live in the same household.
  5. 5. Daughters of staff Daughters of staff employed for at least two years and/or recruited to fill a post with a demonstrable skills shortage.
  6. 6. Distance All other qualified girls, nearest first, by shortest road route from home to the school gate nearest her home (or a footpath the school considers a safe walking route).

Good to know

  • In each of the last three years places went down to the distance criterion, with the last offers between 6.8 and 7.8 miles.
  • A higher test score gives no extra priority once your daughter reaches the standard.
  • Tell the council about any change in Free School Meals eligibility before 31 October 2026.
  • A girl who doesn't reach the standard in Year 6 can't be re-assessed until September of Year 7.
