Ermysted's Grammar School

Skipton

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son sits the school's own FSCE test: two papers of English and maths on one Saturday morning (26 September 2026). Boys who reach the 'required standard', which is set each year from how that year's candidates perform, can be considered. If more qualify than there are places, looked-after boys come first, then boys living in the catchment area, then everyone else. Within each of the last two groups, Pupil Premium boys come first, then boys with a brother at the school, then the nearest. Test scores are not used to rank qualifiers.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys who reach the standard, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Lives in the catchment area Qualifying boys whose home is in the catchment area when they register for the test: Pupil Premium boys first, then brothers of current pupils, then the nearest.
  3. 3. Lives outside the catchment area All other qualifying boys, ordered the same way. Boys whose parents don't return a complete SIF with evidence are placed at the very end of this group (sub-category (iii), by distance only).

Good to know

  • Ermysted's and Skipton Girls' High School test on the same Saturday with FSCE papers, but each runs its own test with its own registration and required standard.
  • Register from your current address and send proof of any move before 27 November 2026.
  • A Pupil Premium boy outside the catchment still ranks behind every qualifying catchment boy.
  • Free familiarisation guides are on the school website, and Pupil Premium boys can get free extra support through Atom Learning.
