Dr Challoner's Grammar School

Amersham

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Your son needs 121 in the Buckinghamshire Secondary Transfer Test (or a successful Selection Review); the score above that doesn't matter. After looked-after boys and Pupil Premium boys in catchment, priority goes to catchment siblings, then up to 12 places for catchment Pupil Premium/looked-after boys scoring 115+ (or 110-114), sons of staff, and then the rest of the catchment by distance. You must complete the school's SIF to count as in catchment, and you must have lived there since 1 April 2026.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care outside England) who qualify.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium in catchment Qualified boys living in catchment who receive Pupil Premium.
  3. 3. Siblings in catchment Qualified boys in catchment with a brother at the school.
  4. 4. Up to 12 places: catchment Pupil Premium / looked-after boys scoring 115+ (or 110-114) Up to 12 places for catchment boys receiving Pupil Premium (or looked after) who scored at least 115. If these aren't all taken, up to 6 can go to such boys scoring 110-114. Qualified boys (121+) in this group are already placed under rules 1-2.
  5. 5. Sons of staff Qualified sons of eligible staff.
  6. 6. Catchment, by distance Qualified boys living in catchment (continuously since 1 April 2026), nearest first. In some years not every catchment boy has got a place.
  7. 7. Siblings outside catchment Qualified boys outside catchment with a brother at the school, nearest first.
  8. 8. Everyone else by distance Remaining places to other qualified boys, nearest first.

Good to know

  • You must have lived in catchment since 1 April 2026, earlier than the 1 September date most Bucks grammar schools use.
  • DCGS is the only school in its group that needs a Supplementary Information Form. Without it, catchment priority can't be confirmed.
  • Catchment Pupil Premium or looked-after boys can get in with a score of 110-120 under rule 4.
  • About 500 qualified boys list DCGS each year and about 200 put it first.
