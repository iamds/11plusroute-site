Upton Hall School FCJ

Wirral

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

She sits Upton Hall's own entrance exam (verbal reasoning and English) in September and must reach the Qualifying Standard, including a minimum in each paper. Places then go in faith-based order: looked-after girls first, then baptised Catholic girls (those on Free School Meals first), staff daughters, then non-Catholic girls (FSM first). Within each group the highest exam scores win. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (adopted, or under a child arrangements or special guardianship order), including those adopted from state care abroad. Faith is not required.
  2. 2. Baptised Catholic girls on Free School Meals Baptised Catholic girls (or girls baptised Christian and since received into the Catholic Church) who are eligible for Free School Meals when you apply.
  3. 3. Baptised Catholic girls All other baptised Catholic girls. No Mass attendance or priest's reference is required, only proof of baptism.
  4. 4. Children of staff Daughters of staff employed by the school for at least two years.
  5. 5. Non-Catholic girls on Free School Meals Girls not baptised Catholic whose parents want a Catholic education for them and who are eligible for Free School Meals when you apply.
  6. 6. Other girls (non-Catholic) by exam score Girls not baptised Catholic whose parents want a Catholic education for them, in exam score order. This is the category where the last places went in 2025 and 2026.

Good to know

  • For Catholic priority you only need proof of Catholic baptism: there is no Mass-attendance test or priest's form.
  • Non-Catholic girls can get places: in 2025 and 2026 the last places went to non-Catholic girls by exam score.
  • Upton Hall's exam is separate from the Wirral 11+, so register with the school, not Wirral.
  • The Qualifying Standard and the minimum marks for each paper aren't published.
