Devonport High School for Girls

Plymouth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the shared Plymouth 11+ (Quest Assessments English and maths papers on two Saturdays in September) at either DHSG or Plymouth High School for Girls (PHSG). Places go in score order. Looked-after girls, and up to 12 girls on free school meals or Ever 6 FSM, can get in with a lower score (5 points below the 'adjusted cut off'). There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Looked-after and previously looked-after girls (including those adopted from state care abroad) scoring no more than 5 points below the adjusted cut off score, in score order.
  2. 2. Free school meals pupil premium or Ever 6 FSM (up to 12 places) Up to 12 girls on free school meals or Ever 6 FSM who score no more than 5 points below the adjusted cut off score, in score order. Without the SIF evidence she isn't treated as eligible.
  3. 3. All other girls by score Everyone else, in order of 11-plus score. The score of the last girl offered a place here is the school's 'cut off score'.

Good to know

  • There is no fixed pass mark and no sibling, catchment or distance priority. Distance only breaks ties.
  • Plymouth's 11+ moved to Quest Assessments for the September 2026 test, so older practice material (GL/CEM, free writing) no longer applies.
  • Allocation results come out on Monday 1 March 2027.
  • One free school meals form covers both DHSG and PHSG.
  • An optional practice 11+ in July lets children try the test and venue; its result doesn't count. Children eligible for free school meals in the last six years get free Atom Learning 11+ practice.
