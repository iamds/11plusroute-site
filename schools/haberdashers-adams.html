Haberdashers' Adams

Newport

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Co-educational in Year 7 since 2024 (boarding for boys only). Your child sits the West Midlands Grammar Schools test and must reach the entry requirement score (205 for 2027 entry, 195 for Pupil Premium). Day places go to looked-after children, then Pupil Premium/Service Premium children, then children at 36 listed Telford & Wrekin primary schools, then everyone else by score.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the required standard.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium or Service Premium Children recorded as getting Pupil Premium or Service Premium in the October 2025 school census, with a score no more than 5% below the qualifying score. The Pupil Premium Form must be in by the registration deadline.
  3. 3. On roll at one of 36 designated Telford & Wrekin primary schools Children on roll at a designated primary school (all TF10 primaries and others across Telford & Wrekin) by the registration deadline (26 June 2026). If there are more than places, by test score. Children who move from a designated school after the deadline are treated as at their new school.
  4. 4. Everyone else by test score All other children who reach the required standard, in rank order of test score.

Good to know

  • Year 7 has been co-educational since September 2024. Boarding is for boys only.
  • There are 135 day places, plus up to 15 boys' boarding places, so 150 places in all.
  • The 5% score leeway applies to Pupil Premium and Service Premium children, not to looked-after children.
  • Adams has no catchment area. Its local priority is the 36 designated Telford & Wrekin primary schools.
  • Tick SWW when you register for the test, or your child won't be considered for Adams.
