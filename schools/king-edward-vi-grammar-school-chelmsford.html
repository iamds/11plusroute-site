King Edward VI Grammar School, Chelmsford (KEGS)

Chelmsford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Essex CSSE 11+ (one English paper and one maths paper). After EHCP places, 120 places go to boys living within 12.5 miles of the school, in order of score. Up to 8 of those are kept for Pupil Premium or looked-after boys scoring 330+. The remaining places (about 30) go to the highest scorers wherever they live, with up to 2 kept for Pupil Premium or looked-after boys scoring 350+. No offers go to anyone scoring below 303.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Priority area: Pupil Premium or looked-after boys scoring 330+ Up to 8 of the 120 priority-area places go to the highest-scoring Pupil Premium or looked-after boys who live in the priority area and score at least 330.
  2. 2. Priority area: everyone else by score, until 120 priority-area places are filled The remaining priority-area places go to boys living within 12.5 miles, highest score first, until 120 priority-area boys have places (the 120 includes criterion 1). The offer depends on living continuously in the area from 10 October in Year 6 until starting at KEGS.
  3. 3. Any address: Pupil Premium or looked-after boys scoring 350+ Of the places open to all addresses, up to 2 go to the highest-scoring Pupil Premium or looked-after boys who score at least 350, wherever they live.
  4. 4. Any address: everyone else by score All remaining places (the rest of the 150 after EHCP places, the 120 priority-area places and criterion 3) go to the highest scorers, wherever they live. Priority-area boys who missed out in criteria 1-2 compete here too.

Good to know

  • You must have lived in the priority area since 10 October in Year 6 (it used to be 31 October).
  • A priority-area boy who misses the top 120 can still get one of the places open to all if his score is high enough.
  • Places open to boys outside the 12.5-mile area number roughly 30 minus EHCP admissions.
  • For 2026 entry the lowest scores offered were 353 inside the priority area and 370 outside. These exclude Pupil Premium/looked-after and appeal places.
  • Free English and maths familiarisation papers can be downloaded from the CSSE website. Shop-bought practice books are at a similar level but not in the same format as the real papers.
