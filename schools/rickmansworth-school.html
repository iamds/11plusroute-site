Rickmansworth School

Rickmansworth

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A comprehensive with selective places. Up to 60 places (25%) go by rank in the SW Herts Consortium academic test (GL verbal reasoning and maths), and up to 24 (10%) by the consortium music aptitude test. Almost all of these go to children in the priority catchment area (WD3-5, WD17-19, WD23-25, HA6 2 and HA6 3). Before that come looked-after children, 24 places for the nearest children who don't qualify any other way, staff children and siblings. Remaining places go by distance. Your child doesn't need to sit a test to apply for the non-test places.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after children, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. 2. Distance 1: 24 places for the nearest children who don't qualify any other way Up to 24 places for the nearest children who don't qualify under another criterion. Last distance offered: 580.41 m in 2026.
  3. 3. Children of staff Children of staff.
  4. 4. Siblings Children with a sibling at the school. All sibling applicants got places in 2024-2026. Twins and other multiple births are admitted together, above PAN if needed.
  5. 5. Musical aptitude (up to 24 places) Up to 24 places in descending order of audition score. 2 are for children outside the catchment (lowest offered 43 in 2026, against 33 in the catchment).
  6. 6. Academic ability (up to 60 places) Up to 60 places in descending order of standardised score. Lowest score offered: 195 in 2026 and 2025, 191 in 2024.
  7. 7. Distance 2: remaining places, nearest first Remaining places go to the children living nearest the school (last distance 941.47 m in 2026).

Good to know

  • Your child doesn't need to sit a test to apply for the non-test places.
  • Return the school's form with your child's consortium test reference number, or they won't be considered for music or academic places.
  • Children of staff come before siblings here, unlike most schools.
  • Siblings at the school don't need to sit the consortium tests unless applying to another consortium school as a higher preference.
  • Everyone offered a distance place must prove their permanent address. Moving during the process or short lets under 6 months can trigger an investigation.
  • In 2026 the lowest scores offered were 195 (academic) and 33 (music, catchment); the last distances were 580.41 m (Distance 1) and 941.47 m (Distance 2).
  • The consortium publishes free familiarisation papers for verbal reasoning and maths, and there is a short break between the two tests.
