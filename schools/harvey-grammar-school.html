The Harvey Grammar School

Folkestone

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

A boy qualifies by passing either the Kent Test or the school's own Shepway Test. If more qualified boys apply than there are places, looked-after boys come first, then Pupil Premium boys living in Folkestone and Hythe district, then other boys living in the district (nearest first), then boys from outside (nearest first).

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care outside England) who have passed come first.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium boys living in Folkestone and Hythe district Boys eligible for Pupil Premium who live in Folkestone and Hythe district, if the supplementary form reaches the school by 31 October. Pupil Premium boys outside the district get no extra priority.
  3. 3. Lives in Folkestone and Hythe district Other qualified boys living in the district. If there are more than places, nearest first.
  4. 4. Other qualified boys, by distance Qualified boys living outside the district, nearest first.

Good to know

  • To sit the Shepway Test your son must also be registered for the Kent Test.
  • The school has 150 places (five classes of 30) and can no longer take an extra class.
  • Pupil Premium boys living outside Folkestone and Hythe district get no extra priority.
  • Twins and triplets are all admitted, even if this takes the school over its 150 places.
  • For September 2027 entry the Kent Test pass mark was a total of 345 with no score below 111.
  • In the 2026 Kent Test, 8,820 of 16,462 children were found suitable for grammar school.
