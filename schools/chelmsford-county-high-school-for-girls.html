Chelmsford County High School for Girls

Chelmsford

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Girls sit the school's own entrance test (the FSCE test: KS2 subjects up to Year 5 plus a Creativity task), not the CSSE 11+. Looked-after girls scoring up to 5 marks below the eligible score come first, then girls with an EHCP. Of the next 144 places, up to 30 go to Pupil or Service Premium girls within 12.5 miles (again up to 5 marks below), and the rest to girls within 12.5 miles in score order. The last places (up to 36) go to the highest scorers wherever they live.

  4. 3(b). Rest of the 144: girls within 12.5 miles with an eligible score The rest of the 144 places go to girls with an eligible score living within 12.5 miles, highest score first. She must have lived in the area since 31 October 2026.

Good to know

  • CCHS doesn't use the Essex CSSE 11+. If you are also applying to CSSE schools, you need both registrations.
  • The eligible score isn't fixed in advance. For 2027 entry it was a standardised score of 92 or above plus 'of the standard' in Creativity (476 of 1,119 candidates).
  • Looked-after girls and Pupil or Service Premium girls within 12.5 miles need a score only up to 5 marks below the eligible score.
  • CCHS test scores (about 69-136) are on a different scale from CSSE totals (about 300).
  • A consultation on the 2028/29 admissions policy runs from 6 November to 18 December 2026.
