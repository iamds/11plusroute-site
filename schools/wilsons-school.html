Wilson's School

Wallington

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys sit the Sutton Selective Eligibility Test (SET) in English and maths. Those who reach Wilson's pass mark sit a written Second Stage exam in English and maths, and must pass both papers and an overall mark to qualify. Qualifying boys are offered places in this order: looked-after children, up to 14 boys on Free School Meals, up to 9 sport (football) and up to 9 music aptitude places, then everyone else by test rank. Where you live only matters to break exact ties.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys (including those adopted from state care abroad) who pass the tests come first, in order of their entrance-test score.
  2. 2. Free School Meals (14 places) Up to 14 places go to qualifying boys eligible for income-related Free School Meals when they registered for the SET, in order of entrance-test score.
  3. 3. Sport (football) aptitude (9 places) Up to 9 qualifying boys above the sport aptitude benchmark, in order of their Sport Aptitude Test score, not their academic score. These boys become Football Scholars and are expected to play for the school, including Saturday fixtures.
  4. 4. Music aptitude (9 places) Up to 9 qualifying boys above the music aptitude benchmark, in order of their Music Aptitude Test score, not their academic score. They're expected to commit to school ensembles throughout their time at the school.
  5. 5. All other qualifying boys by test rank All remaining places, plus any unfilled FSM or aptitude places, go to qualifying boys in order of their standardised entrance-test score.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area and no sibling priority: where you live only breaks exact ties, with Sutton residents first.
  • Aptitude places go by aptitude score: a boy only needs to pass the academic tests, not rank highly, to win one.
  • Taking an aptitude test doesn't affect your son's chances under the other criteria, but there's no alternative date if he's ill.
  • A boy can pass for one SET school and not another, because each sets its own pass mark.
  • The school advises a journey of no more than about an hour.
