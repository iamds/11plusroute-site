Bishop Wordsworth's Church of England Grammar School

Salisbury

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

Boys register with the school and sit its own 11+ (two papers: verbal skills; maths and non-verbal reasoning). If too many apply, only the 480 living nearest sit the test. Boys who reach the qualifying standard (10% lower for looked-after and Pupil Premium boys) are ranked: looked-after, Pupil Premium, designated area (a mapped area around Salisbury), sons of staff, Service Premium, then everyone else. There are no faith criteria. No place is offered unless BWS is your first preference.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. a. Looked after / previously looked after children Looked-after and previously looked-after boys, including those adopted from state care outside England.
  2. b. Pupil Premium Boys eligible for the Pupil Premium (FSM in the last six years) at the time of the exam.
  3. c. Lives in the designated area Boys whose permanent home is in the designated area at the time of application, or who can show they will permanently move there.
  4. d. Sons of staff (2+ years) Boys living with a parent or step-parent on the school's staff for at least two years.
  5. e. Service Premium Service children (Service Premium now or in the last three years, or an MOD child pension).
  6. f. Other qualifying boys All other boys who met the entry requirements.

Good to know

  • Although it is a Church of England school, there are no faith-based admission criteria.
  • Nearly all offers go to boys who put the school first (155 of 157 in 2024/25), so the school advises naming it as your first preference.
  • From 2028 entry the test is set by Quest Assessments: two 45-minute papers, one English and verbal problem solving, one maths and non-verbal problem solving.
