Sutton Coldfield Grammar School for Girls

Sutton Coldfield

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One entrance test (the Birmingham grammar schools' GL test, shared across the West Midlands). Girls must reach the qualifying score of 205. Looked-after girls come first, then up to 36 Pupil Premium girls, then everyone else, all in score order. There is no catchment area and distance only breaks ties.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after children (incl. internationally adopted) Looked-after and previously looked-after girls who reach 205, in score order.
  2. 2. Pupil Premium (up to 36 places) Up to 36 Pupil Premium girls who reach 205, in score order. Any others are still considered under criterion 3 on their score.
  3. 3. All other girls by score Everyone else who reached 205, in score order. Where she lives doesn't matter.

Good to know

  • There is no catchment area: where you live only matters to break a tie.
  • Pupil Premium priority depends on the primary school receiving Pupil Premium funding for your daughter at the time of the test; 'expanded' free school meals from September 2026 don't count.
  • Scores are on the Birmingham test's combined scale, so don't compare them with scores out of about 100 at other schools.
  • In recent years the last score offered to girls without priority has been 222-229 in March, falling a few marks by September.
  • Only the separate answer sheet is marked; the question booklet has space for working out.
  • Free familiarisation questions are published on the West Midlands Grammar Schools website.
