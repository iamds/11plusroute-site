Ribston Hall High School

Gloucester

September 2027 entry · Last checked 25 November 2026

One multiple-choice test (the Gloucestershire grammar schools' joint 11+). Looked-after girls come first, then 30 places for Pupil Premium girls (Gloucester GL1-GL4 postcodes first); both groups get in if they reach the qualifying standard or rank up to 100 places below it. All other places go to qualifying girls in rank order. There is no catchment area.

Who gets a place, in priority order

  1. 1. Looked after / previously looked after girls Girls in care or previously in care (including those adopted from state care abroad) who reach the qualifying standard or rank up to 100 places below it, by rank.
  2. 2. 30 Pupil Premium places 30 places for Pupil Premium girls who reach the qualifying standard or rank up to 100 places below it: first those living in GL1-GL4, by rank, then those living elsewhere, by rank.
  3. 3. Qualifying girls by rank All other girls who reach the qualifying standard, by rank.

Good to know

  • The qualifying standard is set after the test and isn't published in advance. In 2026 the last girl offered a place on allocation day was ranked 619.
  • Siblings, feeder schools and catchment count for nothing.
  • Looked-after and Pupil Premium girls can qualify up to 100 ranked places below the qualifying standard.
  • Name the school on your application only if your daughter met its qualifying standard; qualifying is not an offer.
  • From September 2028 entry the Gloucestershire grammar schools switch to an FSCE test sat at the end of Year 5 (Saturday 3 July 2027).
