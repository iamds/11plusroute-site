After offer day

What happens from 1 March, when secondary school offers come out, and what you can do if you didn't get the school you wanted.

1. Offer day

National Offer Day for September 2027 entry is Monday 1 March 2027. Your offer comes from your home council (the one you applied to), even if the school is in another area, usually by email or through the council's online system.

2. Accept your offer

Reply to your council by its deadline, usually in mid-March. Accept the place you've been offered even if you hope for another school. Accepting it doesn't affect your place on waiting lists or your right to appeal, and it makes sure your child has a school in September.

3. Waiting lists

Waiting lists are ranked by each school's admission rules, not by when you joined, so your position can go down as well as up. Schools must keep them until at least 31 December. Some councils add you automatically for schools you ranked higher than the one you were offered; others ask you to request it, so check with your council. Each school page has a "Waiting list and late applications" section with the details.

4. Reallocation

Places given up by other families are re-offered from waiting lists, usually from mid-March. Some councils hold a set reallocation day; after that, schools offer places from their own lists as they come up.

5. Appeals

You have the right to appeal for any school you applied for and weren't offered. The deadline is at least 20 school days after offer day; many are in April. Appeals are heard by an independent panel, usually in May or June.

For a grammar school, the panel normally considers whether your child is of grammar school ability (school reports, test scores and a letter from the primary head teacher help) and whether the admission rules were applied correctly. Send any evidence by the deadline you're given.

6. Late tests and moving house

If your child missed the test or you've moved into the area, some areas offer a late test after offer day. Check the "Missing the test" details on the school's page.
