Privacy

This site has no accounts, sets no cookies and doesn't ask for your name. Here is everything that happens with your data.

Your postcode or town

If you enter a postcode or town, your browser sends it to postcodes.io (a free UK postcode lookup service) to find its location, so distances can be worked out. The calculation happens in your browser; the postcode is not sent to us. It is saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to type it again (use "Clear" to remove it), and it is added to the page address so you can bookmark or share a search. Anyone you share that link with will see the postcode.

"Use my location"

If you press "Use my location", your browser first asks your permission. If you agree, it sends your approximate coordinates to postcodes.io to find the nearest postcode, and from then on it works exactly as if you had typed that postcode. Your coordinates aren't stored or sent anywhere else.

Visitor counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. We send only the page path (for example /schools/tiffin-school.html), never the postcode or search in the address, along with the referring site, browser type, screen size and approximate country that GoatCounter works out from the request.

Maps

The map view loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap and the map software from cdnjs. Like any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser requests them. Nothing is loaded from them unless you open the map.

Shortlist calendar

Your shortlist is saved in your browser. If you choose "Add whole shortlist to calendar", we store a random ID and the list of schools you've starred (nothing else: no name, email or postcode) so your calendar app can fetch your dates. Your browser updates that list when you star or unstar a school. It's deleted automatically two years after the last change. If you never use that button, nothing is stored.

Reporting a mistake

The "Tell us" link opens a Google Form. Anything you type there, including an email address if you choose to give one, goes to Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy. We use it only to check and fix the page and, if you asked, to reply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and may keep it in security logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts and all other files are served from this site.

Questions

Contact us through this form. You have rights under UK data protection law, including to ask what we hold about you (for this site, only what you've sent through the form) and to have it deleted.
